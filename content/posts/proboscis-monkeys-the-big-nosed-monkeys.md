On the island of Borneo, in the green mangrove forests that grow where the rivers meet the sea, lives one of the funniest-looking monkeys in the world. It is called the proboscis monkey, and it is famous for one amazing feature: its nose. The male proboscis monkey has a huge, long nose that hangs down over his mouth like a ripe mango. On the island of Borneo lives a monkey with a nose so big it hangs down over its mouth, a pot belly, and a surprising talent for swimming.

## A Nose That Makes a Sound

Why would a monkey need such a giant nose? Scientists think the big nose helps the male monkey make loud, honking calls that travel far through the forest. The bigger the nose, the louder and deeper the call, and the more impressive the monkey seems to other monkeys. Female proboscis monkeys have much smaller noses, and baby monkeys have tiny noses that grow bigger as they get older.

The male's nose can also turn bright red when he is excited or showing off. It wobbles when he moves, and some people say it looks like the monkey is wearing a funny mask. But for the proboscis monkey, that big nose is a badge of honour. A male with a large nose is more likely to become the leader of his group, because the other monkeys know he is strong and healthy.

## A Monkey with a Pot Belly

Proboscis monkeys have another funny feature: a big, round belly. Their bellies are so round that they look as if they have eaten a huge dinner. But this belly is not fat. Inside it is a special stomach that works like the stomach of a cow. It is full of tiny helpers that break down tough leaves, which most animals cannot digest.

Proboscis monkeys eat mostly young leaves, seeds and unripe fruit. Their special stomachs let them eat leaves that would make other monkeys sick, which means they can find food in places where other monkeys cannot. When they eat, they sit in the trees and munch slowly, like people enjoying a long, lazy lunch. Their round bellies and reddish-brown fur make them easy to spot among the green leaves.

## The Best Swimmers of the Monkey World

Most monkeys do not like water, but proboscis monkeys are different. They are some of the best swimmers in the whole monkey family. When danger comes, or when they want to reach the trees on the other side of a river, they leap from the branches with a big splash and swim across, using their arms and legs like paddles.

Their hands and feet are partly webbed, a little bit like a duck's feet, which helps them push through the water. They can swim under the water for a short time, and they are not afraid to dive in from a high branch. Young monkeys learn to swim by watching the older monkeys, and soon they are splashing and playing in the river just like children in a swimming pool.

At night, proboscis monkeys sleep in the trees right beside the river, where they are safe from many hunters on the ground. In the morning, they wake up, stretch, and begin their day of eating leaves and playing in the branches.

## Living Together in the Trees

Proboscis monkeys live in groups. A group usually has one adult male, several females and their babies. The male is the leader, and he keeps watch while the others eat and play. The females help look after all the babies, not just their own, and the young monkeys play together in the trees, chasing each other and swinging from branch to branch.

Baby proboscis monkeys have blue faces when they are born, which slowly turn grey and then reddish as they grow older. The mothers hold their babies close, and the little ones cling to their mothers' fur as they move through the trees. As the babies grow, they become braver and start to explore the branches on their own, always ready to run back to their mothers if they get scared.

Proboscis monkeys spend most of their lives in the trees, and they rarely come down to the ground. Their long tails help them balance as they leap from branch to branch, and their strong hands grip the branches tightly. They are graceful movers, even with their big noses and round bellies.

## A Monkey That Needs the Mangroves

Proboscis monkeys live only on the island of Borneo, in Malaysia, Indonesia and Brunei. They need the mangrove forests and the trees along the rivers to survive, because that is where their food grows. But those forests are being cut down to make room for farms and buildings, and the monkeys are losing their homes.

Luckily, people are working hard to protect them. Parks and reserves keep parts of the mangrove forest safe, and scientists study the monkeys to learn how to help them. Some proboscis monkeys also live in zoos, where people can see them and learn about why their home in Borneo is so special.

## A Monkey Worth Meeting

The proboscis monkey shows us that nature loves to make animals that are a little bit silly and a lot wonderful. With its huge nose, its round belly and its love of swimming, it is one of the most unusual monkeys on Earth. It reminds us that every animal has its own special way of living, and that the forests of Borneo hold treasures that exist nowhere else in the world.

Next time you see a picture of a monkey with a giant nose, remember the proboscis monkey, the big-nosed swimmer of Borneo, leaping from the trees and splashing into the river below.
