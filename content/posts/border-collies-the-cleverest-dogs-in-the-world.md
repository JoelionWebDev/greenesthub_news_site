# Border Collies: The Cleverest Dogs in the World

Imagine a medium-sized dog with a black and white coat, bright, watchful eyes and a look of deep concentration on its face. It crouches low, creeps forward slowly, and stares hard at a flock of sheep. With just that stare, the sheep begin to move exactly where the dog wants them to go. This is a Border Collie, and many people believe it is the cleverest dog in the world.

Border Collies are famous for their brains, their energy and their amazing ability to work with people. They can learn hundreds of words, solve problems, and herd sheep better than almost any other dog. Let us meet these brilliant dogs.

## Where Do Border Collies Come From?

Border Collies come from the border land between England and Scotland, in the United Kingdom. Their name tells their story: "border" because they come from the border country, and "collie" is an old word for a sheepdog. For hundreds of years, farmers in those green, hilly lands needed dogs that could help them look after sheep.

Sheep farming in that part of the world is hard work. The hills are steep, the weather is cold and wet, and the sheep spread out across wide fields. Farmers needed a dog that was clever, fast and tireless — a dog that could run for hours, listen to commands from far away, and think for itself when the farmer was not close by. The Border Collie became that dog.

Over many years, farmers chose the cleverest and hardest-working dogs to breed from. The result was a dog with an amazing brain, a strong body and an incredible love of work. Today, Border Collies still herd sheep all over the world, but many also live as family pets and compete in dog sports.

## What Do Border Collies Look Like?

Border Collies are medium-sized dogs with a graceful, athletic build. They are not as big as a German Shepherd, but they are strong and fast. Their most famous look is black and white, with a white chest, a white stripe up the face and white paws. But they can also be brown and white, red and white, blue and white, or even three colours at once.

They have two kinds of coats. Some have a smooth, short coat, and others have a longer coat that is a little rough and wavy. Both kinds need brushing to keep them clean. Their ears can be floppy, half up, or fully upright, and their eyes are often brown, though some have one blue eye or two blue eyes.

The most special thing about a Border Collie is its eyes. Border Collies have a famous "eye", a strong, intense stare that they use to control sheep.

## The Cleverest Dogs in the World

Border Collies are often called the cleverest dogs in the world, and there is good reason for that. They can learn new commands faster than most other dogs, sometimes after hearing them only a few times. They are brilliant at learning words too. One famous Border Collie learned the names of hundreds of different toys and could fetch the right one just by hearing its name.

But their cleverness is not just about words. Border Collies can solve problems, remember routes, and work out what their owner wants, even without being told. They watch people carefully and learn from watching. If you leave a Border Collie alone with a puzzle toy, it will work at it until it finds the answer.

This cleverness means Border Collies need a lot of mental work. They get bored quickly if they have nothing to do. A bored Border Collie might start herding children, chasing cars or chewing things in the house.

## The Amazing Sheepdog

Herding sheep is what Border Collies were born to do, and they are the best in the world at it. A shepherd can stand far away in a field and give commands by whistling. The Border Collie listens, even from a great distance, and moves the sheep exactly where the shepherd wants them.

The dog uses a clever trick called "the eye". It crouches low, moves slowly and stares at the sheep. The sheep feel nervous under that stare and move away. The dog then moves around the flock, guiding the sheep gently, never hurting them. It can gather sheep from all over a hillside and bring them together in one group.

Border Collies can also do a trick called "shedding", where they separate one sheep from the rest of the flock. It takes great skill and patience. Watching a good Border Collie work with sheep is like watching a dancer: smooth, graceful and perfectly timed.

Today, there are sheepdog trials, which are competitions where Border Collies show off their herding skills. Dogs and their handlers work together to move sheep through gates and into pens, and judges mark them on how well they do. The best sheepdogs in the world are almost always Border Collies.

## Loving Family Pets

Border Collies can be wonderful family pets, but they need an active family. They are not happy lying on the sofa all day. They need long walks, runs, games and training every single day. They love to play fetch, learn tricks and go on adventures.

They are loyal and loving with their families, and they often form a very strong bond with one person. They are gentle with children, though their herding instinct can make them try to herd small children by gently nudging them.

A Border Collie needs a garden or a park to run in, and an owner who enjoys being outdoors. If you give a Border Collie plenty of exercise, games and love, it will be the happiest dog you have ever met.

## Fun Facts About Border Collies

- Border Collies come from the border land between England and Scotland.
- They are often called the cleverest dogs in the world.
- They can learn the names of hundreds of toys.
- Their intense stare, called "the eye", helps them control sheep.
- A shepherd can guide a Border Collie from far away using whistles.
- They are stars at agility, flyball and obedience competitions.
- They need lots of exercise and brain games every day.
- Their coats are often black and white, but they can be many colours.

Border Collies are clever, fast, loyal and full of energy. They are brilliant sheepdogs, amazing athletes and loving friends.
