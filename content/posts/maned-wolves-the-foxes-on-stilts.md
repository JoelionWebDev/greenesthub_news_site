# Maned Wolves: The Foxes on Stilts

Imagine a fox that stood on stilts. That is the maned wolf! It has long, thin legs, big pointy ears, and a coat of warm reddish-orange fur. It looks like a fox, it has "wolf" in its name, but it is neither a true fox nor a true wolf. It is something all its own, and it lives in the wide grasslands of South America.

## A Wolf That Walks on Stilts

The first thing you notice about a maned wolf is its legs. They are very long and thin, almost like stilts. Its legs are black from the knee down, which makes it look as if it is wearing long black socks.

Those long legs are not just for show. The maned wolf lives in tall grass, and its legs lift its body high above the grass so it can see where it is going. It can spot food, watch for danger, and find its way through the grasslands. When it walks, it moves in a special way, swinging the two legs on the same side of its body together. It looks a little funny, but it works very well.

## Big Ears and a Dark Mane

The maned wolf has very big ears that stand up straight. It can turn them this way and that to listen for tiny sounds. Its hearing is so good that it can hear a small animal moving in the grass, even when it cannot see it. At dusk, when the light is low, those big ears help it find its dinner.

Along its neck and back grows a dark mane of longer hair. When the maned wolf feels scared or excited, it can raise this mane so it looks bigger than it really is. It is a clever trick to scare away other animals. The mane is black, just like the lower parts of its legs, and it stands out against the orange-red fur.

## Where Do Maned Wolves Live?

Maned wolves live in South America, in countries like Brazil, Paraguay, Argentina, and Bolivia. Their home is the grassland, a place of wide open fields, tall grass, and scattered trees and bushes. There are no thick forests where they live, just open spaces that stretch as far as the eye can see.

They like to be out in the early morning and in the evening, when the sun is low and the air is cooler. During the heat of the day, they rest in the shade of a bush or a patch of tall grass. They are shy animals and they like to keep to themselves.

A maned wolf usually lives alone. It does not run in a pack like other wolves do. Each wolf has its own area, and it walks through that area looking for food.

## What Is for Dinner?

Maned wolves eat many different things. They are omnivores, which means they eat both plants and animals. They hunt small animals like rats, mice, rabbits, birds, and insects. They also eat fruit, and one fruit is so important to them that it is called the wolf apple.

The wolf apple looks a little like a tomato. It grows on small bushes in the grasslands, and maned wolves love to eat it. It gives them food and water, and it may even help keep them healthy. A maned wolf will eat lots of wolf apples when they are ripe. They are not fussy eaters. If they find eggs, they eat eggs. If they find berries, they eat berries. Whatever the grassland offers, the maned wolf tries it.

## A Family of Their Own

Maned wolves are usually alone, but when it is time to have babies, a mother and father come together for a while. The mother finds a safe, hidden place in the tall grass to have her pups.

Baby maned wolves are called pups. When they are born, they are small and dark, almost black, with fluffy fur. As they grow, their fur slowly changes to the reddish-orange colour of their parents. The pups stay with their mother while they are small, drinking her milk and learning how to live in the grasslands.

The mother takes good care of her pups. She brings them food and keeps them safe and warm. As the pups grow bigger and stronger, they start to explore. They practise pouncing and listening, just like their parents. When they are old enough, they leave to find a home of their own, and the wide grasslands welcome another young wolf.

## Why Maned Wolves Need Our Help

Maned wolves are in trouble. Their grassland homes are getting smaller because people are turning grasslands into farms and towns. When the grasslands shrink, the wolves have less room to hunt and live. Roads are also a danger, because wolves sometimes cross them at night and are hit by cars.

People are working hard to help maned wolves. In some places, special parks and safe areas are set aside so the wolves can live and raise their pups. Scientists study them to learn what they need. Zoos in many countries keep maned wolves too, and they help people learn about them and care for them.

The next time you see a picture of a maned wolf, look at those long legs and big ears. It may look like a fox on stilts, but it is really a one-of-a-kind animal with a very special life. And the wide grasslands of South America would not be the same without it.

## Fun Facts to Remember

- The maned wolf is the tallest wild dog in the world.
- It has long black legs that look like stilts and help it see over tall grass.
- It is not a fox and not a true wolf. It belongs to its very own group.
- It loves to eat a fruit called the wolf apple.
- It lives alone and is very shy.
- Its big ears help it hear small animals hiding in the grass.
- When it feels scared, it raises its dark mane to look bigger.
