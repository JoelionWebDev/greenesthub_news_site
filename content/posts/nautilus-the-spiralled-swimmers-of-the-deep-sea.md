# Nautilus: The Spiralled Swimmers of the Sea

Deep in the ocean lives an animal with a shell like a spiral staircase. It is the nautilus, a gentle sea creature that looks like it has swum straight out of a very old storybook. It has a beautiful shell, many little arms, and big eyes. And it has been swimming in the world's oceans for a very, very long time.

## A Shell Like a Spiral

The nautilus is famous for its shell. The shell is round and flat, with a beautiful spiral shape that grows bigger and bigger as it turns. Inside the shell are many small rooms, called chambers. The nautilus lives in the biggest room, the one at the front. As it grows, it builds a new, bigger room and moves into it, leaving the old rooms behind.

The shell is not just a home. It is also a float! The nautilus can fill the empty rooms with gas or water to help it move up or down in the sea. When it wants to swim up, it fills the rooms with gas, which makes it lighter. When it wants to swim down, it lets water in, which makes it heavier. It is a bit like a tiny submarine!

The outside of the shell is smooth and shiny, with stripes of orange and brown. The inside is shiny and pearly, like the inside of a seashell you might find on the beach. It is one of the most beautiful shells in the whole ocean.

## Many Arms and Big Eyes

The nautilus has many arms, or tentacles, that stick out from its shell. It has lots of them, many more than an octopus or a squid. These arms help it to catch food and to feel its way around in the dark water.

Its arms do not have suckers like an octopus. Instead, they are sticky, and they help the nautilus to grab small animals and bring them to its mouth. The nautilus has a strong beak, like a parrot's beak, that it uses to eat its food.

The nautilus has big eyes, too. They help it to see in the deep, dark water where it lives. Its eyes are simple, without lenses like our eyes have, but they are still good enough to help it find food and stay safe.

## Where Do Nautiluses Live?

Nautiluses live in the warm waters of the Pacific Ocean, around places like the Philippines, Indonesia, and Australia. They live in deep water during the day, where it is dark and cool. At night, they swim up to shallower water to look for food.

They live on the slopes of coral reefs and in deep sea valleys. They like water that is not too warm and not too cold, and they need clean, clear water to live in. They are shy animals and they do not like bright light, so they stay in the deep during the day.

## What Do Nautiluses Eat?

Nautiluses eat small animals that live in the sea. They eat shrimp, small fish, and other creatures that they can catch with their arms. They also eat dead animals that they find on the sea floor. They are not fussy eaters, and they will eat whatever they can find.

They hunt at night, when they swim up to shallower water. They use their arms to feel for food in the dark, and their big eyes to spot movement. When they find something tasty, they grab it with their arms and bring it to their beak to eat.

## Growing Up in a Shell

When a mother nautilus is ready to lay eggs, she lays them on the sea floor, in a safe place among rocks and coral. The eggs are large and they take a long time to hatch. When the baby nautiluses hatch, they are tiny, but they already have a small shell.

Baby nautiluses are called hatchlings. They are ready to swim and find food on their own as soon as they hatch. They grow slowly, adding new rooms to their shells as they get bigger. It takes many years for a nautilus to grow up and become an adult.

Nautiluses live for a long time, but they grow very slowly. This means that if too many are caught, it takes a long time for their numbers to grow back. That is why it is important to protect them.

## Why Nautiluses Need Our Help

Nautiluses are in danger because people catch them for their beautiful shells. The shells are sold in shops and used to make jewellery and decorations. When too many nautiluses are caught, there are not enough left to have babies and keep the family going.

Pollution is also a problem. When dirty water flows into the sea, it can hurt nautiluses and the coral reefs where they live. Nautiluses need clean water and healthy reefs to survive.

People are working to protect nautiluses. In some places, there are rules about how many can be caught. Scientists are studying them to learn more about how they live, and how to keep them safe. By protecting the ocean and its reefs, we help nautiluses and many other sea animals too.

## Fun Facts to Remember

- The nautilus has a beautiful spiral shell with many small rooms inside.
- It can fill the rooms with gas or water to move up or down in the sea, like a tiny submarine.
- It has many arms that help it catch food, and a strong beak for eating.
- It lives in the deep waters of the Pacific Ocean.
- It is a living fossil, and its family has been in the sea for hundreds of millions of years.
- It swims slowly and gracefully, using a jet of water to push itself along.
- It can pull itself inside its shell to stay safe from hunters.

The nautilus is a gentle, beautiful creature with a very long history. Next time you see a spiral shell, think of the nautilus drifting through the deep blue sea, carrying its home on its back.
