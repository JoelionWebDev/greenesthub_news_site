Imagine a bird with feet as blue as the sky on a sunny day. Not pale blue, not grey-blue, but bright, brilliant blue, like a pair of fancy shoes. That is the blue-footed booby! These funny, beautiful seabirds live on rocky islands in the Pacific Ocean, and they are famous for their blue feet, their silly dances, and their spectacular dives into the sea. Let us meet them and find out how they live.

## Meet the Blue-Footed Booby

The blue-footed booby is a seabird, which means it lives by the sea and spends much of its life over the water. It has a white head and belly, brown wings, and a long, pointed beak. But the star of the show is its feet. They are bright blue, and they stand out against the brown rocks and the grey sea.

Why are their feet blue? The blue colour comes from the food they eat. Blue-footed boobies eat fish, and the fish contain special nutrients that help make their feet bright blue. The brighter the blue, the healthier the bird. Female boobies look for males with the brightest blue feet, because bright feet show that the male is healthy and good at finding food.

Blue-footed boobies live on islands in the eastern Pacific Ocean, off the coasts of South America and Central America. The most famous place to see them is the Galapagos Islands, a group of islands far out in the Pacific where many amazing animals live. They also live on islands off Mexico, Peru, and other countries in the region.

## The Famous Blue-Foot Dance

Blue-footed boobies are famous for their dance. When a male wants to impress a female, he puts on a show. He lifts one bright blue foot high in the air, then the other, stepping from side to side like a dancer. He spreads his wings, points his beak up to the sky, and whistles. It looks very funny, like a bird doing a careful dance in bright blue shoes.

The female watches closely. If she likes the dance, she joins in, and they dance together, stepping and whistling. This dance is not just for fun. It helps the two birds get to know each other and decide if they want to be partners. Once they have danced together, they stay together to raise their chicks.

The dance is also a way for the male to show off his blue feet. He lifts each foot high so the female can see how bright and blue it is. The brighter the feet, the better the dancer looks. It is a bit like showing off a new pair of shoes!

## Diving for Fish

Blue-footed boobies are amazing hunters. They fly high over the sea, looking for schools of small fish swimming near the surface. When they spot a good meal, they fold their wings back and dive straight down into the water, like an arrow. They can dive from high up in the sky, and they hit the water with a big splash.

Under the water, they use their wings and feet to swim after the fish. Their beaks are long and pointed, perfect for catching slippery fish. They can catch several fish in one dive, and then they pop back up to the surface, shake off the water, and fly off to find more.

## Life on Rocky Islands

Blue-footed boobies spend much of their time on rocky islands, where they rest, dance, and raise their chicks. They do not build fancy nests like some birds. Instead, they make a simple nest on the ground, in a circle of twigs and stones, or just a bare patch of rock. The female lays her eggs right on the ground, and both parents take turns sitting on them to keep them warm.

The parents take turns in a clever way. They do not have a bare patch of skin on their belly like many birds do to warm their eggs. Instead, they use their big blue feet! They stand on the eggs with their warm feet, keeping them cosy until they hatch. It is one of the most unusual ways of keeping eggs warm in the bird world.

When the chicks hatch, they are small, soft, and covered in white fluff. They cannot fly or find food for themselves, so their parents feed them by bringing back fish and sharing it with them. The chicks grow fast, and they soon start to grow their adult feathers. Their feet start out a dull grey colour, and slowly turn blue as they grow older and start eating fish for themselves.

## Raising Chicks Together

Blue-footed booby parents work together to raise their chicks. The mother and father take turns looking after the nest, going out to sea to catch fish, and feeding the chicks. This teamwork helps the chicks grow strong and healthy.

Chicks stay with their parents for many months. They learn how to fly, how to dive, and how to catch fish by watching their parents and copying them. Young boobies practise their diving in shallow water first, and slowly get better and better. When they are big and strong enough, they leave their parents and start their own lives on the islands and over the sea.

## Why Blue Feet Matter

The blue feet of the blue-footed booby are more than just a funny sight. They are a sign of health. A bird with bright blue feet is eating well and is strong. A bird with pale feet may be unwell or not finding enough food. Scientists study the colour of boobies' feet to learn about how healthy the birds are and how much food is in the sea.

## Fun Facts About Blue-Footed Boobies

- Their feet are bright blue, like the sky on a sunny day.
- The blue colour comes from the fish they eat.
- The brighter the blue feet, the healthier the bird.
- Males dance by lifting their blue feet high, one after the other.
- They use their warm blue feet to keep their eggs cosy.
- They dive into the sea like arrows to catch fish.
