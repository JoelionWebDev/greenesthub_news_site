There is a little dog with long, silky ears, big, soft brown eyes, and a tail that never stops wagging. It loves nothing more than sitting on your lap, snuggling under a blanket, and being close to the people it loves. It is called the Cavalier King Charles Spaniel, and it is one of the sweetest and gentlest dogs in the whole world. With silky ears, big brown eyes and a heart that only wants to be close to you, the Cavalier Spaniel is one of the gentlest little dogs in the world.

## A Dog Fit for a King

The Cavalier Spaniel is named after King Charles the Second, who was the King of England hundreds of years ago. The king loved his little spaniels so much that he took them everywhere with him, even to important meetings and to church. People said that the king cared more about his dogs than about anything else, and the little spaniels became famous all over England.

The dogs were bred to be companions, not hunters or workers. Their job was to sit on laps, keep their owners warm, and be loving friends. In those days, people even believed that a little spaniel sitting on your lap could help keep you warm on cold winter nights, and that its soft fur could soothe aches and pains. Whether that was true or not, the little dogs were loved for their gentle nature.

Today, Cavalier Spaniels are still one of the most popular little dogs in the world, and they are loved for the same reasons the king loved them: their sweetness, their beauty and their loving hearts.

## A Face That Melts Your Heart

Cavalier Spaniels are small dogs, about the size of a large cat, with a short nose, a round head and ears so long and silky that they look like curtains. Their coats are soft and flowing, and they come in four lovely colours. The most famous is called Blenheim, which is a mix of rich chestnut brown and white, named after Blenheim Palace in England.

Their eyes are large, round and dark brown, and they have a gentle, pleading look that can melt anyone's heart. When a Cavalier looks up at you with those big eyes, it is very hard to say no to it. Their tails wag almost all the time, and their whole body wiggles with happiness when they see someone they love.

Cavaliers are not loud or noisy dogs. They bark only when they need to, and they are polite and well-mannered. They are graceful little dogs, with a smooth, flowing walk, and they carry themselves like tiny royalty.

## The Happiest Little Companions

Cavalier Spaniels are happiest when they are with people. They are not dogs that like to be left alone in the garden. They want to be in the house, on the sofa, and preferably on your lap. They follow their owners from room to room, and they love to be part of everything the family does.

They are wonderful dogs for children, because they are gentle, patient and never grumpy. They love to play, but they are not rough or wild. They enjoy a game of fetch in the garden, a gentle walk in the park, and a long cuddle on the sofa afterwards. They get along well with other dogs and cats too, and they are happy to share their home with other pets.

Cavaliers are also very clever. They learn quickly, and they love to please their owners. They can learn tricks such as sit, stay, shake hands and roll over, and they enjoy training games. They are not stubborn dogs, and they respond best to kind words and gentle praise.

## Caring for a Cavalier

Cavalier Spaniels need regular brushing to keep their silky coats looking beautiful. Their long ears can get tangled, so owners brush them gently a few times a week, and check their ears to make sure they are clean and healthy. Cavaliers also need regular walks, but they do not need as much exercise as bigger, more energetic dogs. A couple of short walks a day, plus some playtime in the garden, is usually enough to keep them happy.

They love food, and they will eat almost anything you offer them, so owners need to be careful not to give them too many treats. A Cavalier that eats too much can become overweight, which is not good for its health. With good food, regular walks and lots of love, a Cavalier can live a long and happy life.

Because they are so friendly, Cavaliers make wonderful therapy dogs. They visit hospitals, schools and care homes, where they bring comfort and joy to people who need a little cheering up. Their gentle nature and loving hearts make them perfect for this important work.

## A Little Dog with a Royal Heart

The Cavalier King Charles Spaniel shows us that the best dogs are not always the biggest or the strongest. Sometimes the best dog is the one that sits quietly on your lap, looks up at you with big brown eyes, and loves you with all its heart. That is the Cavalier, a little dog with a royal past and a heart full of love.

Next time you see a little spaniel with silky ears and a wagging tail, remember the Cavalier, the sweetest lap dog of all, and the little friend that once sat on the lap of a king.
