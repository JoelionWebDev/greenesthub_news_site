Have you ever touched something so soft that you never wanted to stop? That is what it feels like to stroke a Rex rabbit. Rex rabbits have the softest, velvety fur of any rabbit in the world, and people love them for their gentle nature and their beautiful coats. They are calm, friendly, and fun to be around, and they make wonderful pets for families who want a quiet, loving friend. Let us meet the Rex rabbit and find out what makes this velvety rabbit so special.

## Fur That Feels Like Velvet

The most amazing thing about a Rex rabbit is its fur. Most rabbits have two kinds of fur, a soft undercoat and longer guard hairs that stick out on top. Rex rabbits are different. Their fur is all the same short length, and it stands straight up instead of lying flat. When you stroke a Rex rabbit, your hand sinks into fur that feels just like velvet or plush velvet cloth. It is smooth, dense, and wonderfully soft. Rex rabbits come in many beautiful colours, including black, white, brown, grey, orange, and lovely spotted patterns. Some Rex rabbits even have a colour called chinchilla, which is a mix of grey and white that looks like the fur of a wild rabbit. No matter what colour they are, every Rex rabbit has that same amazing velvety coat.

## A Rabbit From France

The story of the Rex rabbit began in France, in the year 1919. A farmer found a litter of baby rabbits with very unusual fur. Their coats were short, dense, and velvety, quite unlike any rabbit fur anyone had seen before. The farmer was amazed, and he kept the special rabbits and bred more of them. The new rabbits were called Rex, a Latin word that means king, because their fur was so fine that it seemed fit for a king. Before long, Rex rabbits were shown at rabbit shows in France and Britain, where people fell in love with their soft coats. From there, Rex rabbits spread to many other countries, and today they are one of the most popular rabbit breeds in the whole world.

## Calm, Gentle, and Friendly

Rex rabbits are known for their calm and gentle nature. They are friendly rabbits that enjoy being around people, and they often like to be stroked and petted. Many Rex rabbits will sit quietly on your lap while you stroke their velvety fur, and some will even flop over on their sides when they are feeling happy and relaxed. They are intelligent rabbits too, and they can learn their names, come when they are called, and even learn to use a litter tray. Rex rabbits are also playful. They love to hop around, explore new places, and play with toys such as cardboard boxes, tunnels, and balls. A happy Rex rabbit will sometimes jump into the air and twist its body, a joyful move that rabbit owners call a binky.

## How Big Do Rex Rabbits Grow?

Rex rabbits are medium to large rabbits. A grown-up Rex rabbit usually weighs about as much as a small cat, and it has a strong, well-shaped body with powerful back legs for hopping. Their ears stand upright and are quite long, and they use them to listen for sounds and to keep cool on hot days. Their eyes are bright and curious, and they are always watching what is going on around them. Rex rabbits have short, curly whiskers, which is another special feature of the breed. Unlike some rabbits that are shy and nervous, Rex rabbits are usually calm and confident, which makes them a great choice for families with children who are gentle and kind.

## Caring for a Rex Rabbit

Looking after a Rex rabbit is a happy job, but it does need care. Rex rabbits need a safe, roomy home where they can hop, stretch, and play. They need fresh hay every day, which is the most important food for a rabbit, along with fresh vegetables such as carrots, leafy greens, and herbs. They also need clean water at all times. Rex rabbits should be brushed gently once a week to keep their coats looking lovely, and their nails need trimming now and then. They also need time outside their home every day to run, hop, and explore in a safe place. Like all rabbits, Rex rabbits should be handled gently and never picked up by their ears. If you are kind and patient, your Rex rabbit will soon trust you and enjoy spending time with you.

## Rabbits Need Friends and Play

Rabbits are social animals, and Rex rabbits are no different. They love company, and many Rex rabbits are happiest when they live with another friendly rabbit. They groom each other, snuggle together, and play side by side. Rex rabbits also need toys and things to keep them busy. They love cardboard boxes to hide in, tunnels to run through, and wooden toys to chew on. Chewing is very important for rabbits, because their teeth keep growing all their lives, and chewing helps keep their teeth the right length. A Rex rabbit that has plenty of space, good food, toys, and a friend to share it all with is a very happy rabbit indeed.

## Fun Facts About Rex Rabbits

Here are some fun facts about Rex rabbits. First, their velvety fur is caused by a special gene that makes all their fur the same short length. Second, Rex rabbits were once so rare and precious that people paid a lot of money for them. Third, a Rex rabbit's fur is so dense that it can feel like touching a soft toy. Fourth, Rex rabbits come in many colours, and some breeders have created rabbits with beautiful spotted and striped patterns. And fifth, Rex rabbits are often used in rabbit shows, where judges look at the softness of their fur, the shape of their bodies, and how calm and friendly they are.

## A Soft and Gentle Friend

Rex rabbits are truly wonderful animals. With their velvety fur, calm nature, and friendly hearts, they bring softness and joy into any home. They remind us that sometimes the gentlest animals make the very best friends. If you are looking for a pet that is quiet, loving, and wonderfully soft to stroke, the Rex rabbit might be the perfect velvety friend for you.
