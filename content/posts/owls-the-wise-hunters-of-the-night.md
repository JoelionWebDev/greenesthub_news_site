Owls are some of the most magical birds in the world. They come out at night, when most other birds are asleep, and they hunt in the dark. Owls have big round eyes, soft feathers, and a serious face that makes them look very wise. People have told stories about wise owls for hundreds of years. Owls live all over the world, in forests, deserts, and even in towns and cities. They are quiet, clever, and full of surprises. When you hear an owl hoot in the night, you know a wonderful hunter is nearby.

## What is an owl?

An owl is a bird of prey, which means it hunts other animals for food. Owls have strong, sharp claws called talons, and a hooked beak for tearing food. Their eyes are big and face forwards, just like ours, which helps them judge how far away things are. Owls come in many sizes. Some owls are small, about the size of a cup, while others are big, with wings as wide as a child is tall. Owls have soft feathers that cover their bodies, and many owls have ear tufts that look like little horns but are not ears at all. Owls are mostly active at night, which makes them different from most other birds.

## Big eyes for the dark

An owl's eyes are amazing. They are very big compared to the size of its head, and they help the owl see in the dark. Owls cannot move their eyes like we can. Their eyes are fixed in place, so to look around, an owl must turn its whole head. Owls can turn their heads much further than we can. They can turn their heads almost all the way around, which looks very strange and wonderful. An owl's eyes are made to catch even tiny amounts of light, so they can see a small animal moving in the dark. Some owls have eyes that are yellow, orange, or black. Their big eyes are one of the reasons people think owls look so wise.

## Super hearing

Owls have wonderful hearing. They can hear the tiniest sounds in the dark, like a mouse moving under leaves or under snow. An owl's ears are hidden under its feathers, and one ear is often higher than the other. This helps the owl tell exactly where a sound is coming from. The owl's face is shaped like a dish, and it helps catch sound and send it to the ears, a bit like a satellite dish. An owl can find its food just by listening, even in complete darkness. Some owls can hear a small animal moving under thick snow and catch it without ever seeing it. Their hearing is one of their greatest hunting tools.

## Silent flight

Owls fly almost silently. Their feathers are soft and have special fluffy edges that break up the sound of the air as they fly. This silent flight helps owls sneak up on their food without being heard. Most other birds make a swooshing sound when they fly, but an owl glides through the night like a ghost. When an owl spots its food, it swoops down quickly and grabs it with its strong talons. Owls hunt mice, small birds, frogs, and insects. After catching its food, an owl often swallows it whole. Later, the owl brings up a pellet made of fur and bones that it could not digest. Scientists study these pellets to learn what owls have been eating.

## Where do owls live?

Owls live all over the world, except in Antarctica. They live in forests, grasslands, deserts, and mountains. Some owls live in towns and cities, nesting in old buildings or parks. Owls do not build fancy nests like many other birds. Many owls use old nests made by other birds, or they nest in holes in trees. Some owls nest on the ground or in burrows dug by other animals. Owls are happy to live wherever they can find food and a safe place to rest during the day. During the day, owls sleep, hiding in trees where their feathers help them blend in with the bark. At night, they wake up and start to hunt.

## Owl families

Mother and father owls work together to raise their babies. Most owls lay a few eggs in their nest. The mother sits on the eggs to keep them warm, while the father brings her food. When the baby owls hatch, they are covered in soft, fluffy down. They are called owlets. The parents bring food to the nest, and the owlets grow quickly. Owlets are hungry birds, and they need lots of food. After some weeks, the owlets start to practise flying. They hop from branch to branch, flapping their wings. Soon they can fly on their own. Young owls stay near their parents while they learn to hunt, and then they go off to find their own place to live.

## Owls in stories

Owls have been in stories for a very long time. In many tales, the owl is a wise old bird that gives good advice. People have loved owls in books, films, and poems. But in some places, people once thought owls were scary because they come out at night and make strange sounds. Today we know that owls are helpful birds. They eat mice and other small animals that can damage crops, so farmers like having owls nearby. Owls are an important part of nature. They help keep the numbers of small animals in balance. When we see an owl, we can remember that it is a clever hunter, a caring parent, and a wonderful part of the night.

## Fun facts about owls

- Owls can turn their heads almost all the way around because their eyes cannot move like ours.
- An owl's feathers have soft, fluffy edges that let it fly almost silently through the night.
- Owls can hear a tiny animal moving under leaves or snow and catch it in complete darkness.
- Owls often swallow their food whole and later bring up a pellet of fur and bones.
- Owls live all over the world except Antarctica, and they do not build fancy nests.
