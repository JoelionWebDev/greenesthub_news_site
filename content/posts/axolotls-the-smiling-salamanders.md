Have you ever seen an animal that looks like it is smiling all the time? It has a wide, happy mouth, feathery pink frills on its head, and little arms and legs. It looks like a cartoon character come to life. Meet the axolotl — one of the strangest and most wonderful animals in the world.

## What Is an Axolotl?

An axolotl is a type of salamander. Salamanders are animals a bit like lizards, but with smooth, wet skin instead of scales. Most salamanders start their lives in water as tadpole-like babies, then grow up, lose their gills, and move onto land.

Axolotls are different. They keep their baby features for their whole lives. They keep their feathery gills and stay in the water, even when they are grown up. This is called staying in their young form, and it makes axolotls very special.

The name "axolotl" comes from an old language spoken in Mexico, where axolotls come from. In that language, the name is linked to a water god. Some people say the name means "water monster" — but axolotls are not monsters at all. They are gentle, peaceful creatures.

A grown-up axolotl is about as long as a school ruler. Its body is long and smooth, with a wide, flat head and a little mouth that curves up at the corners — which is why it always looks like it is smiling. Its skin is soft and slippery. Wild axolotls are usually dark grey or brown with speckles, which helps them hide. Pet axolotls can be pink, white, golden, or black.

## Those Feathery Frills

The most eye-catching thing about an axolotl is the feathery frills on the sides of its head. These are its gills — the parts it uses to breathe under water, just like a fish uses its gills.

Axolotl gills look like little pink feathers or tiny trees. They wave gently in the water as the axolotl swims. The gills take oxygen from the water so the axolotl can breathe. Axolotls can also breathe a little through their skin, and they sometimes swim up to the surface to gulp a little air.

Axolotls have four little legs with tiny toes. Their front legs have four toes and their back legs have five. They use their legs to walk slowly along the bottom of the water, and they use their long, flat tail to swim. They are not fast swimmers. They prefer to drift and float, or to sit still on the bottom and wait.

## Where Do Axolotls Come From?

Axolotls come from Mexico, from a place of lakes and canals near Mexico City. Long ago, there were big lakes there, full of axolotls. But today, axolotls are very rare in the wild. Their lakes have shrunk, the water has become dirty, and other fish have been brought in that eat young axolotls.

Because of this, wild axolotls are now in great danger. Scientists are working hard to protect them and to clean up their home waters. Many people hope that one day, axolotls will be safe in the wild again.

## The Amazing Power to Regrow

Here is the most amazing thing about axolotls: they can regrow parts of their body. If an axolotl loses a leg, it can grow a new one. If it loses part of its tail, it can grow that back too. It can even regrow parts of its heart and brain!

## What Do Axolotls Eat?

Axolotls are carnivores, which means they eat other animals. In the wild, they eat small water creatures like worms, tiny fish, and little insects. They are hunters, but they are slow and patient hunters.

An axolotl does not chase its food. It sits quietly on the bottom and waits. When a worm or a small fish swims close, the axolotl opens its mouth very fast and sucks the food in, like a vacuum cleaner. Its mouth is wide, so it can swallow food whole. Axolotls do not have sharp teeth for chewing. They have tiny teeth that help them grip their food, but they swallow it whole.

## Life in the Water

Axolotls are calm, quiet animals. They spend their days swimming slowly, resting on the bottom, or hiding under rocks and plants. They are most active at night, when they come out to hunt for food.

They like cool, clean water. If the water gets too warm, axolotls can become sick. That is why pet axolotls need a tank with cool water and a good filter to keep it clean. They also need places to hide, like caves, pots, or plants, so they feel safe. Axolotls do not like bright lights. In the wild, they live in murky lake water where the light is soft.

## Fun Facts

- Axolotls keep their baby gills and stay in the water for their whole lives, even as adults.
- They can regrow legs, tails, and even parts of their heart and brain.
- Axolotls come from the lakes near Mexico City, and they are very rare in the wild today.
- A wild axolotl is usually dark and speckled, but pet axolotls can be pink, white, gold, or black.
- They hunt by sucking their food into their wide mouths, like a vacuum cleaner.
- The axolotl's smile is not really a smile — it is just the shape of its mouth. But it makes them look friendly, and that is one reason people love them so much.

## Why We Love Axolotls

Axolotls are a reminder that nature is full of surprises. An animal that stays young forever, smiles all the time, and can regrow its own body parts sounds like something from a storybook — but it is real, and it lives in the waters of Mexico.

Axolotls also teach us to look after our world. They are in danger because their home is shrinking and the water is getting dirty. By learning about them and protecting their lakes, we can help make sure these smiling salamanders are still around for children to wonder at for many years to come.
