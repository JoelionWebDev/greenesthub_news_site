Pet rats are clever, friendly little animals that make wonderful pets. They have soft fur, bright eyes, long whiskers, and long pink tails that help them balance when they climb. Pet rats love people, and they often run to the front of their cage when their owner comes near, hoping for a cuddle or a treat. They are very smart: they can learn their names, find their way through mazes, and even learn little tricks. They are also playful and curious, and they love to explore, climb, and play games with their owners.

## What is a pet rat?

A pet rat is a small rodent, which means it is a mammal with strong front teeth that keep growing all through its life. Pet rats are also called fancy rats, and they come in many colours and patterns, including white, black, brown, grey, cream, and spotted coats. Some pet rats have smooth fur, while others have curly fur or very little fur at all. A pet rat is about the size of a hamster, but with a longer body and a long, thin tail. It has big, round ears, a pointed nose, and long whiskers that help it feel its way around in the dark.

## Where do pet rats come from?

Pet rats come from wild brown rats, which are also called Norway rats. Wild brown rats live all over the world, in cities, farms, and fields, and they are very good at finding food and shelter. Long ago, people began to keep the tamest and friendliest brown rats as pets, and over many years they bred them to be gentle, calm, and comfortable around people. That is how the fancy rat was born. Pet rats are very different from wild rats: they are used to people, they enjoy being held, and they depend on their owners for food, water, and a safe home. Today, pet rats are loved by people all around the world.

## Life in the cage

Pet rats need a roomy cage with lots of things to do. A good rat cage is tall, with several levels, ramps, and shelves, because rats love to climb. The cage should have a soft bed at the bottom, made from safe bedding that keeps the rats warm and comfortable. Rats also need a cosy hiding place, like a small house or a hammock, where they can sleep during the day. Pet rats sleep a lot during the daytime and are most active in the morning and evening. The cage needs to be cleaned regularly, with fresh water every day and fresh food to nibble on. Rats are happiest when they have company, so it is best to keep at least two rats together, because a rat on its own can get lonely.

## What do pet rats eat?

Pet rats eat a mix of foods, and they enjoy a healthy diet just like we do. Their main food is a special rat food that comes in blocks or pellets, which has all the good things a rat needs to grow strong and stay healthy. On top of that, pet rats love fresh fruit and vegetables, such as apple slices, banana, peas, carrots, and broccoli. They also enjoy small treats like cooked egg, plain pasta, or a tiny piece of cheese now and then. Rats should always have fresh water to drink, in a bottle that hangs on the side of the cage. Some foods are not safe for rats, such as chocolate, raw beans, and fizzy drinks, so it is important to check before giving a rat a new food.

## How clever are pet rats?

Pet rats are some of the cleverest small pets you can keep. Scientists have found that rats can learn to find their way through mazes, remember where food is hidden, and even learn to press levers and open little doors to get a treat. Pet rats can learn their names and come when they are called, especially if a tasty treat is waiting for them. They can learn tricks too, like spinning in a circle, standing up on their back legs, or jumping through a hoop. Rats are also very social and clever in the way they talk to each other. They make soft sounds that we can hear, and they also make very high-pitched sounds that only other rats can hear. When a rat is happy, it sometimes grinds its teeth softly, which is called bruxing, and its eyes may wiggle with joy. That is a rat's way of smiling.

## Playing with your pet rat

Playing with a pet rat is one of the best parts of keeping one. Rats love to explore, so their owners can make fun playgrounds for them out of cardboard boxes, tunnels, and paper bags. A rat will happily climb up a sleeve, ride on a shoulder, or curl up in a pocket for a nap. Rats enjoy gentle games, like hiding a treat in a box for them to find, or letting them chase a small toy across the floor. It is important to be gentle with a rat and to let it come to you, especially at first, so that it learns to trust you. With a little patience, a pet rat becomes a true friend that looks forward to playtime every single day.

- A pet rat's front teeth keep growing all through its life, so it needs things to chew on.
- Rats use their long tails to help them balance when they climb.

- Rats wash themselves many times a day, just like cats do.

Pet rats are smart, gentle, and full of fun. They may be small, but they have big personalities, and they love their owners with all their little hearts. With a roomy cage, good food, a friend to play with, and plenty of cuddles, a pet rat will be happy and healthy for years. If you want a pet that is clever, friendly, and always ready to play, a pet rat might be just the little friend you have been looking for. Just remember to be kind, gentle, and patient, and your clever little rat will soon be running to greet you every day.
