If you have ever seen a cat with no fur at all, big ears like a bat, and skin covered in soft wrinkles, you have met a Sphynx cat. Sphynx cats look very different from other cats, and some people think they look a little bit strange at first. But anyone who has lived with a Sphynx will tell you that these cats are some of the sweetest, friendliest and funniest pets in the world. With no fur, big ears, wrinkled skin and a heart full of love, the Sphynx cat is one of the most unusual and affectionate pets in the world.

## A Cat with No Fur

The Sphynx cat is famous for having almost no fur. Its skin feels warm and soft, a bit like a peach, and it is covered in tiny wrinkles, especially around the face and shoulders. Sphynx cats come in many colours, from pink and cream to black and grey, and you can see their colour right on their skin.

The first hairless cats appeared in Canada in the 1960s, when a normal cat gave birth to a kitten with almost no fur. People were so charmed by the little kitten that they decided to breed more cats like it, and over time the Sphynx became a proper breed of its own. Today, Sphynx cats are loved all over the world, and they often appear in films and on television because they look so unusual.

Because they have no fur to keep them warm, Sphynx cats love warm places. They curl up under blankets, snuggle into soft beds, and sit in sunny spots by the window. In cold weather, some owners even dress their Sphynx cats in little jumpers to keep them cosy.

## A Cat That Loves People

Sphynx cats are some of the most loving cats you will ever meet. They adore people, and they want to be near their owners all the time. A Sphynx cat will follow you from room to room, sit on your lap while you read, and even climb onto your shoulder like a little parrot. They are not shy cats at all. In fact, they are more like dogs than cats in the way they love company.

They are also very playful. Sphynx cats love to chase toys, play hide and seek, and show off with silly jumps and spins. They are clever cats, and they can learn to fetch toys, walk on a lead, and even do little tricks. They are wonderful pets for children, because they are gentle, patient and full of fun.

Sphynx cats are also very talkative. They chirp, trill and meow to tell their owners what they want, whether it is food, a warm lap, or just a little bit of attention. They have big, lemon-shaped eyes that seem to look right into your heart, and they use their paws like hands to reach out and touch you.

## Bath Time for a Hairless Cat

Because Sphynx cats have no fur, their skin makes a lot of natural oil. On a furry cat, that oil is soaked up by the fur, but on a Sphynx it stays on the skin, so the cat needs regular baths to stay clean. Most Sphynx cats are used to bath time, and some of them even enjoy it. Owners give them a gentle bath with warm water and special cat shampoo, and then dry them off with a soft towel.

Sphynx cats also need their ears cleaned regularly, because their big ears collect dirt more easily than a furry cat's ears. And because they have no fur to protect them, they can get sunburned if they spend too long in strong sunshine. Owners keep them indoors on very hot days, or put a little bit of pet-safe sunscreen on their skin.

Their skin is warm to the touch, warmer than most cats, because their bodies work hard to stay warm without fur. That is why they eat a little more food than other cats, to give them the energy they need to stay cosy. Snuggling up with a warm Sphynx cat on a cold day is one of the nicest feelings in the world.

## A Cat Full of Character

Sphynx cats have big personalities. They are bold, curious and a little bit cheeky. They love to explore every corner of the house, climb to the highest shelf, and investigate anything new. They are not afraid of visitors, and they often greet guests at the door like a little host.

They get along well with other cats, dogs and children, as long as they are treated gently. Because they love company so much, they do not like being left alone for a long time. A Sphynx cat that is left on its own may become sad, so owners often get two Sphynx cats, so they can keep each other company.

Sphynx cats are also very intelligent. They learn quickly, and they love to solve puzzles and play games. Some owners teach them to open doors, ring bells, and even turn on taps. They are always up to something, and life with a Sphynx is never boring.

## A Special Kind of Friend

Owning a Sphynx cat is a special experience. They need a little more care than other cats, with their baths and their warm jumpers, but they give back so much love in return. They are loyal, affectionate and endlessly entertaining, and they become a true member of the family.

If you ever meet a Sphynx cat, do not be surprised if it climbs straight into your lap and starts purring. That is just its way of saying hello, and of telling you that it likes you. It may look unusual, but underneath those wrinkles is one of the biggest hearts in the whole cat world.

Next time you see a hairless cat, remember the Sphynx, the wrinkled little cat with the warm skin and the huge heart, waiting for a lap to sit on and a person to love.
