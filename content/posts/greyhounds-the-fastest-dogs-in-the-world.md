Imagine a dog that can run faster than a car driving through a town. That dog is the greyhound. Greyhounds are the fastest dogs in the world, and when they run, they look like arrows flying through the air.

But here is a funny secret about greyhounds: even though they are the fastest dogs on Earth, they are also some of the laziest. When they are not running, greyhounds love nothing more than curling up on a soft sofa and sleeping for hours. They are speed champions and champion nappers, all in one dog.

## Built for Speed

A greyhound's body is made for running fast. Everything about it helps it go quickly. Its legs are long and slim, its chest is deep, and its body is narrow and smooth. When a greyhound runs at full speed, its legs stretch out so far that for a moment, all four paws are off the ground at the same time. It is almost like the dog is flying.

Greyhounds are sight hounds, which means they hunt by sight. Long ago, people used them to chase animals across open fields. Their sharp eyes can spot something moving far away, and once they see it, they can chase it down with amazing speed. Today, most greyhounds are pets and racing dogs, but their sharp eyesight and love of running are still there.

## A Very Old Breed

Greyhounds are one of the oldest dog breeds in the world. People have kept greyhounds for thousands of years. Pictures of dogs that look just like greyhounds have been found in the tombs of ancient Egypt, painted on walls and carved into stone. Long ago, greyhounds were the dogs of kings and queens, and only important people were allowed to own them.

Even though they have been around for so long, greyhounds have not changed very much. They still look like the dogs in those ancient Egyptian paintings, with their long legs, slim bodies, and gentle faces. They are living links to the past.

## Gentle and Calm at Home

If you only saw a greyhound running, you might think it was a wild and restless dog. But the truth is quite the opposite. At home, greyhounds are calm, gentle, and quiet. They are often called couch potatoes, because they love to lie on a soft sofa and do nothing for hours. A greyhound can sleep for most of the day and be perfectly happy.

Greyhounds are very gentle with people, including children. They are not noisy dogs, and they rarely bark. They are polite and well behaved, and they love to be near their families. Many greyhound owners say that their dogs are like big, gentle cats, because they are so quiet and love to curl up in warm, comfy places.

## The Life of a Racing Greyhound

Greyhound racing is a sport where greyhounds run around a track, chasing a toy that moves along the ground. The dogs run very fast, and crowds of people cheer them on. Racing greyhounds are trained to run, and they love the excitement of the race. When the race starts, the dogs burst out of their starting boxes and run as fast as they can towards the finish line.

Not all greyhounds race, and many greyhounds that do race later become pets. When a racing greyhound retires, it needs a new home. Many kind people adopt these retired racers, and the dogs make wonderful pets. They are already used to people, they are calm and gentle, and they are happy to trade the racetrack for a soft sofa. Adopting a retired greyhound is a lovely way to give a fast dog a slow and happy life.

## Looking After a Greyhound

Greyhounds are easy dogs to look after. Their short, smooth coats need very little brushing, and they do not shed much hair. They are clean dogs, and they do not have a strong doggy smell. A quick brush once a week is usually enough to keep their coats looking shiny.

Greyhounds need good food to stay strong and healthy, and they need fresh water to drink. They also need regular visits to the vet, who checks that they are fit and well. Because greyhounds have thin skin and little fat, they can get cold easily, so they need a warm coat on cold days and a cosy bed to sleep in at night.

One important thing to remember about greyhounds is that they should never be let off their lead in an open place unless it is safely fenced. If a greyhound sees something small and fast moving in the distance, like a squirrel or a rabbit, it may chase it without thinking. Their hunting instinct is very strong, and once they start running, they can go a long way very quickly. A fenced garden or a safe enclosed park is the best place for a greyhound to run free.

## Why Greyhounds Are Special

Greyhounds are special because they are full of surprises. They are the fastest dogs in the world, but also some of the calmest. They look like serious racing dogs, but they are really gentle, loving pets who just want a soft place to sleep and a kind person to love them.

If you ever meet a greyhound, you will see a dog that is tall, slim, and graceful, with kind eyes and a gentle nature. And if you are lucky enough to see one run, you will never forget it. Watching a greyhound run is like watching the wind itself, fast, free, and beautiful. Then, when the run is over, the greyhound will happily curl up beside you and fall fast asleep, dreaming of its next race.
