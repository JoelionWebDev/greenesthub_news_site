## What is a pig?

A pig is a farm animal with a round body, short legs, a curly tail and a flat nose called a snout. Pigs come in many colours. Some are pink, some are black, some are brown, and some have spots. Their skin is usually covered with short, rough hairs called bristles.

A female pig is called a sow, a male pig is called a boar, and a baby pig is called a piglet. Piglets are small, pink and very cute, with tiny snouts and floppy ears. They love to snuggle up close to their mother and to each other to stay warm.

Pigs have a wonderful sense of smell. Their snout is strong and flexible, and they use it to dig in the ground and sniff out food. A pig can smell food hidden under the soil.

Pigs are much cleverer than many people think. They can learn new things, remember where food is hidden, and even learn to open simple gates.

## Where do pigs live?

Pigs live on farms all over the world. On a farm, pigs live in a pen or a sty, which is a fenced area with a shelter where they can sleep. A good pig pen has clean straw for bedding, fresh water to drink, and a muddy patch where the pigs can roll around.

Wild pigs, called wild boars, live in forests and grasslands. They make their homes in thick bushes and under trees, where they can hide and rest during the day.

Pigs like to live in groups. On a farm, they sleep close together in a pile, which keeps them warm at night. They are social animals, and they like the company of other pigs.

## What do pigs eat?

Pigs eat almost anything. They are omnivores, which means they eat both plants and other foods. On a farm, pigs are given grains such as corn and barley, vegetables, fruits and special pig food. They also enjoy kitchen scraps like vegetable peelings and leftover fruit, but they should never be given food that is bad for them.

In the wild, pigs dig in the ground with their snouts to find roots, bulbs, insects and small creatures. They also eat nuts, berries, leaves and mushrooms that fall from trees. A pig's strong snout works like a little shovel, turning over the soil to find tasty treats underneath.

Pigs need fresh, clean water every day. They drink a lot of water, especially in hot weather. Farmers make sure the pigs always have water, because a pig without water can become sick very quickly.

Piglets drink their mother's milk when they are born. As they grow bigger, they start to eat solid food, just like the grown-up pigs.

## Why do pigs roll in mud?

One of the most famous things about pigs is that they love to roll in mud. But they do not do it because they are dirty. In fact, pigs are quite clean animals when they have enough space. They roll in mud to cool down, because pigs cannot sweat the way people do. The cool mud helps them feel comfortable on a hot day.

The mud also works like a natural sunscreen. It covers the pig's skin and protects it from the sun. It also helps keep away flies and other biting insects.

## Pig families

Pigs are loving mothers. A sow usually gives birth to several piglets at a time. The piglets are born in a warm nest made of straw, and the mother stays close to them, feeding them and keeping them safe.

Newborn piglets are tiny and wobbly on their feet, but they learn fast. Within a short time, they can walk, find their mother's milk and follow her around. Piglets love to play. They run, jump, chase each other and play little games, just like puppies do.

As piglets grow, they stay close to their mother until they are old enough to live with the other pigs. Pig families are close, and pigs can tell each other apart by their voices and smells.

## How clever are pigs?

Pigs are one of the cleverest farm animals. Scientists who study animals have found that pigs can solve puzzles, learn tricks and remember things for a long time. A pig can learn to push a lever to get food, find its way through a maze, and even play simple video games with its snout.

Pigs can also learn from each other. If one pig finds out how to open a gate, other pigs watching nearby may learn to do it too. Pigs have good memories, and they can remember the places where they found food, even after a long time.

Pigs are curious animals. They love to explore new things, sniff new objects and dig in new places. Farmers give pigs toys, balls and straw to play with, because a bored pig can become unhappy.

Some people keep pigs as pets. Pet pigs need a lot of space, good food, fresh water and plenty of attention. They can be trained to walk on a lead and to use a litter tray, a bit like a cat. But pigs grow big, so a pet pig needs a home with a garden and a family that understands what pigs need.

## Pigs and people

People have kept pigs for thousands of years. Pigs are useful farm animals because they give us meat, and their skin can be used to make leather. In some countries, pigs are also trained to help people find truffles, which are a special kind of mushroom that grows underground and is very valuable.

## Fun facts about pigs

- Pigs cannot sweat, which is why they love rolling in cool mud.
- A pig's snout is very strong and works like a little shovel.
- Pigs are clever enough to learn their own names and come when called.
- Baby pigs are called piglets, and a group of piglets is called a litter.
- Pigs have an excellent sense of smell, better than a dog's in some ways.
- A pig's curly tail is one of its most famous features.
- Pigs can run surprisingly fast when they want to.
- Pigs talk to each other with grunts, snorts and squeals.
- Some pigs are trained to find truffles, a special mushroom that grows underground.
- Pigs love to play, and they enjoy toys, balls and piles of straw.
