Have you ever picked up a cat and felt it go completely floppy in your arms, like a soft toy? That is a Ragdoll cat. Ragdolls are famous for relaxing totally when they are held, going limp like a rag doll — and that is how they got their name. They are big, beautiful cats with blue eyes, silky fur and the sweetest, calmest nature. Many people say that Ragdolls are more like dogs than cats, because they follow their owners around the house and love to be near people.

## A Cat That Goes Floppy

The most famous thing about Ragdolls is their floppiness. When you pick one up, many Ragdolls relax all their muscles and hang limply in your arms. It is very funny and very sweet. Not every Ragdoll does it every time, but most of them are happy to be held and cuddled for a long time. They trust their owners completely.

This relaxed nature makes Ragdolls wonderful pets for families. They are not jumpy or nervous. They like to be around people, and they do not mind being picked up by children, as long as everyone is gentle. A Ragdoll will often flop down on the floor and roll over, inviting you to stroke its tummy.

## Big, Blue-Eyed and Beautiful

Ragdolls are large cats. A grown Ragdoll can be much bigger than an ordinary cat, with a long body and strong legs. They take a long time to grow up — it can take three or four years before a Ragdoll is fully grown. But they are worth the wait, because they grow into truly beautiful cats.

Their eyes are a lovely bright blue, which is unusual for cats. Their fur is semi-long and silky, and it feels soft when you stroke it. Ragdolls have a special pattern called colourpoint, which means their faces, ears, paws and tails are darker than the rest of their bodies. They come in colours like seal, blue, chocolate and lilac, often with white markings. Some Ragdolls have white paws that look like little mittens, and some have a white stripe down their noses.

Unlike some long-haired cats, Ragdoll fur does not tangle very easily, because it does not have a thick woolly undercoat. That means they are easier to groom than you might think. A gentle brush a couple of times a week is usually enough to keep their coats looking lovely.

## A Cat That Acts Like a Dog

Ragdolls are often called puppy-cats, because they behave a bit like dogs. They follow their owners from room to room, greet them at the door when they come home, and even come when they are called. Some Ragdolls learn to play fetch, chasing a toy and bringing it back to you, just like a dog would.

They are also very chatty, but in a soft, gentle way. A Ragdoll does not usually yowl loudly. It makes sweet little trills and chirps, as if it is having a quiet conversation with you. If you talk to a Ragdoll, it will often answer back with a soft meow.

Ragdolls love company and do not like to be left alone for long periods. They are happiest in a home where someone is around most of the day. If you work away from home all day, a Ragdoll might be lonely, so it is best to have two cats, or to choose a more independent breed. A Ragdoll's greatest wish is simply to be near you.

## Where Did Ragdolls Come From?

Ragdolls are a fairly new breed of cat. They were first bred in California, in the United States, in the 1960s. A breeder called Ann Baker noticed a lovely white cat with a gentle nature, and she bred from her to create a new kind of cat that was big, beautiful and very relaxed. The breed became popular quickly, and today Ragdolls are loved all over the world.

Because they are a pedigree breed, Ragdolls are usually bought from a breeder. But sometimes you can find Ragdolls and Ragdoll crosses in animal shelters too. If you are thinking of getting a Ragdoll, it is worth checking shelters first — you might find a beautiful cat that needs a home.

## Living with a Ragdoll

Ragdolls are indoor cats. They are so trusting and relaxed that they are not good at looking after themselves outside. They might wander up to a stranger, or fail to run away from danger. So most Ragdoll owners keep their cats indoors, or let them out only into a safe, enclosed garden.

Indoors, Ragdolls need things to keep them happy. They love a tall scratching post, a cosy bed, and a window seat where they can watch birds outside. They enjoy playing with toys, especially ones they can chase and pounce on. Even though they are calm, they still need play every day to stay healthy and happy.

Ragdolls eat good quality cat food and need fresh water every day. Because they are big cats, they eat a little more than smaller breeds. They also need regular visits to the vet, to make sure they stay healthy.
