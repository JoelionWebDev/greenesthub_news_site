Penguins are some of the most loved birds in the whole world. They cannot fly through the sky like other birds, but that does not stop them from being amazing. Penguins waddle on land, slide on their bellies on the ice, and swim through cold water like little black-and-white rockets. They look like they are wearing smart black suits with white shirts. When you see a group of penguins standing together, it almost looks like a crowd of tiny people at a fancy party. Penguins live mostly in the cold south of our planet, and they are full of surprises.

## What is a penguin?

A penguin is a bird that cannot fly, but it is a wonderful swimmer. Penguins have wings, but their wings are not made for flying in the air. Instead, their wings are short and strong, more like flippers. They use them to push through the water, steering and speeding along. Their bodies are shaped like torpedoes, which helps them move fast under the water. Penguins have feathers that keep them warm and dry in freezing cold water. Under their feathers they have a thick layer of fat that works like a warm coat. There are many kinds of penguins, and they come in different sizes. The biggest penguins are the emperor penguins, and the smallest are the little penguins. All penguins are birds, even though they never fly.

## Where do penguins live?

Most penguins live in the Southern Hemisphere, which is the bottom half of the Earth. Many penguins live in Antarctica, the coldest place on Earth, where the ground is covered in ice and snow. Other penguins live in places like South Africa, New Zealand, Australia, and South America. Some penguins live on the Galapagos Islands, which are near the equator, where it is much warmer. Penguins do not live in the North Pole. That is a fun fact many people get wrong. Penguins live near the sea because the sea gives them their food. They need cold water to swim in and land or ice to rest on. Wherever they live, penguins are always close to the ocean.

## What do penguins eat?

Penguins eat food from the sea. Their favourite foods are small fish, squid, and tiny sea animals called krill. Krill look like very small shrimps, and penguins eat lots of them. A penguin dives into the cold water and chases its food, catching it with its strong beak. Penguins do not have teeth, but their beaks and tongues help them hold slippery fish. Some penguins can dive very deep into the water to find food. They hold their breath while they hunt under the sea. After a good swim and a full meal, a penguin returns to land to rest. Penguins need to eat a lot because swimming and staying warm in cold water uses lots of energy.

## How do penguins move?

On land, penguins waddle from side to side. Their short legs and big bodies make them waddle in a funny, cute way. When the ground is covered in snow and ice, penguins often slide on their bellies. This sliding is called tobogganing. It is much faster than waddling, and it looks like lots of fun. Penguins can also hop up small rocks and climb steep snowy hills. In the water, everything changes. Penguins become fast and graceful. They zoom through the water and leap out of it like little torpedoes. Some penguins can jump out of the water and land on ice or rocks. Watching a penguin swim is like watching a bird fly underwater.

## Penguin families

Penguins are very caring parents. Most penguins lay one or two eggs. The mother and father take turns looking after the egg. Emperor penguins are famous for this. The father emperor penguin keeps the egg warm on his feet, under a special fold of skin, while the mother goes to the sea to eat. He stands in the freezing cold for weeks, holding the egg safe. When the chick hatches, both parents help feed it. Penguin chicks are fluffy and grey, and they stay close to their parents for warmth and food. When the parents go fishing, the chicks huddle together in groups to stay warm. These groups are called creches. As the chicks grow, their fluffy feathers are replaced by smooth waterproof feathers, and they learn to swim.

## Staying warm in the cold

Antarctica is one of the coldest places on Earth, so how do penguins stay warm? Penguins have clever ways to beat the cold. Their feathers are packed tightly together, keeping cold water and wind out. Under their skin, a thick layer of fat keeps their bodies warm. Penguins also huddle together in big groups. Hundreds of penguins stand close together, sharing body heat. The penguins on the outside of the huddle slowly move to the inside, so everyone gets a turn in the warm middle. This is a wonderful example of teamwork. Young penguins stay warm by snuggling next to their parents or in a creche with other chicks. Penguins are built for the cold in every way.

## Penguins and people

People love penguins. We watch them in films and books, and we visit them in zoos and aquariums. Scientists study penguins to learn about the cold places where they live. Penguins can tell us a lot about how the ocean is changing, because they depend on the sea for food. When the sea is healthy, penguins do well. People must take care of the places where penguins live. Keeping the sea clean helps penguins find food. Protecting the ice and the beaches where penguins rest and raise their chicks is very important. When we look after penguins, we also look after the whole ocean. Penguins remind us that even in the coldest places on Earth, life is full of warmth, family, and fun.

## Fun facts about penguins

- Penguins cannot fly in the air, but they can "fly" through the water with their strong flippers.
- Emperor penguin fathers keep the egg warm on their feet while the mother goes to sea to eat.
- When penguins slide on their bellies over snow and ice, it is called tobogganing.
- Penguins huddle together in big groups to share body heat and stay warm in the freezing cold.
- A group of penguins on land is sometimes called a waddle, and a group in the water is sometimes called a raft.
