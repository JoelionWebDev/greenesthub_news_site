Imagine you are walking beside a pond, and you see a lizard. That is not very surprising. Lizards are common animals, and they often sit in the sun or scurry away when you get too close. But what if that lizard suddenly ran across the top of the water, as if the pond was a road? You would rub your eyes and look again, because it seems impossible. Yet there is a real lizard that can do just that. It is called a basilisk lizard, and it is famous for walking on water.

Basilisk lizards live in the rainforests of Central and South America, in warm, wet places where there are lots of trees, rivers, and ponds. They are also known as Jesus Christ lizards, because of the way they seem to walk on water, just like the story of Jesus walking on the Sea of Galilee. It is a funny nickname, but it shows just how amazing people find this lizard to be.

## A Green Lizard With a Crown

Basilisk lizards are lovely to look at. Most of them are bright green, which helps them hide among the leaves of the rainforest. Some have stripes or spots, and some have a little blue or yellow on their bodies. Their green colour is perfect camouflage, which means it helps them blend in with their surroundings so that other animals cannot spot them easily.

Male basilisk lizards have something extra special. They have a tall crest on their head and along their back, a bit like a crown or a small sail. The crest makes them look grand and impressive, and it helps them attract a mate. Female basilisk lizards are also green, but their crests are smaller. Both males and females have long tails, long toes, and strong legs, and all of these help them in their amazing water-walking trick.

Basilisk lizards are not huge. Most are about as long as your arm from your elbow to your fingertips, including their long tail. Their bodies are slim and light, which helps them stay on top of the water.

## How Do They Walk on Water?

This is the question everyone asks, and the answer is all about speed and special feet. When a basilisk lizard is scared, it does not swim away. Instead, it stands up on its back legs and runs across the surface of the water. It moves its legs very, very fast, slapping the water with its big, flat feet.

Each time its foot hits the water, it pushes down hard and creates a little pocket of air and a splash. That push gives the lizard a lift, just enough to keep it from sinking. Then, before the foot has time to sink, the lizard lifts it up and puts the other foot down. It does this again and again, very quickly, and that is how it stays on top of the water. It is a bit like running across a trampoline, bouncing from one foot to the other before you have time to fall through.

The lizard's long toes help, too. They spread out wide when they hit the water, which gives the lizard more surface to push against. Its light body also helps, because it does not need as much lift to stay up. Young, small basilisk lizards are even better at walking on water than big, heavy adults, because they are lighter and can move their legs even faster.

A basilisk lizard cannot walk on water forever. It can run across a small pond or a quiet stretch of river, but if the water is too wide, it will eventually get tired and start to sink. That is all right, because basilisk lizards are also very good swimmers.

## Life in the Rainforest

Basilisk lizards live in trees and bushes near water. They like to sit on branches overhanging a river or a pond, where they can bask in the sun and watch for food. If danger comes, they can drop straight down into the water below and run away across the surface, or dive under and swim to safety. Living near water gives them a quick escape route, and that helps them survive in a forest full of hungry animals.

During the day, basilisk lizards are busy. They bask in the sun to warm up, they look for food, and they climb through the trees. At night, they sleep on branches, holding on tight with their toes and tail.

## What Do Basilisk Lizards Eat?

Basilisk lizards eat a wide range of food. They love insects, such as crickets, grasshoppers, and beetles, and they catch them with a quick snap of their jaws. They also eat spiders, worms, and other small animals. Sometimes they eat fruit, berries, and flowers, too, which gives them extra energy and vitamins.

They are patient hunters. A basilisk lizard will sit very still on a branch, watching carefully, and when an insect comes close enough, it will dart forward and grab it in a flash.

Baby basilisk lizards are born from eggs, which the mother lays in a safe, hidden place. The babies hatch on their own and are ready to look after themselves almost straight away.

## Staying Safe From Danger

The rainforest is full of animals that would love to eat a basilisk lizard. Birds of prey, snakes, and larger lizards are all dangers. But the basilisk lizard has many ways to stay safe. Its green colour helps it hide among the leaves, its speed helps it run away, and its water-walking trick is its most famous escape.

If a bird swoops down, the lizard can drop into the water and run across the surface to the other side. If a snake comes too close, it can dive under the water and hide. It can also climb high into a tree, where many hunters cannot follow. With so many escape routes, the basilisk lizard is a hard animal to catch.

## A Lizard Like No Other

The basilisk lizard is a wonderful animal. It is bright green, it has a crown-like crest, it climbs trees, it swims, and it can run across the top of the water. It is one of the most surprising lizards in the world, and it shows us that nature is full of amazing tricks.

Next time you are near a pond, imagine a little green lizard running across the water, with its legs moving so fast you can hardly see them. It is not magic, it is nature at its cleverest.
