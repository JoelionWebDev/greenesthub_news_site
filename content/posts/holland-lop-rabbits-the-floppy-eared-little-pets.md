If you could design the cutest rabbit in the world, what would it look like? It would probably be small and round, with soft fur, a sweet face, and long ears that flop down on either side of its head. That rabbit already exists, and it is called the Holland Lop. It is one of the smallest pet rabbits in the world, and one of the most loved. With its floppy ears and gentle nature, the Holland Lop has stolen the hearts of children and families everywhere.

The Holland Lop comes from the Netherlands, which is a country in Europe that is also called Holland. That is where it gets its name. Lop means that its ears hang down, instead of standing up straight. Many rabbits have ears that stand up, but the Holland Lop has ears that flop down, like two soft ribbons on either side of its face. When a Holland Lop is a baby, its ears may stand up at first, but as it grows, they flop down and stay down. It is one of the sweetest sights in the world to see a tiny Holland Lop with its ears flopping as it hops along.

## A Tiny Rabbit With a Big Heart

Holland Lops are small rabbits, much smaller than the rabbits you might see in a field. They have short, round bodies, short legs, and a face that looks a little bit like a tiny bulldog, with a flat nose and big eyes. Their fur can be many colours, including white, black, brown, orange, grey and spotted patterns. Some Holland Lops are one colour all over, and some have patches and spots that make them look like little works of art.

Even though they are small, Holland Lops have big personalities. They are known for being friendly, gentle and playful. They love to be with people, and they often enjoy being petted and cuddled. A Holland Lop may nudge your hand with its nose to ask for a stroke, or it may flop down on its side to show that it feels safe and happy. When a rabbit flops down like that, it is a sign that it trusts you and feels at home.

## How Holland Lops Play

Holland Lops love to play, and they need time to run, hop and explore every day. They are very curious rabbits. They love to sniff new things, dig in soft earth, and hop through tunnels and boxes. A cardboard box with a hole cut in it can be the best toy in the world for a Holland Lop. It will hop in and out, hide inside, and pop out again, as if it is playing hide and seek.

One of the happiest things a rabbit can do is called a binky. A binky is when a rabbit jumps up in the air, twists its body, and kicks its legs, all at the same time. It looks silly and wonderful, and it means the rabbit is very happy. Holland Lops binky when they are excited, when they have plenty of space to run, and when they feel safe. If you ever see a Holland Lop binky, you will know that it is having the best day ever.

Holland Lops also love to chew. Their teeth never stop growing, so they need to chew on safe things like hay and wooden toys to keep their teeth healthy. Hay is the most important food for a rabbit, and a Holland Lop should have fresh hay to eat and chew all day long.

## A Gentle Friend for Children

Holland Lops are often a good choice for families with children, because they are gentle and they love people. But they are still small and delicate animals, and they need to be handled with great care. A rabbit should never be picked up by its ears, and it should always be held close to the body, with its back end supported, so that it feels safe. Young children should always sit on the floor when they hold a rabbit, and a grown-up should always be nearby to help.

Rabbits are not like dogs. They do not always want to be picked up and cuddled. Sometimes they would rather sit beside you, or hop around your feet, or rest quietly in your lap. The best way to make friends with a Holland Lop is to be patient, quiet and gentle. Sit on the floor, let the rabbit come to you, and offer it a small treat. Before long, it will learn to trust you, and it may hop over to say hello all by itself.

## Looking After a Holland Lop

A Holland Lop needs a safe home, with plenty of space to move around. It needs a clean hutch or a safe indoor area, fresh water every day, and lots of hay. It also needs time outside its home each day, in a safe space where it can run and play. Rabbits are clever animals, and they can learn to use a litter tray, just like a cat. Some Holland Lops even learn to come when they are called by name.

Rabbits also need to be kept safe from other animals, from very hot or very cold weather, and from loud noises.

Grooming is also important. Holland Lops have soft fur that needs to be brushed gently, especially when they are shedding.

## A Little Rabbit to Love

The Holland Lop may be small, but it has a huge amount of love to give. It is a rabbit that loves to play, to explore, and to be close to the people it trusts. It is a rabbit that will make you smile every time you see its floppy ears and its sweet face. And it is a rabbit that will teach you how to be patient, gentle and kind.

So, if you ever meet a Holland Lop, sit down quietly and wait for it to come to you. Watch it hop, sniff and explore. You will be looking at one of the most adorable little pets in the world, a tiny rabbit with a big heart, and a friend that will hop its way into your heart and stay there for ever.
