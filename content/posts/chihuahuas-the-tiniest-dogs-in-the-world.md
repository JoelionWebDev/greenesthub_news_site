The chihuahua is the smallest dog breed in the world, but it has a huge heart and a big personality packed into its tiny body. Some chihuahuas are so small they can fit in a handbag, yet they act as if they are the biggest dog in the room. They are brave, loyal and full of energy, and they have been winning people's hearts for hundreds of years.

## A Little Dog from Mexico

Chihuahuas come from Mexico, and they are named after a state in Mexico called Chihuahua. Long ago, the people who lived there kept small dogs that looked like chihuahuas. These little dogs were treasured companions, and they were often kept by families as pets and friends.

Over time, people in other countries fell in love with chihuahuas too. Travellers brought them to the United States and to Europe, and soon they became popular pets all over the world. Today, chihuahuas live in homes in almost every country, from big cities to small villages.

There are two kinds of chihuahuas. One has a smooth, short coat, and the other has a long, soft coat. They come in many colours, including brown, black, white, cream and mixed colours. Some have spots, and some have a single solid colour.

## Small Body, Big Personality

Chihuahuas are famous for their big personalities. They are brave and confident, and they are not afraid of dogs much bigger than they are. A chihuahua will happily bark at a dog ten times its size, as if to say, "I am in charge here!" This bravery is funny to watch, but owners need to keep an eye on their little dogs so they do not get into trouble.

They are also very loyal. A chihuahua usually picks one special person in the family and follows them everywhere. It will sit on their lap, sleep on their bed and wait by the door for them to come home. They love cuddles and attention, and they are happiest when they are close to their favourite person.

Chihuahuas are clever dogs too. They can learn tricks, they can be trained to come when called, and they enjoy playing games. They like to play with toys, chase balls and explore the house and garden. Even though they are small, they need play and exercise every day to stay happy and healthy.

## Looking After a Chihuahua

Because chihuahuas are so small, they need gentle care. They should be picked up carefully, with both hands, and children should sit down when they hold one, so the little dog does not fall. Chihuahuas are delicate, and a fall can hurt them.

They also get cold easily, because they are so small and have thin coats. That is why you often see chihuahuas wearing little jumpers and coats in cold weather. They look very cute in their tiny outfits, and the clothes really do help keep them warm.

Chihuahuas need good food in small amounts, fresh water, and regular visits to the vet to stay healthy. Their teeth need care too, because small dogs can have crowded teeth. Brushing a chihuahua's teeth and giving it safe things to chew helps keep its mouth healthy.

Chihuahuas can live a long time, often well into their teens, which means they can be a friend for many years. That is a big promise, so families should be sure they are ready to look after a little dog for a long time before they bring one home.

## The Famous Chihuahua

Chihuahuas have become famous in films, adverts and books. You may have seen a chihuahua in a movie, sitting in a handbag or riding in a car, looking very proud. Their big eyes, big ears and tiny size make them hard to forget.

Their ears are one of their cutest features. They are large and stand upright, like two little triangles on top of their heads. When a chihuahua is curious, its ears perk up even higher. When it is relaxed, its ears may tilt a little to the side.

Chihuahuas also have a soft spot on the top of their heads when they are puppies, a little like the soft spot on a human baby's head. This usually closes up as they grow, and it is one of the things that makes chihuahua puppies extra special.

## Is a Chihuahua Right for You?

Chihuahuas are wonderful pets for the right family. They are best in homes where people are gentle and calm, and where someone is around for much of the day, because chihuahuas do not like being left alone for a long time. They can be a little shy with strangers, so they need to meet lots of different people when they are young to help them feel confident.

They are great for people who live in flats or small homes, because they do not need a big garden. A short walk and some playtime indoors is usually enough exercise for a chihuahua. But they still need fresh air, sunshine and new things to see and smell every day.

## A Tiny Friend with a Huge Heart

The chihuahua proves that the best things really do come in small packages. This tiny dog from Mexico has a heart full of love, a brave spirit and a personality far bigger than its body. It reminds us that you do not need to be big to be brave, or to be loved.

If you ever meet a chihuahua, remember to be gentle, let it sniff your hand first, and speak softly. You might just make a new friend — a very small friend, with a very big heart.
