There is a dog that works alongside police officers, soldiers and search-and-rescue teams all over the world. It is fast, clever and brave, and it can learn a new job in just a few tries. It is called the Belgian Malinois, and it is one of the hardest-working dogs on the planet. Smart, fast and endlessly loyal, the Belgian Malinois is the hard-working dog that helps police, soldiers and search teams keep people safe every day.

## A Dog from Belgium

The Belgian Malinois comes from Belgium, a small country in Europe. Long ago, Belgian farmers used these dogs to herd sheep and guard their farms. The dogs were so clever and quick that they could round up a whole flock of sheep with just a few commands from their owner. They were also good at guarding the farm, because they were brave and alert, and they barked loudly when strangers came near.

The Malinois is named after the city of Malines, in Belgium, where this kind of dog was first bred. It has a short, smooth coat that is usually a warm fawn colour, with a black mask on its face and black tips on its ears. It looks a little bit like a German shepherd, but it is smaller, lighter and even faster. Its ears stand up tall and straight, and its eyes are dark, bright and full of energy.

Today, Belgian Malinois dogs still work on farms, but they are much more famous for their work with the police and the military. They are one of the most popular working dogs in the world, and they are trusted to do some of the hardest jobs of all.

## The Smartest Pupils in Dog School

Belgian Malinois dogs are famous for their intelligence. They learn new commands very quickly, and they love to work. In dog training school, a Malinois will often learn a new trick after just a few tries, while other dogs might need many more. They are also very good at solving problems, and they can figure out how to open doors, find hidden objects and follow a trail that is hours old.

They have an amazing sense of smell, which is one of the reasons they are so useful to the police. A Malinois can sniff out things that people cannot see or smell, such as lost people, hidden objects, or things that are dangerous. They are trained to search buildings, cars and open fields, and they can cover a large area very quickly.

Malinois dogs also have a wonderful memory. They remember people, places and commands for a long time, and they can follow a long list of instructions without getting confused. This makes them perfect partners for people who need a dog they can rely on in difficult situations.

## Heroes on Four Legs

Belgian Malinois dogs do many heroic jobs. They work with the police to find missing people, catch criminals and keep crowds safe. They work with soldiers to search for dangerous things and protect their teams. They also work in search-and-rescue teams, helping to find people who are lost in forests, buried under rubble after an earthquake, or trapped in the snow.

One of the most famous Belgian Malinois dogs was a military dog named Cairo, who took part in a very important mission with soldiers in the United States. Cairo's story showed the world just how brave and clever these dogs can be. There are many other Malinois heroes whose stories are not as famous, but who help people every single day.

These dogs are also trained to help people with disabilities, and some of them work as service dogs, guiding people who cannot see or helping people who need extra support. Their loyalty and intelligence make them wonderful helpers in many different ways.

## A Dog That Needs a Job

Belgian Malinois dogs are full of energy, and they need a lot of exercise and training every day. They are not couch potatoes. A Malinois that does not have enough to do can become bored and unhappy, and it may start chewing things or digging holes in the garden. That is why they are best suited to people who have plenty of time to play with them, train them and take them on long walks and runs.

They love to play games such as fetch, tug-of-war and hide and seek. They enjoy agility courses, where they run, jump and climb over obstacles, and they are very good at it. They also love to learn new tricks, and they are happiest when they have a job to do, even if that job is just carrying a toy or fetching a ball.

Malinois dogs are very loyal to their families, and they form strong bonds with the people they live with. They are protective of children, and they love to be part of family life. But because they are so energetic and strong, they need an owner who understands them and can give them the training and exercise they need.

## A True Working Partner

The Belgian Malinois shows us that dogs are not just pets. They are partners, helpers and heroes, and they can do amazing things when they work together with people. With its quick mind, its brave heart and its endless energy, the Malinois is a true working dog, and one of the most impressive dogs in the world.

Next time you see a police dog at work, take a closer look. It might just be a Belgian Malinois, the clever working dog, doing its job with a wagging tail and a happy heart.
