Imagine a chicken so small that it could fit in the palm of your hand. A chicken that stands up straight like a little soldier, puffs out its chest, and struts around the garden as if it owns the place. That is a Serama chicken, the smallest chicken in the whole world. From Malaysia comes the smallest chicken in the world, a tiny bird with a proud chest, a big personality, and a walk like a little soldier on parade.

## A Tiny Chicken from Malaysia

Serama chickens come from Malaysia, a country in South East Asia. They were first bred there many years ago, when people crossed small chickens together to make an even smaller, prettier bird. The result was the Serama, a chicken that is famous for being tiny. A grown Serama rooster is only about as tall as a can of drink, and it weighs less than a bag of sugar. The hens are even smaller.

Serama chickens come in many different colours, from white and black to red, brown and speckled. Some have feathers that shine like silk, while others have feathers that look like a patchwork quilt. No two Seramas look exactly alike, which makes each one special. They have small combs on their heads, bright eyes and little beaks that are always busy pecking at the ground.

Even though they are tiny, Seramas have a very big presence. They hold their heads high, puff out their chests and hold their tails up straight behind them. When they walk, they strut proudly, like a soldier on parade. It is a funny sight to see such a tiny bird acting like a king.

## A Chicken with a Big Personality

Serama chickens are known for being very friendly and tame. Unlike some chickens that run away from people, Seramas often walk right up to their owners and peck gently at their shoes, hoping for a treat. They love to be held, and they will happily sit in your hand or on your lap, looking around with their bright eyes.

They are also very brave for their size. A Serama rooster will crow loudly, even though his voice is small and squeaky, and he will stand up to much bigger chickens without fear. Serama hens are good mothers, and they look after their chicks with great care, calling them with soft clucks and showing them where to find food.

Serama chickens are clever too. They learn to recognise their owners, and they come running when they hear their owner's voice or see a treat. They love to explore the garden, scratching in the soil and chasing insects, and they enjoy a dust bath, where they roll around in dry soil to keep their feathers clean.

## Eggs Like Little Jewels

Serama hens lay small eggs that are about the size of a large grape. The eggs are white or cream coloured, and they are so small that it takes several of them to make one normal-sized egg. But they are still good to eat, and some people love to collect them because they are so cute.

Serama hens are good at sitting on their eggs until they hatch. The mother hen keeps the eggs warm under her soft feathers, turning them gently with her beak. After about three weeks, the tiny chicks hatch out, and they are the smallest chicks you will ever see. They are fluffy, wobbly and very cute, and they follow their mother everywhere, peeping loudly.

Because Serama chickens are so small, they do not need a big space to live in. They can live happily in a small garden or even in a large indoor pen, as long as they have food, water and a warm, safe place to sleep. This makes them perfect pets for people who do not have a big farm.

## Keeping Seramas Warm and Safe

Serama chickens come from a warm country, so they do not like cold weather. In places where the winters are cold, owners keep their Seramas in a warm, dry hen house, and they may even bring them inside the house on very cold nights. Some owners give their Seramas little jackets to wear, which looks very funny but keeps the tiny birds warm.

They also need protection from animals that might want to catch them, such as cats, dogs and birds of prey. Because they are so small, they are easy prey, so owners keep them in a safe pen with a roof, where they can scratch and play without danger. Seramas are happy in a small flock, and they love to have other Seramas to play with.

Serama chickens eat a mixture of grain, seeds and greens, and they love treats such as mealworms, corn and bits of fruit. They need fresh water every day, and a little bit of grit to help them digest their food. With good care, a Serama can live for many years and bring joy to its owner every single day.

## A Little Chicken with a Big Heart

The Serama chicken shows us that the smallest animals can have the biggest personalities. With its proud chest, its tiny body and its friendly nature, the Serama is a true star among chickens. It may be the smallest chicken in the world, but it has a heart as big as any other bird.

Next time you see a chicken, imagine one small enough to fit in your hand, standing tall and proud like a little soldier. That is the Serama, the tiniest chicken of all, and one of the sweetest pets you could ever meet.
