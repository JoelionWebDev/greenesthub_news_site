Imagine an animal as big as a small car, with skin that looks like armour and a horn on its nose. That is a rhino! Rhinos are some of the biggest land animals in the world, and even though they look tough, they are mostly calm, gentle plant-eaters. They spend their days munching grass and leaves, wallowing in mud, and looking after their babies.

## What Is a Rhino?

A rhino is a very large mammal with a heavy body, short legs, and thick skin. Its skin can look grey or brown, and it often has folds that make it look as if it is wearing armour. Rhinos have poor eyesight, so they cannot see very far, but they have a very good sense of smell and good hearing. Their ears can turn in different directions to listen for sounds.

The most famous part of a rhino is its horn. Some rhinos have one horn, and some have two. A rhino horn is not made of bone. It is made of keratin, the same stuff that our fingernails and hair are made of. Rhinos use their horns to dig, break branches, and protect their babies. There are different kinds of rhinos in Africa and Asia, including the white rhino, the black rhino, and the Indian rhino. White rhinos have a wide mouth for grazing grass, while black rhinos have a pointed lip for picking leaves.

## Where Do Rhinos Live?

Rhinos live in warm places with plenty of plants to eat. In Africa, many rhinos live on grasslands called savannas, with wide open spaces, tall grasses, and scattered trees. In Asia, rhinos live in grasslands, forests, and swampy areas where the ground is soft and wet. They like places with water nearby, because they enjoy drinking and cooling off.

A rhino needs a lot of space, because it is so big and eats so much. Rhinos often live alone or in small groups, wandering around looking for food and water. Rhinos also love mud! On a hot day, a rhino will roll around in a muddy pool. This is called wallowing. The mud cools the rhino down and protects its skin from the hot sun and biting insects. Birds sometimes sit on a rhino's back and eat tiny insects from its skin, which helps the rhino and gives the bird a meal.

## What Do Rhinos Eat?

Rhinos are herbivores, which means they only eat plants. They do not hunt or eat meat. White rhinos graze on grass, using their wide lips to pull up mouthfuls of grass. Black rhinos browse on leaves, twigs, and branches, using their pointed lips to pluck food from bushes and trees. Asian rhinos eat grass, leaves, fruit, and water plants.

Rhinos eat a lot of food every day, because their bodies are so big. They spend many hours eating, walking slowly from one patch of plants to another. They have big, strong teeth for grinding tough plants. Rhinos also drink water at rivers and waterholes, especially in the morning and evening when it is cooler. Baby rhinos, called calves, drink their mother's milk when they are young. As they grow, they start to nibble on grass and leaves, and their mother teaches them which plants are safe to eat.

## Baby Rhinos

A baby rhino is called a calf. When a calf is born, it is already quite big, but it stays close to its mother for safety. The mother is very protective and will keep her calf near her at all times. If danger comes close, the mother will stand between the calf and the danger, and she may charge to scare it away.

Calves are playful and curious. They like to run around, explore, and practise using their legs. They stay with their mothers for a long time, often for a few years, learning how to find food, where to find water, and how to stay safe. The calf learns by watching its mother and copying what she does. As calves grow, they become bigger and stronger. Their horn starts to grow, and their skin becomes thicker. When they are old enough, young rhinos leave their mothers and start to live on their own.

## Fun Facts About Rhinos

A rhino can weigh as much as a small car, yet it can run surprisingly fast when it needs to. Even with its big, heavy body, a rhino can charge quickly for a short distance if it feels scared or if it is protecting its baby. That is why it is important to watch rhinos from far away and never try to get close to one in the wild.

Rhinos have very thick skin, but it is also sensitive. That is why they love mud baths and why birds help them by eating insects off their backs. Rhinos also communicate with each other using sounds. They can snort, grunt, and make deep calls. They also use their sense of smell to learn about other rhinos. Another fun fact is that rhinos are very good swimmers. Some Asian rhinos enjoy spending time in water and can swim across rivers, while white rhinos prefer to stay on dry land and graze.

## How We Can Help Rhinos

Sadly, rhinos need our help. Some people hunt rhinos for their horns, even though horns are made of the same stuff as our fingernails and have no special magic powers. This hunting is called poaching, and it is against the law. Because of poaching, and because their homes are being lost as land is used for farms and towns, some kinds of rhinos are now rare and in danger.

Many people are working hard to protect rhinos. Rangers guard rhinos in national parks and reserves, watching over them day and night. Scientists study rhinos and help to move them to safe places where they can live and have babies. Zoos and wildlife parks also help by looking after rhinos and teaching visitors about them. You can help too, even if you live far away. Learn about rhinos and tell your friends why they are special. Support wildlife charities that protect rhinos and their homes. When we protect rhinos, we also protect the grasslands where many other animals live.
