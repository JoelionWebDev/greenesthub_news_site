If you ever visit a farm and see a goat with very long, floppy ears that hang down past its face, you have met a Nubian goat! Nubians are some of the friendliest and most talkative goats in the world. They have beautiful long ears, Roman noses, and sweet, chatty personalities that make them a joy to be around.

Nubian goats are famous for their rich, creamy milk, which is used to make delicious cheese and soap. They are also wonderful pets, and they love to be with people. Let us meet these charming long-eared goats.

## What Do They Look Like?

Nubian goats are medium to large goats with a very special look. Their most famous feature is their ears — long, wide, and floppy, hanging down beside their faces like soft curtains. Their ears are much longer than those of most other goats, and they give the Nubian a very sweet, gentle look.

Nubians have a curved nose, sometimes called a Roman nose, which gives their face a noble, proud shape. Their coats come in many lovely colours and patterns — some are solid brown, black, or white, and others have spots, patches, or a mix of colours. No two Nubians look exactly alike!

They have short, glossy coats and long, sturdy legs. Female Nubians, called does, have large udders, where their rich milk is stored. Male Nubians, called bucks, are bigger and stronger, and they often have a beard under their chin. Both does and bucks have bright, curious eyes and a friendly expression.

## Where Do They Come From?

Nubian goats come from a warm, dry part of the world. Their ancestors lived in Africa and the Middle East, in places like Nubia, an old land along the River Nile. Long ago, goats from these warm lands were brought to England, where farmers bred them with other goats to create the Nubian goat we know today.

Because they come from warm places, Nubians love sunshine and warm weather. They are not fans of cold, wet days, and they prefer to stay in a dry, cosy barn when it rains. Today, Nubian goats live on farms all over the world, in many different countries.

On the farm, Nubians live in a barn or a goat house, where they have dry straw to sleep on and plenty of fresh food and water. During the day, they wander around the yard or the field, munching on grass and leaves, climbing on things, and chatting away to each other.

## What Do They Eat?

Nubian goats are herbivores, which means they eat only plants. They love to eat grass, hay, and the leaves of shrubs and small trees. They are very good at browsing, which means picking leaves and shoots from bushes and branches. They will stretch up on their back legs to reach tasty leaves that are high up!

Goats are famous for being curious eaters, and Nubians are no different. They will nibble at almost anything that looks interesting, but they are actually quite picky about what they eat. They like fresh, clean food, and they will turn their noses up at food that is dirty or stale.

Farmers also give Nubians grains and special goat feed to help them stay healthy and produce lots of milk. Fresh, clean water is very important, and Nubians drink a lot of it every day. Like all goats, they need minerals too, which they get from special mineral blocks that farmers put in their barn.

## The Chatty Goats

Nubian goats are famous for being very talkative! They make a lot of noise, calling out to their friends, their farmers, and anyone who will listen. Their voices are loud and clear, and they have many different sounds — from happy bleats when they see their farmer to loud calls when they want food or attention.

If you visit a farm with Nubians, you will hear them chatting away all day long. They call to each other across the yard, they greet their farmers with a loud hello, and they let everyone know when it is feeding time! Some people say that Nubians are the noisiest goats of all, but their chatter is part of their charm.

Nubians are also very friendly and social. They love to be with people, and they will come up to the fence to say hello to visitors. They enjoy being stroked and petted, and they may even lean against you for a cuddle. They are clever too, and they can learn their names and come when they are called!

## Milk and Family Life

Nubian goats are famous for their milk. Their milk is rich, creamy, and sweet, with a high fat content that makes it perfect for making cheese, yoghurt, and even soap. Farmers love Nubian milk because it is so rich and tasty, and it is used to make some of the best goat cheese in the world.

A baby goat is called a kid. When a kid is born, it is small and wobbly, but it soon learns to stand up and walk. Kids drink their mother's milk, and they stay close to her for the first part of their lives. As they grow, they start to eat grass and hay, just like the grown-up goats.

Kids are playful and full of energy. They love to jump, climb, and chase each other around the yard. They will climb on hay bales, fences, and anything else they can find! Nubian mothers look after their kids carefully, keeping them safe and showing them which foods are good to eat.

- Their milk is rich and creamy, perfect for making cheese.
- Nubians come in many different colours and patterns.
- They love warm, sunny weather and do not like cold, wet days.
