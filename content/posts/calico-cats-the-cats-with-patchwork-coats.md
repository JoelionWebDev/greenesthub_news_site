Imagine a cat wearing a coat made of patches, like a quilt sewn from pieces of orange, black and white cloth. That is a calico cat. No two calico cats look exactly alike. Each one has its own special pattern of patches, spots and swirls, so every calico is one of a kind. Some have big bold patches, some have tiny spots, and some look as if someone painted them with a brush and could not decide which colour to use.

Calico is not a breed of cat. It is a pattern of colours. A calico cat can be many different breeds, from a small house cat to a larger pedigree cat. What makes a cat a calico is its coat. A true calico has three colours: orange, black and white. The white is usually the main colour, with patches of orange and black on top. If you see a cat with those three colours, you are looking at a calico, and you are looking at a very special cat indeed.

## Why Are Almost All Calicos Girls?

Here is a fun fact that surprises many people. Almost every calico cat is a girl. That is not a guess, and it is not a story. It is true, and the reason is hidden inside the cat's body, in something very tiny called a gene.

A gene is like a little instruction that tells the body how to grow, what colour the fur should be, and many other things. The instructions for orange fur and black fur are carried on a special part of the body called the X chromosome. Girl cats have two X chromosomes, and boy cats have only one. Because a girl cat has two, she can have both orange and black patches in her coat. A boy cat has only one, so he can usually have only orange or only black, but not both.

Very rarely, a boy cat can be a calico, but this is so uncommon that it is almost like finding a treasure. Most of the calico cats you will ever meet will be girls. That makes the calico cat one of the most interesting cats in the world, and a wonderful example of how nature works in clever and surprising ways.

## A Coat Like No Other

Calico cats are famous for their coats, and people love to look at them. Some calicos have patches that are big and clear, like pieces of a jigsaw puzzle. Others have a coat that is more mixed up, with tiny spots of colour all over the white fur. This mixed-up pattern has a special name. It is called tortoiseshell and white, or sometimes just tortie and white. No matter what the pattern is, every calico is beautiful in its own way.

In many places around the world, calico cats are thought to bring good luck. In Japan, calico cats are called lucky cats, and people believe they bring good fortune to homes and businesses. You may have seen a small statue of a cat with one paw raised in a shop or a restaurant. That is a lucky cat, and it is often coloured like a calico. In other countries, sailors once believed that calico cats on a ship would bring good luck and keep the ship safe on the sea. Whether you believe in luck or not, a calico cat certainly brings joy to the people who love it.

## What Are Calico Cats Like?

Because calico is a colour pattern and not a breed, calico cats can have many different personalities. Some are playful and full of energy. Some are quiet and love to curl up on a lap. Some are bold and curious, always exploring the house and garden. But many people who live with calico cats say they have something in common. They often have strong personalities, and they know what they want. They may be loving and cuddly one minute, and independent and bossy the next. Cat lovers sometimes call this calico attitude, or tortitude, and they say it with a smile.

Calico cats love to play, just like other cats. They chase toys, pounce on shadows, climb to high places and hide in boxes.

## Looking After a Calico Cat

Looking after a calico cat is the same as looking after any other cat. It needs fresh water every day, good food, a clean litter tray, and a safe and warm place to sleep.

Calico cats also need love and attention. They are not toys. They are living animals with feelings, and they need a family that will care for them every day. If you are kind and gentle with a calico cat, it will often become your best friend. It may sit on your lap while you read, sleep at the end of your bed, or greet you at the door when you come home. Cats show their love in quiet ways, with a soft purr, a gentle head bump, or a slow blink of their eyes.

## A Cat Worth Celebrating

Calico cats are loved all over the world, not just for their beautiful coats, but for the mystery behind them. How can a cat be almost always a girl? How can no two calicos look the same? These are the kinds of questions that make science fun, and calico cats are a perfect example of how amazing nature can be.

So, the next time you see a cat with orange, black and white patches, stop and take a good look. Look at the pattern of its coat. Is it bold and bright? Is it soft and swirly? Is it mostly white, with just a few patches of colour? Whatever it looks like, you are looking at a cat that is truly one of a kind. And if it is a girl, which it almost certainly will be, you are looking at one of nature's cleverest little surprises.

A calico cat is a patchwork of colours, a bundle of personality, and a reminder that the most beautiful things in life are often the ones that are different. That is what makes the calico cat so special, and why people all over the world love to meet one.
