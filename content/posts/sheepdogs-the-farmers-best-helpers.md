On a green hillside, a farmer gives a short whistle, and far away, a black and white dog springs into action. It runs in a wide curve around a flock of sheep, and slowly, gently, the sheep begin to move. The dog does not bark or bite. It simply watches, waits, and moves, and the sheep follow. This clever dog is a sheepdog, and it is one of the farmer's best helpers.

## What is a sheepdog?

A sheepdog is a dog that has been trained to help a farmer look after sheep. The most famous sheepdogs are Border Collies, black and white dogs with bright, clever eyes. They are quick, smart, and full of energy, and they love to work. Other kinds of dogs can be sheepdogs too, but the Border Collie is the most loved, because it is so good at the job.

A sheepdog's job is to move sheep from one place to another, to bring them in from the fields, and to keep them together in a flock. Sheep are gentle animals, but they can be nervous, and they like to stay close together. A sheepdog understands this. It moves slowly and calmly, and it uses its eyes to guide the sheep. When a sheepdog stares at a sheep, the sheep feels watched and moves away from the dog. This is called the eye, and it is the sheepdog's most important tool.

## How do sheepdogs learn?

Sheepdog training starts when the dog is still a puppy. At first, the puppy just learns to be a good, friendly dog. It learns to come when called, to sit and stay, and to walk nicely on a lead. It spends time around sheep, so it gets used to their smell and sound.

When the dog is older, the farmer starts to teach it the real work. The farmer shows the dog how to run around the sheep in a wide circle, how to bring them towards the farmer, and how to stop them from running away. The farmer uses whistles and short words to tell the dog what to do. Each whistle means something different: one whistle means go left, another means go right, and another means lie down and wait. The dog learns to listen carefully, even from far away across the hillside.

Not every dog can be a sheepdog. The dog must be clever, quick, and brave, but it must also be gentle and patient. The best sheepdogs are the ones that love the work more than anything else in the world.

## A day in the life of a sheepdog

A sheepdog's day starts early, when the sun is just coming up over the hills. The farmer opens the door, and the dog runs out, wagging its tail, ready for work. The first job is to bring the sheep in from the field. The farmer stands by the gate and blows a whistle. The dog runs out in a wide arc, going around the sheep, and then slowly brings them towards the farmer.

During the day, the sheepdog may help move the sheep to fresh grass, or bring them into the barn if a storm is coming. The dog runs many miles in a day, up and down hills, through mud and rain, but it never seems to get tired. In the evening, when the work is done, the dog comes home, eats a good meal, and curls up by the fire.

## Sheepdog trials

Because sheepdogs are so clever, farmers love to show off their skills in competitions called sheepdog trials. In a trial, a dog and its farmer work together to move a small group of sheep around a course. The dog must bring the sheep through gates, around posts, and into a small pen. The farmer can only use whistles and short words, and the dog must listen carefully. Judges watch every move and give points for how well the dog and the farmer work together.

Sheepdog trials are exciting to watch. The dogs run fast, the sheep move this way and that, and the crowd holds its breath. Sometimes the dog makes a mistake, and the sheep run the wrong way, but a good dog quickly puts things right. Trials are held in many countries, and the best dogs and farmers compete for prizes.

## The bond between farmers and sheepdogs

The bond between a farmer and a sheepdog is very special. The farmer and the dog work as a team, and they must trust each other completely. The farmer trusts the dog to do the job well, even when it is far away on the hillside. The dog trusts the farmer to give it clear instructions and to look after it. Over time, they learn to understand each other so well that they hardly need to speak.

Farmers often say that their sheepdog is their best friend. The dog is with them all day, in sunshine and in rain, and it shares their work and their life. Many farmers have had sheepdogs all their lives, and each dog is remembered with love.

## How to be kind to sheepdogs

If you meet a sheepdog, remember that it is a working dog, and it needs to focus when it is with the sheep. Do not shout at it, chase it, or try to play with it while it is working. A sheepdog that is distracted could let the sheep wander off, and that could be a big problem for the farmer. If the dog is resting at home, you can ask the farmer if it is all right to say hello.

If you have a dog of your own, remember that not all dogs are sheepdogs. Sheepdogs need a lot of exercise and a job to do. A Border Collie that is bored can become unhappy, so it needs long walks, games, and things to learn.

- Farmers use different whistles to tell a sheepdog to go left, go right, or lie down.
