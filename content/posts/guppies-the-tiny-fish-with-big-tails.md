Guppies are tiny fish with big, beautiful tails, and they are some of the most popular pet fish in the whole world. Male guppies are the show-offs of the fish world, with bright colours and long, flowing tails that look like little flags waving in the water. They come in reds, blues, yellows, oranges, and greens, and no two guppies look quite the same. Female guppies are bigger and plainer, with shorter tails and soft, silvery bodies. Guppies are cheerful, active little fish that swim all over the tank in happy little groups.

## What is a guppy?

A guppy is a small freshwater fish that belongs to the livebearer family, which means that mother guppies give birth to live baby fish instead of laying eggs. Guppies are very small: the males are usually about the length of your thumb from tip to first joint, and the females are a little bit bigger. The male guppy is easy to spot because of his bright colours and his big, fancy tail, which can be shaped like a fan, a sword, or a flowing veil. Guppies are peaceful fish that like to live in groups, and they spend their days swimming, exploring, and nibbling at tiny bits of food in the water.

## Where do guppies come from?

Guppies come from the warm, shallow waters of South America and the Caribbean islands, in countries like Venezuela, Guyana, and Trinidad. In the wild, they live in streams, ponds, and slow-moving rivers, where the water is warm and full of plants. Wild guppies are not as colourful as pet guppies: the wild males have small spots of colour on their tails, while the females are plain grey-green. Over many years, people who keep fish have bred guppies to have bigger tails and brighter colours, and now there are many wonderful kinds, with names like fancy guppies, cobra guppies, and tuxedo guppies. Even though pet guppies look very different from their wild cousins, they still love the same things: warm water, plants to hide in, and friends to swim with.

## Life in the tank

Guppies are easy fish to look after, which is why they are such a good choice for a first tank. They need a tank with clean, warm water and a gentle filter to keep the water fresh. Guppies love to have plants in their tank, because plants give them places to hide and rest, and they make the tank look like a real river. Floating plants are very good for guppies, because baby guppies like to hide among their roots at the surface of the water. Guppies are happiest in a group, so it is best to keep several of them together. They get along well with other peaceful fish of a similar size, but they should not live with big or nippy fish that might chase them or bite their long, flowing tails. A calm tank with plenty of space and plants is a happy home for a guppy.

## What do guppies eat?

Guppies eat tiny bits of food, and they are always ready for a snack. In the wild, they nibble at tiny water creatures, insect larvae, and bits of algae, which is the soft green stuff that grows on rocks and plants in the water. In a home tank, guppies eat small fish flakes or tiny pellets made just for them, and they enjoy treats like frozen brine shrimp and tiny worms. Guppies should be fed a little bit at a time, once or twice a day, and only as much as they can finish in a couple of minutes. If too much food is dropped into the tank, the leftover food makes the water dirty. Guppies have small tummies, so a tiny pinch of food is plenty.

## Baby guppies

Guppies are famous for having lots of babies, and baby guppies are very cute. Because guppies are livebearers, the mother guppy gives birth to tiny baby fish that can swim right away, instead of laying eggs. A mother guppy can have many babies at once, sometimes a whole crowd of them. When baby guppies are born, they need places to hide, because even grown-up guppies might mistake them for food. Plants with fine leaves and floating plants are perfect hiding places for the little ones. Baby guppies eat very small food, like crushed flakes or special baby fish food, and they grow quickly. In just a few weeks, the little males start to grow their bright colours and fancy tails, and soon they look like tiny versions of their fathers.

## Why guppies are great first fish

Guppies are one of the best first fish for a child or a new fish keeper, and there are good reasons why. They are hardy, which means they can cope well when the water is not quite perfect, though they still need clean water and good care. Guppies are also very lively and interesting to watch. They swim in little groups, chase each other in friendly games, and show off their bright tails. Because they have babies often, children can watch the whole life of a guppy, from a tiny newborn to a bright, fancy-tailed adult. Guppies teach new fish keepers how to feed fish, clean a tank, and look after living things, all while being beautiful and fun.

## Fun facts about guppies

- Guppies are named after a man called Robert John Lechmere Guppy, who first sent some of them to scientists in England.
- Male guppies have much bigger and brighter tails than female guppies.
- Guppies are livebearers, which means mother guppies give birth to live babies instead of laying eggs.
- Wild guppies come from streams and ponds in South America and the Caribbean.
- Guppies eat tiny insect larvae in the wild, which helps keep mosquito numbers down.
- A guppy's tail can be shaped like a fan, a sword, or a flowing veil.

Guppies may be tiny, but they are full of colour, life, and charm. With their bright tails, cheerful swimming, and friendly ways, they bring a splash of joy to any tank. They are easy to care for, fun to watch, and wonderful teachers for anyone learning how to keep fish. If you are thinking of starting your very first aquarium, a little group of guppies is a lovely place to begin. Just give them warm, clean water, a pinch of food, and some plants to play in, and they will fill your tank with colour and happiness every single day.
