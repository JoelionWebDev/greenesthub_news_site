## What is a duck?

A duck is a bird that loves water. Ducks have round bodies, short necks, flat bills and webbed feet. Their feet work a little like paddles, and that helps them swim. Ducks are found in many parts of the world, and people have kept them as farm animals for a very long time.

When you see a duck, the first thing you may notice is its bill. A duck's bill is wide and flat, not sharp like the beak of an eagle. The bill helps the duck scoop up food from the water and from soft mud. Ducks also have strong little legs that are set a bit back on their bodies. That is why they waddle when they walk on land. On water, though, they glide along so smoothly that they look calm and graceful.

Ducks are smaller than geese and swans, but they belong to the same big bird family.

## Where do ducks live?

Ducks live near water. You can find them on ponds, lakes, rivers and marshes. They also live on farms, where people give them a pond or a pool to splash in. Wild ducks can be found in many countries, from cold places to warm places, as long as there is water and food nearby.

Some ducks stay in one place all year round. Others are travellers. When the weather turns cold, some ducks fly far away to a warmer place where they can find open water and food. Then, when spring comes, they fly back again. These long journeys are called migration, and ducks are very good at them.

A duck's home is usually close to the edge of the water. Wild ducks build their nests on the ground, hidden among tall grass or reeds. The mother duck lines the nest with soft feathers to keep the eggs warm and safe. Farm ducks live in a duck house or a sheltered part of the farmyard, and they still love to spend most of the day outside near water.

## What do ducks eat?

Ducks eat many different things. In the wild, they eat small plants that grow in the water, seeds, grains, insects, worms and tiny creatures that live in ponds. They dabble, which means they dip their bills into the water and scoop up food while their tails point up in the air. It looks funny, but it works very well.

On a farm, ducks are given grains such as corn and wheat, as well as green vegetables and clean water to drink. They also enjoy searching the grass for insects and slugs. Ducks need fresh water every day, not only for drinking but also for cleaning their bills and feathers.

If you ever visit ducks at a park, remember that bread is not good food for them. Bread fills them up but does not give them the things their bodies need, and leftover bread can make the water dirty. If you want to feed ducks, it is much better to ask an adult first and to offer food that is safe for them, like small pieces of leafy greens or special duck food from a pet shop.

## Duck families

A mother duck is called a hen, and a father duck is called a drake. Baby ducks are called ducklings, and they are some of the cutest baby animals you will ever see. Ducklings are covered in soft yellow or brown fluff, and they can swim when they are very young.

The mother duck usually lays her eggs in a nest and sits on them to keep them warm until they hatch. When the ducklings hatch, they are ready to follow their mother almost straight away. They can walk, swim and find food with her help. Ducklings stay close to their mother because she protects them and shows them where the good food is.

One of the most famous sights in the animal world is a line of ducklings following their mother in a neat row, waddling along a path or swimming across a pond. The mother duck quacks to call them, and the ducklings peep back in their tiny voices. It is a sweet sound, and it helps the family stay together.

As ducklings grow, their soft fluff is replaced by real feathers. Young ducks learn to fly when their wings are strong enough. Some farm ducks do not fly much because people have kept them for so long, but wild ducks are strong flyers.

## Ducks and people

People and ducks have lived together for a very long time. Long ago, people started keeping ducks on farms because ducks lay eggs that people can eat, and because their meat and soft feathers are useful too. Duck feathers are very soft and warm, and they have been used for pillows and warm jackets.

Today, many people keep ducks as farm animals, and some keep them as pets. Pet ducks need space, clean water, good food and a safe place to sleep at night. They are friendly birds, and they can learn to know the people who feed them. Some ducks will even follow their owner around the garden.

Ducks also help farmers in a clever way. Because ducks eat insects and slugs, they can help keep gardens and rice fields free of pests. In some places, farmers let ducks walk through their fields to eat insects, which means the farmers need fewer chemicals to protect their crops.

Ducks appear in many stories, songs and cartoons for children. From storybook ducklings to famous cartoon ducks, these cheerful birds have made children smile for generations.

## Fun facts about ducks

- A duck's quack does echo, even though some people say it does not.
- Ducklings can swim when they are only a day or two old.
- Ducks have waterproof feathers thanks to the oil they spread while preening.
- Some ducks can fly very high and travel a long way when they migrate.
- A duck's webbed feet work like paddles and help it swim fast.
- Ducks sleep with one eye open sometimes, so they can watch for danger.
- The mallard is one of the most common wild ducks in the world.
- Ducks have very good eyesight and can see colours.
- A group of ducks on the water is called a raft, and a group flying together is called a flock.
- Mother ducks talk to their ducklings before they hatch, and the ducklings peep back from inside the egg.
