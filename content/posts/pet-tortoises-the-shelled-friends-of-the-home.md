A tortoise is an animal that carries its home wherever it goes. Its home is its shell, a hard, round cover on its back that keeps it safe. Tortoises move slowly, munch leaves, and enjoy warm sunshine. They are not in a hurry, and that is part of what makes them so lovely. A pet tortoise is a quiet friend. It will amble around the garden, nibble its food, and look up at you with bright, curious eyes.

## What is a tortoise?

A tortoise is a reptile, and it is related to turtles. The big difference is where they live. Turtles mostly live in water, while tortoises live on land. A tortoise has a hard shell made of bone, covered with plates called scutes. The top of the shell is called the carapace, and the flat part underneath is called the plastron. The shell is part of the tortoise's body, so it cannot climb out of it. A tortoise has four short, strong legs, a small head, and a short tail. Its legs look a bit like tiny elephant legs, with short claws for digging and walking. Tortoises come in many sizes.

## Where do tortoises live?

Wild tortoises live in warm, dry places all around the world. You can find them in deserts, grasslands, and rocky hills in Africa, Asia, Europe, and the Americas. A tortoise likes a home with warm sunshine, soft soil to dig in, and plants to eat. Pet tortoises live in a safe outdoor pen or a large indoor home, depending on the weather and the kind of tortoise. Their home needs sunshine or a warm light, a shady place to rest, fresh water, and room to walk.

## What do tortoises eat?

Tortoises are mostly plant eaters. They love to munch on leaves, grasses, flowers, and soft fruits. In the wild, a tortoise spends a lot of its day slowly walking around and nibbling the plants it finds. It has a hard beak that works a bit like scissors. Pet tortoises eat leafy greens, grasses, and other safe plants that a grown-up chooses for them. Some tortoises also like a little fruit as a treat. Fresh water is very important too. A tortoise drinks water and also likes to sit in a shallow dish of water to stay cool and clean.

## A day in a tortoise's life

A tortoise's day starts when the sun warms the air. In the morning, a tortoise comes out of its hiding place and basks in the sunshine. Basking means sitting in the sun to warm up, and it helps the tortoise feel strong and ready for the day. After warming up, the tortoise goes for a slow walk to find food. It nibbles leaves, sniffs flowers, and explores its home. In the heat of the day, a tortoise may rest in the shade or dig a little hole to cool down. In the evening, it finds a safe place to sleep.

## Baby tortoises

Baby tortoises hatch from eggs. The mother tortoise digs a hole in soft, warm soil and lays her eggs inside it. Then she covers the eggs with soil and leaves them to grow. The sun warms the soil, and the baby tortoises grow inside their eggs. When they are ready, the babies break out of their shells with a tiny egg tooth. Baby tortoises are very small, but they already have a hard little shell. A baby tortoise must be careful, because many animals would like to eat it. It hides among plants and stays close to safe places. Baby tortoises eat soft leaves and grasses, and they drink fresh water. They grow slowly, year by year, and their shells grow with them.

## Looking after a pet tortoise

Tortoises can be wonderful pets, but they need a grown-up to help look after them. A pet tortoise needs a safe home with sunshine or a warm light, a shady spot, and fresh water every day. It needs the right plants to eat, and its home should be cleaned often. A tortoise should never be picked up by its shell and carried around like a toy. If you need to move a tortoise, a grown-up should help, holding it gently with both hands and keeping it low to the ground. Always wash your hands after touching a tortoise or its home. A tortoise is a living animal, not a toy, and it needs quiet time to rest and eat. Before getting a pet tortoise, a family should learn about the kind of tortoise they want, because different kinds need different food and homes. With kind, patient care, a pet tortoise can be a friend for many, many years.

## Fun facts about tortoises

- A tortoise's shell is part of its body, and it cannot climb out of it.
- Tortoises are land animals, while their cousins, the turtles, mostly live in water.
- A tortoise has a hard beak instead of teeth, and it uses it to bite off leaves.
- Tortoises love to bask in the sunshine to warm up in the morning.
- Baby tortoises hatch from eggs buried in warm soil.
- Some tortoises can live for many, many years when they are well looked after.

Pet tortoises teach us that slow and steady is a wonderful way to live. They amble through their days, munching leaves, warming in the sun, and carrying their cosy homes on their backs. They ask for very little: warm sunshine, fresh food, clean water, and gentle care. In return, they give us quiet company and a smile every time we see them plodding happily across the garden.
