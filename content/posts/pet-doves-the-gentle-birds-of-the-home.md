Close your eyes and imagine a soft, gentle sound, like a quiet song floating through the air. That is the coo of a dove. Doves are some of the gentlest birds in the world, and many people keep them as pets. With their soft feathers, calm ways and sweet cooing, doves bring peace and happiness into a home.

## What Does a Pet Dove Look Like?

Pet doves are medium-sized birds with soft, smooth feathers. The most common pet doves are ringneck doves, which have a gentle grey-brown body, a long tail and a thin black ring around the back of the neck, like a little necklace. That ring is how they got their name.

Some pet doves are pure white. White doves are very beautiful, and they have been loved by people for hundreds of years. In many stories and pictures, white doves are symbols of peace and love. You may have seen pictures of white doves being released at weddings and celebrations.

Doves have small heads, dark eyes with a gentle look, and a soft beak. Their feet are a pinkish colour, and they have long tails that help them balance. When a dove flies, its wings move in a smooth, gentle way, and it looks like it is gliding through the air. Their feathers feel very soft to touch, like the softest cotton wool.

## The Gentle Song of the Dove

Doves are famous for their cooing. A dove's coo is a soft, low sound that goes "coo-coo" in a slow and gentle rhythm. It is a calm and relaxing sound, and many people say that listening to a dove coo makes them feel peaceful and happy.

Male doves coo more often than female doves, especially when they are trying to impress a female dove or to tell other doves that a place belongs to them. They puff up their chests, bow their heads and coo again and again. It is their way of singing a little love song.

Doves are not noisy birds. They do not screech or shout like some parrots do. Their cooing is quiet and soft, which makes them wonderful pets for people who live in flats or in quiet homes. A dove's gentle song is one of the nicest sounds a pet bird can make.

## Where Do Pet Doves Come From?

Doves have lived alongside people for a very long time. Wild doves live in many parts of the world, in Europe, Africa and Asia. People began to keep doves as pets hundreds of years ago, because they are calm, beautiful and easy to care for.

The ringneck dove, the most common pet dove, comes from Africa. It was first kept as a pet because of its gentle nature and its lovely cooing. Over time, people bred doves in different colours, including white, so that today you can find pet doves in many shades, from soft grey to snowy white.

Doves are related to pigeons. In fact, doves and pigeons belong to the same family of birds. Doves are usually smaller and slimmer than pigeons, and they have a gentler look. But they share many of the same habits, like eating seeds and building simple nests.


## Life at Home with a Pet Dove

Pet doves are calm and gentle birds that enjoy a quiet home. They need a large cage or aviary, which is a big enclosure where birds can fly and move around. Doves love to stretch their wings, so their home should be wide enough for them to fly a short distance inside it.

Inside the cage, a dove needs perches to sit on, a dish of fresh water and a dish of food. Doves also love to have a bath. A shallow dish of clean water lets them splash and clean their feathers, which keeps them healthy and happy. Watching a dove take a bath is very funny, because it fluffs up its feathers and splashes water everywhere!

Doves are social birds, which means they like company. A dove that lives alone needs lots of gentle attention from its owner, while two doves together will keep each other company. Many owners keep a pair of doves, so the birds are never lonely. Doves are peaceful birds and they get along well with each other.

## Food and Care for a Dove

Doves eat seeds, and their favourite foods are small seeds like millet and sunflower seeds. They also enjoy a little bit of grain, like wheat and corn. Owners give their doves a special seed mix made just for them, along with fresh water every day.

Doves also like to eat a few greens, like lettuce and spinach, and they need a little bit of grit. Grit is made of tiny stones that help the dove grind up seeds inside its body so it can digest them properly. It sounds strange, but it is very important for a dove's health.


## Fun Facts About Pet Doves

- The white dove is a symbol of peace all over the world, and it has been for hundreds of years.
- Ringneck doves are named after the thin black ring around the back of their necks.
- Doves are related to pigeons, and they belong to the same family of birds.
- A dove's coo is a soft, gentle sound that many people find calm and relaxing.
- Doves often stay with one partner for a long time, and they build their nests and look after their babies together.
- Doves love to take baths, and they splash and fluff their feathers in a shallow dish of water.
- White doves are sometimes released at weddings and celebrations as a symbol of love and happiness.

## A Gentle Friend for a Calm Home

The pet dove is a bird that brings peace into a home. It does not need a lot of space, it does not make a lot of noise, and it does not ask for much. All it needs is a clean home, fresh food and water, and a kind owner who talks to it softly and treats it gently.

In return, the dove gives something very special: its soft cooing, its gentle company and its calm, peaceful nature. In a busy and noisy world, a dove is a little reminder to slow down, be gentle and enjoy quiet moments.

If you ever hear a dove cooing, stop and listen for a moment. That soft, gentle song is one of nature's nicest sounds, and it comes from one of the kindest birds you could ever meet.
