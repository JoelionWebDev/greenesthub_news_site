## What Is a Tiger?

A tiger is a very big wild cat. In fact, tigers are the largest cats in the whole world! They are much bigger than the cats that live in our homes, and much bigger than lions and leopards too. A tiger has a beautiful orange coat with black stripes, a white belly, and a long, strong tail. Its face is round, with white patches around its eyes and a pink nose.

Every tiger has its own special pattern of stripes. No two tigers have exactly the same stripes, just like no two people have exactly the same fingerprints. If you look closely at a tiger, you will see that its stripes are not only on its fur — the skin underneath has the same striped pattern too!

Tigers have strong legs, sharp claws, and big teeth. Their paws are soft and quiet, so they can walk through the forest without making a sound. Their eyes are very good at seeing in the dark, which helps them hunt at night. Tigers are powerful animals, but they are also graceful. When a tiger moves through the trees, it looks like a silent orange shadow.

## Where Do Tigers Live?

Tigers live in Asia, in countries such as India, China, Russia, and Indonesia. Long ago, tigers lived in many more places, but today they live in smaller areas than they used to. They need big wild spaces to live in, with lots of trees, water, and animals to hunt.

Tigers like many different kinds of homes. Some live in hot rainforests where it rains a lot and the trees are thick and green. Some live in cold snowy forests in the far north, where their fur grows thicker to keep them warm. Some live near rivers and swamps, where they can swim and cool off. Wherever they live, tigers need plenty of space, because each tiger likes to have a large area all to itself.

A tiger's home area is called its territory. A tiger marks its territory by scratching trees and leaving its scent, so other tigers know that this place is taken. Male tigers usually have bigger territories than female tigers. Tigers mostly live alone, and they do not like sharing their home with other tigers.

## What Do Tigers Eat?

Tigers are meat eaters, which means they eat other animals. They are hunters, and they are very good at it. Tigers mostly hunt big animals such as deer and wild pigs. They will also hunt smaller animals if they get the chance.

A tiger hunts by sneaking up quietly on its prey. Its striped coat helps it hide in the tall grass and in the shadows of the trees. The tiger creeps closer and closer, very slowly, until it is near enough to pounce. Then it leaps with great power and speed. A tiger can jump very far in one great leap!

Tigers usually hunt at night or in the early morning, when it is cool and quiet. Tigers can go without food for many days, but they always need water. Unlike most cats, tigers love water! They are strong swimmers and enjoy splashing in rivers and ponds, especially on hot days. Swimming helps them cool down and it is also a good way to catch fish or cross a river.

## A Tiger's Family Life

Most of the time, tigers live alone. But a mother tiger stays with her cubs for a long time. Baby tigers are called cubs. When cubs are born, they are tiny and blind, and they stay close to their mother in a safe, hidden place, such as a cave or a thick bush.

A mother tiger takes very good care of her cubs. She feeds them, keeps them warm, and protects them from danger. As the cubs grow bigger, she teaches them how to hunt. The cubs learn by playing — they pounce on each other, chase leaves, and practise sneaking up quietly, just like their mother does when she hunts. Playing is very important for young tigers, because it helps them learn the skills they will need as grown-up hunters.

Cubs stay with their mother for a long time, often more than a year, until they are big and strong enough to live on their own. Then they go off to find their own territory. Young tigers must be brave and clever to survive on their own in the wild forest.

## Tigers and People

For a very long time, people have loved tigers. Tigers appear in stories, books, and films all around the world. In some countries, the tiger is a symbol of strength and courage. People admire the tiger for its beauty and power.

But tigers need our help. There are not as many wild tigers today as there were long ago. Their forests are getting smaller because people cut down trees to build farms and towns. Sometimes tigers are also in danger because people hunt them, which is wrong and against the law in many places.

The good news is that many people are working hard to protect tigers. They guard the forests where tigers live, and they help tigers stay safe. In some places, the number of tigers is slowly growing again, which makes animal lovers very happy. When we protect forests, we protect tigers too — and all the other animals that share their home.

If you ever see a tiger in a zoo or a wildlife park, watch it carefully. Look at its stripes, its big paws, and the way it moves. Remember that you are looking at one of the most amazing animals on Earth.

## Fun Facts About Tigers

- No two tigers have the same stripes — each tiger's pattern is one of a kind, just like your fingerprints.
- Tigers are the largest cats in the world.
- Unlike most cats, tigers love water and are strong swimmers.
- A tiger's stripes are not only on its fur — they are on its skin too.
- Tigers can see very well in the dark, which helps them hunt at night.
- A tiger's roar is so loud that it can be heard from far away across the forest.
- Tigers mostly live alone, except for mothers with their cubs.
- Baby tigers are called cubs, and they learn to hunt by playing.
- Tigers use their striped coats to hide in tall grass and shadows.
- A tiger can leap a very long way in one powerful jump.
