Imagine a dog so tall that when it stands on its back legs, it can be taller than a grown-up. That is the Great Dane, one of the tallest dogs in the whole world. With its long legs, big head and kind eyes, the Great Dane looks like a giant. But do not let its size scare you. Inside that huge body is a soft, gentle heart. That is why people call Great Danes gentle giants.

## How Big Is a Great Dane?

A Great Dane is a very tall dog. When it stands on all four legs, its back can reach up to a grown-up's waist or even higher. Its head is big, its chest is deep and its legs are long and strong.

Great Danes come in lots of colours. Some are a golden brown colour called fawn, some are black, some are a grey-blue colour called blue, and some have a spotty black and white coat called harlequin. Others have stripes on their coats, which is called brindle. No matter what colour they are, they all have short, smooth coats that are easy to brush.

Even though they are so big, Great Dane puppies start out small, just like other puppies. But they grow very fast. A Great Dane puppy grows more quickly than most other puppies, and in its first year it can grow into a very large dog indeed. Watching a Great Dane puppy grow is like watching a small plant turn into a huge tree in no time at all.

## Where Do Great Danes Come From?

Great Danes come from Germany. Long ago, in Germany, people bred large, strong dogs to help them hunt wild boar, which is a big and powerful wild pig. The dogs had to be brave, strong and fast. Over time, people began to keep these big dogs as guards and companions instead, and the dogs became calmer and friendlier.

The breed's name is a little confusing, because "Dane" sounds like it comes from Denmark. But the dogs were really developed in Germany, where they were once called German mastiffs. The name Great Dane stuck, and today it is the name everyone uses.

Famous Great Danes have appeared in books, films and cartoons. Perhaps the most famous of all is Scooby-Doo, the funny cartoon dog who solves mysteries with his friends. Scooby-Doo is a Great Dane, and his silly, scaredy-cat personality shows the gentle and funny side of the breed.

## A Gentle Heart in a Giant Body

The best thing about a Great Dane is its personality. These dogs are calm, friendly and patient. They love being with their families and they love children. A Great Dane will happily lie on the floor while children play nearby, and it will enjoy gentle pats and cuddles.

Great Danes are loyal dogs. They like to be where their family is, and they often follow their owners around the house. Many Great Danes think they are small lap dogs and will try to climb onto the sofa for a cuddle.

They are also good watchdogs. A Great Dane's deep bark is loud and strong, and it can scare away strangers. But once they know someone is a friend, they are warm and welcoming. They are not angry dogs. They are big softies at heart.

## Life at Home with a Great Dane

Owning a Great Dane is a big job, because everything about them is big. They need a large bed, a large food bowl and lots of space to stretch out. They are not the best dogs for a very small home, because their long tail can sweep cups and books off low tables with one happy wag!

Great Danes need daily walks and playtime to stay healthy. They enjoy a good walk in the park, a game of fetch and gentle play with their family. But they are not dogs that need to run all day. A Great Dane is quite happy with a good walk and then a long rest on the sofa or on its bed.

Because they are so big, Great Danes need to learn good manners when they are still puppies. Training helps them learn not to jump up on people and to walk nicely on a lead. A Great Dane that jumps up can knock a small child over by accident, just because it is so big and heavy. Kind, patient training from a young age helps them grow into polite giants.

## Food, Care and Exercise

Great Danes eat a lot of food. A big dog needs big meals to keep its body strong and healthy. Puppies need special food that helps them grow at a steady pace, because growing too fast can hurt their bones and joints. Owners work with a vet to make sure their Great Dane gets the right food in the right amount.

Their short coats are easy to look after. A quick brush once or twice a week keeps the coat shiny and helps remove loose hairs. Great Danes love to play, but they should not do too much jumping or rough play when they are young, because their bones are still growing. Gentle walks and calm play are best for puppies.


## Fun Facts About Great Danes

- Great Danes are among the tallest dog breeds in the world, and some stand taller than a kitchen table.
- The cartoon dog Scooby-Doo is a Great Dane, and his name comes from a song.
- Great Danes were once used in Germany to hunt wild boar, a big and powerful wild pig.
- A Great Dane puppy grows very fast, and it can grow into a huge dog in its first year of life.
- Many Great Danes think they are lap dogs and try to climb onto their owners' laps for cuddles.

## The Biggest Friend You Could Ask For

The Great Dane is proof that the biggest dogs can have the biggest hearts. It may look like a giant, but it is really a gentle, loving friend who wants nothing more than to be close to its family. It will lean against you for a cuddle, rest its huge head on your knee and follow you around the house just to be near you.

If you ever meet a Great Dane, do not be frightened by its size. Look into its kind eyes and you will see a gentle soul. Offer it a pat, and you may make a new friend for life.
