Imagine a bird so big that it is taller than most grown-ups. A bird with long, strong legs, a long neck, and huge eyes. A bird that cannot fly at all, but can run faster than a horse. That bird is the ostrich, the biggest bird in the whole world.

Ostriches live in Africa, on wide open grasslands called savannas. They are famous for their size, their speed, and their very big eggs. Let us find out what makes these giant birds so special.

## How Big Is an Ostrich?

An ostrich is the largest bird alive today. A grown-up male ostrich can stand about two and a half metres tall. That is taller than a door in your house. Some very big males can be even taller.

Male ostriches are mostly black, with white feathers on their wings and tail. Female ostriches are grey-brown, which helps them hide in the dry grass. Both males and females have long, bare necks and long, powerful legs. Their legs are so strong that one kick can scare away a lion.

An ostrich can weigh as much as a grown man, or even more. Yet most of that weight is in its body and legs, not in its wings. Its wings are too small to lift such a heavy body into the air. That is why ostriches do not fly. Instead, they use their wings to help them balance when they run, and to show off when they want to impress other ostriches.

Ostriches have the biggest eyes of any land animal. Each eye is about the size of a tennis ball. Big eyes help them spot danger from far away, even on the wide open plains.

## The Fastest Runner on Two Legs

Ostriches cannot fly, but they are amazing runners. They are the fastest birds on land. An ostrich can run at speeds of about seventy kilometres per hour. That is faster than a car driving through a town. Even over a long distance, an ostrich can keep running at a steady speed for a long time without getting tired.

Each step an ostrich takes can be very long, sometimes more than three metres. Its legs work like springs, pushing it forward with every step. Ostriches have only two toes on each foot, which is unusual for a bird. Most birds have three or four toes. Having just two toes, with one big toe that has a strong claw, helps the ostrich run fast and turn quickly.

When an ostrich senses danger, it usually runs away. Its long legs and sharp eyesight help it escape from lions, cheetahs, and hyenas. Young ostriches are not as fast, so they stay close to their parents for safety.

## What Do Ostriches Eat?

Ostriches are not fussy eaters. They eat seeds, grass, leaves, flowers, and fruits. Sometimes they also eat insects and small animals, like lizards. This kind of diet makes them omnivores, which means they eat both plants and animals.

Ostriches do not have teeth, so they swallow small stones and pebbles. The stones sit in their stomach and help to grind up the food, just like teeth would.

An ostrich can go for a long time without drinking water. It gets most of the water it needs from the plants it eats. This is very useful on the hot, dry African plains, where water can be hard to find.

## The Biggest Eggs in the World

Ostriches lay the biggest eggs of any bird alive today. One ostrich egg is about the size of a small melon. It is so big and strong that a grown person could stand on it without breaking it, although you should never try that with a real egg.

A female ostrich lays her eggs in a shallow nest, which is really just a hole scraped in the ground. Several females may lay their eggs in the same nest, and one nest can hold many eggs. The male ostrich often helps to look after the nest, sitting on the eggs at night, while the female sits on them during the day. The female's brown feathers blend in with the dry ground during the day, and the male's black feathers blend in with the dark at night. This is clever camouflage.

Ostrich eggs take about six weeks to hatch. When the chicks come out, they are about the size of a chicken, with stripy brown feathers. They can walk and run soon after hatching, and they follow their parents around, pecking at food on the ground.

## Ostrich Families

Ostriches live in groups called flocks. A flock usually has one main male, a few females, and their chicks. Sometimes many ostriches gather together in a bigger group, especially when there is plenty of food and water.

Male ostriches do a special dance to impress the females. They spread their wings, wave them up and down, and sway their long necks from side to side. It looks very funny, but the females watch carefully and choose the male with the best dance.

Ostrich chicks grow very fast. In their first year, they can grow almost as tall as their parents.

## Fun Facts About Ostriches

- An ostrich's brain is smaller than one of its eyes.
- Ostriches do not bury their heads in the sand. That is just a story. When they feel danger, they either run away or lie flat on the ground to hide.
- A group of ostriches is called a flock, and a baby ostrich is called a chick.
- Ostrich feathers are soft and fluffy, and male ostriches use them in their dances.
- An ostrich can live for many years, often more than forty years in the wild.
- The ostrich is the only bird in the world with just two toes on each foot.

Ostriches are truly amazing birds. They are the biggest, the fastest runners, and the layers of the biggest eggs.
