# Fennec Foxes: The Big-Eared Foxes of the Desert

Imagine a tiny fox with ears so big they look like they belong to a much larger animal. The ears are tall, pointed, and almost as big as the fox's whole head. The fox itself is small, sandy-coloured, and very cute, with a fluffy tail and bright, dark eyes. This is a fennec fox, the smallest fox in the world, and it lives in one of the hottest, driest places on Earth: the Sahara Desert in North Africa.



## The Biggest Ears of Any Fox

The first thing you notice about a fennec fox is its ears. They are huge, and they stick up like two tall triangles on top of its head. The ears help the fennec fox in two important ways. First, they help it hear. Fennec foxes hunt at night, and their giant ears can pick up the tiniest sounds, like a small lizard moving under the sand or an insect crawling nearby. With ears like that, a fennec fox can hear its dinner before it ever sees it.

Second, the big ears help the fennec fox stay cool. The desert is very hot during the day, and the fox's ears are full of tiny blood vessels close to the surface of the skin. As the blood flows through the ears, the heat escapes into the air, a bit like a radiator cooling down a car. This helps the fox keep its body cool in the hot desert sun.

Fennec foxes are the smallest of all foxes. A grown-up fennec fox is not much bigger than a small house cat, and it weighs very little. Its fur is sandy and cream-coloured, which helps it blend in with the desert sand. Its feet are covered with thick, soft fur, which protects them from the hot sand and helps the fox walk on the soft dunes without sinking in.

## Life in the Sahara Desert

The Sahara Desert is the largest hot desert in the world, and it covers much of North Africa. It is a tough place to live. During the day, the sun is very strong and the sand is hot. At night, it can get quite cold. There is very little water, and food can be hard to find. But fennec foxes are perfectly suited to this life.

Fennec foxes are nocturnal, which means they are awake at night and asleep during the day. At night, the desert is cooler, and that is when the foxes come out to hunt. During the day, they sleep in burrows they dig in the sand. These burrows are like little underground homes, and they stay cool even when the sun is blazing above. Fennec fox families sometimes dig burrows with many rooms and entrances, like a small underground house.

Fennec foxes live in small family groups. A mother and father fox often stay together, and they raise their pups as a family. The pups are born in the burrow, blind and helpless, and their parents bring them food until they are old enough to hunt for themselves.

## What Do Fennec Foxes Eat?

Fennec foxes are not picky eaters. They eat almost anything they can find in the desert. They hunt small rodents, like mice and gerbils, and they catch lizards and small birds. They also eat insects, like beetles and grasshoppers, and they eat eggs when they find them. Sometimes they eat fruit and leaves too, which give them extra water.

Fennec foxes get most of the water they need from their food, so they do not need to drink very often. This is very useful in the desert, where water is hard to find. Their bodies are made to save water, and their kidneys are very good at keeping water inside.

When a fennec fox hunts, it listens carefully with its big ears. It can hear a small animal moving under the sand, and then it pounces, digging quickly with its paws to catch its prey. It is a clever hunter, and its ears are its best tool.


## Family Life in the Burrow

Fennec fox families are close. The father fox helps the mother fox take care of the pups, bringing food and guarding the burrow. The pups stay in the burrow for their first weeks, and their mother keeps them warm and safe. When the pups are old enough, they start to explore outside the burrow, playing in the sand and learning to hunt.

The pups grow quickly, and by the time they are a few months old, they are already good at digging and listening for prey. They stay with their parents for a while, and then they go off to find their own burrows and start their own families.

## Protecting the Fennec Foxes

Fennec foxes are not in as much danger as some other desert animals, but they still face problems. Their desert homes are being disturbed by people building roads and towns, and by vehicles driving across the sand. Some fennec foxes are also caught and sold as pets, which is not good for them, because they are wild animals that need the desert to live happily.

People are working to protect fennec foxes and their desert homes. National parks and reserves in North Africa help keep the desert safe. Scientists study fennec foxes to learn more about how they live, and people are taught that wild animals belong in the wild, not in a cage.

## Fun Facts About Fennec Foxes

- Fennec foxes have the biggest ears of any fox, relative to their body size.
- Their ears help them hear prey moving under the sand.
- The big ears also help them stay cool in the hot desert.
- Fennec foxes are the smallest foxes in the world.
- Their feet are covered in thick fur to protect them from hot sand.
- Fennec foxes are nocturnal, which means they are active at night.
- They get most of their water from the food they eat.
- Fennec foxes dig burrows with more than one entrance to escape danger.

Fennec foxes are small, cute, and very clever. With their giant ears, their furry feet, and their love of the night, they are perfectly made for desert life. Next time you picture the Sahara Desert, imagine a tiny fox with huge ears, listening carefully in the dark, ready to pounce on its dinner.
