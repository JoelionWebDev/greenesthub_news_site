Imagine a tiny fish that looks like it is wearing a glowing stripe of blue and red. When the light catches it, the fish seems to shine and sparkle in the water. That little fish is a neon tetra. Neon tetras are small, bright, peaceful fish that live in home aquariums all over the world. They are not big, and they are not loud, but they are one of the most beautiful fish you can keep at home.

Neon tetras come from rivers and streams in South America, in a place called the Amazon. The Amazon is a huge rainforest with many rivers that flow through green trees and soft, dark water. In those rivers, neon tetras swim in groups among plants and roots. Their bright colours help them find each other in the dark water, and help them stay together as a group. Today, people keep neon tetras in tanks at home, where they can watch them swim and shine every day.

## A Little Fish with Bright Colours

A neon tetra is a very small fish. It is only about as long as your thumb is wide, so it could fit easily on a small coin. But what it lacks in size, it makes up for in colour. A neon tetra has a bright blue stripe that runs along its body, from its eye to its tail. Below the blue stripe is a bright red stripe that covers the back part of its body. The rest of the fish is silvery and clear.

The blue stripe is the most amazing part. It shines and glows when light hits it, and it can look blue, green, or even silvery, depending on how the light falls. That is why the fish is called a neon tetra. When a group of neon tetras swims together, their blue stripes flash and sparkle like little jewels in the water.

## Swimming Together in a School

Neon tetras love to swim together. A group of fish that swims together is called a school. Neon tetras are happiest when they live in a school with other neon tetras. They swim close together, turn at the same time, and move through the water like a team. Watching a school of neon tetras is like watching a slow, graceful dance.

Staying together helps neon tetras feel safe. In the wild, a big group of fish is harder for a hungry animal to catch than one fish on its own. The bright colours also help the fish stay together, because they can see each other's shining stripes even in dark water.

A single neon tetra on its own can feel scared and lonely. That is why people who keep neon tetras always keep them in a group. A tank with a school of neon tetras is a happy tank. The fish swim, play, and explore together, and they look after each other in their own quiet way.

## A Peaceful Little Fish

Neon tetras are peaceful fish. They do not fight with other fish, and they do not chase or bite. They are gentle and calm, and they get along well with other small, peaceful fish. That makes them a good choice for a community tank, which is a tank where different kinds of friendly fish live together.

Neon tetras spend their days swimming slowly through the water, exploring plants, and looking for food. They like to swim in the middle of the tank, where they can see everything around them. They are curious little fish, and they like to explore every part of their tank.

## What Do Neon Tetras Eat?

Neon tetras eat tiny bits of food. In the wild, they eat small insects, tiny water creatures, and small bits of plants that float in the river. In a home tank, they eat special fish food made just for small fish, which comes as tiny flakes or small pellets that float on the water.

It is important not to give neon tetras too much food. A little food twice a day is enough, because any food that is left uneaten will sink to the bottom and make the water dirty. A grown-up should help you learn how much food is just right.

## A Happy Home in the Tank

A neon tetra needs a clean, cosy tank to be happy. The tank should have clean, warm water, because neon tetras come from warm rivers. The water should be changed often, and a filter in the tank helps keep the water clean. A grown-up should help you learn how to look after the tank.

Neon tetras love plants. Live plants in the tank give them places to hide, rest, and explore. The plants also help keep the water clean and full of oxygen. A tank with green plants, soft light, and a dark bottom looks like a little piece of the Amazon river. It is a beautiful home for a neon tetra.

The tank should also have places for the fish to hide, such as small caves, smooth rocks, and pieces of wood. But it should also have open space in the middle, so the school can swim together freely.

## Why Neon Tetras Are So Loved

People all over the world love neon tetras, and it is easy to see why. They are small, bright, and peaceful. They simply swim, shine, and make the tank look like a little underwater garden full of glowing jewels. Watching them can be calm and relaxing.

Looking after neon tetras teaches you how to be kind and responsible. You learn to feed them the right amount, to keep their water clean, and to watch them carefully to make sure they are healthy. In return, the fish give you beauty and calm every single day.

If you ever see a tank full of neon tetras, take a moment to watch them. You will see a school of tiny fish, glowing blue and red, moving together like a dance. And if you are lucky enough to have neon tetras of your own, you will have a little piece of the Amazon river right in your home, shining bright every day.
