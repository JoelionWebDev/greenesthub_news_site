When you eat a piece of fish for dinner, have you ever wondered where it came from? Some fish are caught in the wild by fishermen, but many of the fish we eat are raised by fish farmers! Fish farmers look after fish in ponds, tanks, and cages in the sea, feeding them and keeping them healthy until they are ready to eat.

Fish farming is also called aquaculture, and it is one of the fastest-growing ways of producing food in the world. It helps to feed many people, and it gives farmers a good living. Let us visit a fish farm and meet the people who raise fish.

## What Is a Fish Farm?

A fish farm is a place where fish are raised for food. There are many different kinds of fish farms. Some are ponds dug into the ground and filled with fresh water. Others are big tanks on land, filled with clean water and kept at just the right temperature. And some are cages floating in the sea or in a big lake, where the fish swim around in the natural water.

On a fish farm, the fish live in a safe, controlled place where they have plenty of food and clean water. The farmer looks after them every day, checking the water, feeding them, and making sure they are healthy and happy. When the fish have grown big enough, they are harvested — which means they are caught and taken to be sold as food.

Fish farms can be small or large. Some are run by a single family with just one or two ponds, while others are big farms with many ponds or sea cages and lots of workers. No matter the size, the job of the fish farmer is the same: to look after the fish and help them grow.

## What Kinds of Fish Do Farmers Raise?

Fish farmers raise many different kinds of fish. Tilapia is one of the most popular farmed fish in the world. It is a hardy, fast-growing fish that lives in warm, fresh water, and it is raised in many countries in Africa, Asia, and South America. Tilapia is a tasty, healthy fish that many people love to eat.

Catfish is another popular farmed fish. It is raised in ponds in many parts of the world, including Africa and North America. Catfish are strong, hardy fish that grow well in ponds, and they are a favourite food for many families.

Salmon is a famous farmed fish too. It is raised in large cages in the cold, clean waters of the sea, in countries like Norway, Scotland, and Chile. Salmon is a beautiful pink fish that is rich in goodness and very popular around the world. Other farmed fish include carp, trout, and sea bass, and farmers also raise prawns and other seafood.

## A Day in the Life of a Fish Farmer

A fish farmer's day starts early. The first job is to check the water in the ponds or tanks. The water must be clean, with just the right amount of oxygen for the fish to breathe. The farmer tests the water and makes sure everything is just right.

Next, it is feeding time! The farmer throws special fish feed into the water, and the fish swim up eagerly to eat. The feed is made from grains, fish meal, and other good things that help the fish to grow strong and healthy. The farmer watches the fish as they eat, to make sure they are all feeding well and looking healthy.

During the day, the farmer may clean the ponds or tanks, check the fish for any signs of sickness, and repair nets or equipment. They may also sort the fish by size, moving the bigger fish to one pond and the smaller fish to another, so that all the fish have plenty of space to grow.

In the evening, the farmer feeds the fish once more and checks the water again before going home. It is a busy day, but it is a rewarding one, because the farmer knows they are helping to feed people with fresh, healthy fish.

## How Do Baby Fish Grow Up?

Fish farming starts with baby fish, which are called fry or fingerlings. The farmer buys the baby fish from a special hatchery, where fish eggs are hatched and the tiny fish are looked after until they are big enough to move to the farm.

The baby fish are put into the ponds or tanks, where they are fed special food to help them grow. As they get bigger, they are moved to larger ponds or cages, where they have more space to swim. The farmer watches them grow, feeding them every day and keeping their water clean.

It takes time for fish to grow big enough to eat — sometimes many months. The farmer is patient, looking after the fish day after day until they are ready. Then the fish are harvested, and new baby fish are brought in to start the cycle all over again.


## Fun Facts

Here are some fun facts about fish farmers:

- Fish farming is also called aquaculture.
- Tilapia, catfish, and salmon are some of the most popular farmed fish.
- Baby fish are called fry or fingerlings.
- Fish farmers check the water every day to make sure it is clean and healthy.
- Fish feed is specially made to help the fish grow strong and healthy.
- Some fish farms are ponds on land, and others are cages floating in the sea.
- Fish farming helps to protect wild fish in the oceans.
- Fish is a healthy food, full of protein and goodness.

## Why Fish Farmers Are Special

Fish farmers are special because they help to feed the world with fresh, healthy fish. They work hard every day, looking after their fish, keeping the water clean, and making sure their fish grow up strong and healthy. Their work is important for our food, for our oceans, and for the families and communities that depend on fish farming for their living.

So next time you eat a piece of fish, think of the fish farmer who looked after it, feeding it every day and keeping its water clean. It is a job that takes patience, care, and a love of fish — and it helps to put healthy, delicious food on our plates. What a wonderful job!
