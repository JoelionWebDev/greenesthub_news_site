Imagine a tiny dog with a long, silky coat that flows like a beautiful dress, a little squashed face, and a big, happy smile. That is the Shih Tzu, one of the sweetest small dogs in the world. Its name means "little lion" in Chinese, and long ago these little dogs lived in the palaces of Chinese emperors, where they were treated like tiny kings and queens.

## What Does a Shih Tzu Look Like?

A Shih Tzu is a small dog with a long body and short legs. Its most famous feature is its coat. The hair on a Shih Tzu grows long and silky, and it can flow right down to the floor. Lots of owners tie the hair on top of its head into a little topknot, so the dog can see properly.

Shih Tzus come in many colours. Some are gold and white, some are black and white, some are all black, and some have a mix of several colours. Their faces are flat and short, with big, dark, round eyes and a sweet expression that makes people want to cuddle them straight away.

Their tails are another lovely feature. A Shih Tzu's tail curls up over its back like a little plume. When a Shih Tzu walks, it holds its head high, like a proud little lion showing off its beautiful coat.

## The Little Lions of Chinese Palaces

The Shih Tzu has a very special history. Hundreds of years ago, these dogs lived in China, in the palaces of emperors and empresses. They were companion dogs, which means their job was to sit with the royal family, keep them company and look beautiful. They were so loved that they were kept inside the palace and were not allowed to leave.

The name Shih Tzu means "little lion" in Chinese. Lions were seen as symbols of power and good luck in China, and these little dogs were bred to look a little like lions, with their long coats that look like a lion's mane and their proud, brave faces. But unlike real lions, Shih Tzus are gentle and loving, and they would much rather sit on your lap than roar!

The dogs were so special to the Chinese royal family that for a long time, very few people outside the palace were allowed to have one. It was only much later that Shih Tzus travelled to other parts of the world and became popular pets everywhere.

## A Big Heart in a Small Body

The best thing about a Shih Tzu is its personality. These little dogs are friendly, happy and loving. They love people, and they love children. A Shih Tzu will happily follow you around the house, sit beside you while you read and curl up on your lap for a sleep.

Shih Tzus are not shy dogs. They like meeting new people and they often greet visitors with a wagging tail and a happy face. They get along well with other pets too, including other dogs and cats, especially when they are introduced gently.

They are playful dogs as well. A Shih Tzu loves a game of fetch, a toy to chew or a gentle run around the garden. But they also love to rest. After a good play, a Shih Tzu is very happy to curl up in a soft bed or on a warm lap for a long nap. They are perfect dogs for people who want a loyal friend who is always close by.

## Life at Home with a Shih Tzu

Shih Tzus make wonderful family pets, and they are well suited to living in houses and flats, because they are small and do not need a huge garden. They are happiest when they are indoors with their family, sharing everyday life.

These dogs do not like being left alone for a very long time. They are companion dogs, which means they were made to be with people. A Shih Tzu that is left alone too much can get sad and lonely. They are happiest in a home where someone is around for most of the day.

Shih Tzus are also quite quiet dogs. They may bark to let you know someone is at the door, but they are not noisy dogs that bark all the time. This makes them good pets for people who live in flats or in quiet neighbourhoods.

## Looking After That Beautiful Coat

A Shih Tzu's long, silky coat is beautiful, but it needs care. Owners brush their Shih Tzu's coat every day to stop the hair from getting tangled and knotted. A gentle brush keeps the coat soft, shiny and free of tangles.

Some owners choose to keep their Shih Tzu's coat short, in what is called a puppy cut. This makes the coat much easier to look after, and the dog still looks very cute. Whether the coat is long or short, regular brushing and a bath now and then keep the dog clean and happy.

Shih Tzus also need their eyes cleaned gently, because their flat faces can make their eyes water a little. Their ears need checking too, and their teeth should be brushed to keep them healthy.


## Fun Facts About Shih Tzus

- The name Shih Tzu means "little lion" in Chinese, because these dogs were bred to look a little like lions.
- Shih Tzus once lived in the palaces of Chinese emperors, where they were kept as royal companions.
- A Shih Tzu's coat can grow so long that it touches the floor, and owners often tie the hair on its head into a little topknot.
- Shih Tzus have big, dark, round eyes and a sweet face that makes them look like they are always smiling.
- Even though their name means "little lion", Shih Tzus are gentle and would much rather cuddle than roar.

## A Tiny Lion with a Giant Heart

The Shih Tzu may be small, but its heart is huge. It was once a royal palace dog, and it still carries itself like a little king or queen, with its head held high and its beautiful coat flowing behind it. But what makes it truly special is not its looks. It is the love it gives to its family every single day.

If you ever meet a Shih Tzu, you will probably notice its long, silky coat first. But stay a little longer and you will notice something even better: a happy, friendly little dog that just wants to be your friend.
