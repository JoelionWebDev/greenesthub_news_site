Dogs love walks. They love to sniff the grass, wag their tails, and trot along beside a friend. But sometimes a dog's owner is busy at work or away from home, and the dog still needs its walk. That is when a dog walker comes to help. A dog walker is a person whose job is to take dogs out for walks and play, so the dogs stay happy, healthy, and full of joy. Dog walkers visit dogs at their homes, clip on the lead, and head outside for fresh air and exercise. For many dogs, the dog walker is one of their favourite people in the whole world.

## What is a dog walker?

A dog walker is a person who walks dogs for their owners. Many dog owners work during the day, and their dogs would be alone at home for hours. A dog walker comes in the middle of the day, takes the dog out for a walk, and brings it home again, tired and happy. Some dog walkers walk one dog at a time, and some walk a small group of dogs together. They walk in parks, along streets, and on country paths, letting the dogs sniff and explore. A good dog walker knows each dog's name, what it likes, and how fast it likes to walk. They are like a trusted friend for the dog, and for the dog's owner too.

## Why do dogs need walks?

Dogs need walks for the same reasons that we need to go outside and play. Walking keeps a dog's body strong and healthy, and it helps the dog stay at a good weight. Walks also keep a dog's mind busy, because there are so many interesting smells, sounds, and sights to discover. A dog that does not get enough walks can get bored, and a bored dog might chew shoes or bark a lot at home. Walks help dogs learn good manners too, because they meet other dogs and other people along the way. Dogs are social animals, which means they like to be with others, and a walk is a wonderful chance for a dog to make friends and have fun.

## What does a dog walker do on a walk?

A dog walker's job starts before the walk even begins. First, the dog walker picks up the dog from its home, gives it a friendly hello, and checks that the dog is ready to go. Then they set off, walking at a pace that suits the dog. Along the way, the dog walker lets the dog sniff the grass and trees, because sniffing is a dog's way of reading the news. In a park, the dog walker might play fetch, throwing a ball for the dog to chase, or let the dog run and play with other friendly dogs. The dog walker always carries water for the dog on hot days, and bags to clean up after the dog. At the end of the walk, they bring the dog home, give it fresh water, and say goodbye until next time.

## Keeping dogs safe on walks

Keeping dogs safe is the most important part of a dog walker's job. A dog walker always keeps a dog on its lead near roads, so the dog cannot run into traffic. They watch out for other dogs, and they only let dogs play together if both dogs are friendly. They stay away from anything that could hurt a dog, like broken glass or food left on the ground that might make a dog sick. On hot days, a dog walker walks in the shade and checks that the ground is not too hot for the dog's paws. A good dog walker also knows what to do if a dog gets scared or tired, and they always stay calm and kind, so the dog feels safe.

## A day in the life of a dog walker

A dog walker's day is full of wagging tails and happy barks. In the morning, the dog walker plans the day, checking which dogs need a walk and what time to visit each one. Then they set off, walking from house to house, picking up dogs along the way. At one house, a big, bouncy dog rushes to the door, wagging its tail so hard that its whole body wiggles. In the park, the dogs run and play, chasing balls and sniffing every tree. The dog walker talks to the dogs, gives them treats, and takes photos to send to their owners. By the end of the day, the dog walker has walked for miles, and all the dogs are tired, happy, and ready for a nap.

## Why dog walkers matter

Dog walkers do a wonderful job for dogs and for their owners. They make sure that dogs get the exercise and fresh air they need, even when their owners are busy. They give dogs company during the day, so the dogs do not feel lonely. For owners, a dog walker brings peace of mind, because they know their dog is being looked after by someone kind and careful. Dog walkers also help dogs stay healthy and well behaved, which makes life better for the whole family. Dog walkers bring joy to dogs, and dogs bring joy to them.

## Fun facts about dog walkers

- A dog walker might walk many miles every day, visiting lots of different dogs.
- Dogs use their amazing sense of smell to learn about the world on a walk.
- Some dog walkers walk a whole group of dogs together, like a little pack.
- A good walk helps a dog stay healthy, happy, and well behaved.
- Many dogs get so excited when they see their dog walker that their whole body wiggles.

Dog walkers are some of the kindest people a dog will ever meet. They turn an ordinary day into an adventure, with new smells to sniff, new friends to meet, and games of fetch in the park. They keep dogs healthy, happy, and safe, and they bring smiles to the dogs and their owners. The next time you see a person walking a happy dog in the park, remember that they might be a dog walker on a very important job. And if you have a dog of your own, you already know how much a good walk can mean to a furry friend.
