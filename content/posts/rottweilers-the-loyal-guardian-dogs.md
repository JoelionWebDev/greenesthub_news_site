Big, strong and brave — that is the Rottweiler! With its black coat, rusty-brown markings and powerful body, a Rottweiler looks like a real guardian. But behind that strong look is a loyal, loving dog that adores its family. Rottweilers have been helping people for hundreds of years, and they are still one of the most trusted guardian dogs in the world.

Rottweilers come from Germany, from a town called Rottweil. Long ago, their ancestors helped Roman soldiers by herding cattle and pulling carts. Later, butchers in Rottweil used these strong dogs to drive cattle to market and to guard their money. The dogs were so strong and clever that people trusted them with important jobs — and the breed was named after the town where they worked.

## What Does a Rottweiler Look Like?

A Rottweiler is a large, strong dog. Grown-ups stand tall and have broad chests, strong legs and big heads. Their coats are short, shiny and black, with lovely rust-coloured markings above the eyes, on the cheeks, on the chest and on the legs. Those little brown "eyebrows" give Rottweilers a very special look.

Their tails are often short, and their ears hang down close to their heads. When a Rottweiler is happy, its whole back end wags along with its tail — a funny little wiggle that owners love. Even though they look serious, Rottweilers can be very silly and playful when they are with their family.

Rottweilers are strong dogs, and they grow up to be heavy — often as heavy as a grown-up person. That is why they need owners who are strong enough to walk them safely and train them well. A Rottweiler is not a dog for everyone, but for the right family, it is a wonderful friend.

## Loyal to the Very End

The best thing about Rottweilers is their loyalty. They love their families deeply and want to be near them all the time. A Rottweiler will follow you from room to room, lie at your feet while you read, and watch over the children as they play. They are calm and gentle at home, and they love cuddles and belly rubs just as much as smaller dogs do.

Rottweilers are also natural guardians. They are watchful and brave, and they will bark to warn their family if a stranger comes near. They are not noisy dogs — they usually only bark when something is wrong. That calm, watchful nature is what makes them such good protectors. But a good guardian is also a friendly dog, so Rottweilers need to meet lots of different people and animals when they are young, so they learn to be confident and calm.

With children, Rottweilers can be gentle and patient, especially when they grow up together. But because they are so big and strong, children should always be with an adult when playing with a Rottweiler, and the dog should be trained to be gentle. A well-trained Rottweiler is a safe and loving family member.

## Clever Dogs That Love to Learn

Rottweilers are very clever dogs. They learn commands quickly and love to work. In the past, they pulled carts and herded cattle. Today, Rottweilers work as police dogs, search-and-rescue dogs, guide dogs and therapy dogs. They can learn many different jobs because they are smart, brave and eager to please.

Training is very important for a Rottweiler. Because they are big and strong, they must learn to walk nicely on a lead, come when called and listen to their owners. Training should always be kind and patient, using treats and praise — never shouting or hurting the dog. A Rottweiler that is trained with love grows up to be confident, calm and well-behaved.

Rottweilers also need lots of exercise and play. They love long walks, games of fetch and exploring new places. A bored Rottweiler can become unhappy and may chew things it should not, so daily walks and playtime are a must. Puzzle toys and training games help keep their clever minds busy too.

## Caring for a Rottweiler

Rottweilers need good food, fresh water, exercise and regular vet visits, just like all dogs. Their short coats are easy to look after — a quick brush once a week keeps them shiny and removes loose hair. They do shed, especially in warmer weather, so a brush and a good vacuum cleaner are a dog owner's best friends!

Because Rottweilers are big dogs, they need a home with enough space. A garden to play in is wonderful, but Rottweilers are family dogs — they should live inside the house with their people, not alone outside. They need company, love and attention every day. A lonely Rottweiler is a sad Rottweiler.

Like all dogs, Rottweilers need to be fed the right food for their size and age. Puppies need food that helps them grow strong bones, while grown-up dogs need food that keeps them fit and healthy. Treats are fine in small amounts, but too many treats can make a dog overweight, which is bad for its joints and heart. Fresh water should always be available.

## A Big Dog With a Big Heart

Rottweilers teach us something important: strength and kindness can live in the same heart. A Rottweiler looks tough, but it is gentle with its family, patient with children and loyal to the people it loves. It shows us that being strong is not about being scary — it is about protecting the ones you love.

They also teach us about responsibility. A big, strong dog needs a family that is ready to train it, exercise it and care for it for many years. Rottweilers can live for around ten years, and they depend on their owners for everything: food, shelter, health care and love. Bringing a Rottweiler home is a big promise, and it should never be made lightly.

If you ever meet a Rottweiler, look past its strong, serious face and watch how it wags, wiggles and leans in for a cuddle. Behind those rusty eyebrows and that powerful body is a loyal friend with a huge heart — a true guardian, and a true member of the family.
