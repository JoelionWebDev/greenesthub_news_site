Have you ever seen a dog that looks like it is smiling all the time? That is a Samoyed. With its thick white coat, dark eyes and happy face, this dog looks like a fluffy cloud that learned how to smile. People often call the Samoyed the smiling dog, and once you meet one, you will understand why. Its mouth turns up at the corners, so it almost always looks cheerful, even when it is just sitting quietly and watching the world go by.

Samoyeds come from a very cold place. Long ago, they lived in the far north, in a snowy land called Siberia. The people who lived there were called the Samoyede people, and the dogs were named after them. Life in Siberia was cold, snowy and hard, and the dogs helped the people in many ways. They pulled sleds across the snow, they helped to herd reindeer, and at night they slept close to the children to keep them warm. That is why Samoyeds love people so much. For a very long time, they were not just working dogs. They were part of the family.

## A Coat Made for Snow

A Samoyed has one of the thickest, fluffiest coats of any dog. It is usually white, although some Samoyeds have a coat that looks a little creamy, like fresh milk with a touch of honey. The coat has two layers. The soft layer close to the skin keeps the dog warm, and the longer layer on top keeps out snow, wind and rain. When snow falls on a Samoyed, it often just sits on top of the fur instead of melting into it, which helps the dog stay dry and warm.

That beautiful coat needs a lot of care. Samoyeds shed their fur, and at certain times of the year they shed a great deal of it. Owners brush them often to keep the coat clean and free of tangles. Brushing also helps the dog feel comfortable and gives the owner and the dog some happy time together. If you ever brushed a Samoyed, you would probably end up with a small cloud of white fluff on your clothes, and the dog would wag its tail as if to say thank you.

## The Famous Sammy Smile

The most famous thing about a Samoyed is its smile. Look closely at a Samoyed and you will see that the corners of its mouth turn upward. Its dark eyes and black nose stand out against the white fur, which makes the smile even easier to see. People love this smile because it makes the dog look friendly and kind, and most Samoyeds really are friendly and kind.

Samoyeds are known for being gentle and playful. They love to be with people, and they often follow their owners from room to room, just to be close to them. They are not dogs that like to be left alone for a long time. They want to join in with whatever the family is doing, whether that is a walk in the park, a game in the garden, or a quiet evening at home. A Samoyed that is left alone too much can become bored and sad, so it needs a family that has plenty of time for it.

## A Dog That Loves to Play

Samoyeds are full of energy. They love to run, jump, play and explore. In the snow, they are in their happiest place. They can run and roll in the snow for hours, and their thick coats keep them warm while they play. In warmer places, they still love to play, but they need shade, cool water and rest, because their thick coats can make them hot.

Samoyeds are often wonderful friends for children. They are patient, gentle and playful, and they enjoy games like fetch and hide and seek. They are big dogs, though, and they can be bouncy when they are excited, so young children should always play with them while a grown-up is nearby. When children are kind and gentle with a Samoyed, the dog will often become their best friend and follow them everywhere.

Because Samoyeds love people so much, they are not the best dogs for guarding a house. They are more likely to greet a visitor with a wagging tail than with a bark of warning. They do bark, of course, especially when they are excited or when they want attention, but their bark is usually a happy one. They want to be friends with everyone they meet.

## Life With a Samoyed

Life with a Samoyed is never boring. These dogs need plenty of exercise every day, and they love long walks, games and adventures. They also need a lot of brushing, fresh water and good food. Because they are so friendly, they do best in a home where someone is around for most of the day. They are happiest when they are part of everything the family does.

If you ever meet a Samoyed, you will probably notice three things straight away. First, its coat is softer and fluffier than it looks in pictures. Second, it will probably try to say hello to you, with a wagging tail and a happy face. And third, you will understand why people call it the smiling dog. That smile is not just in its face. It is in the way it plays, the way it greets people, and the way it loves to be close to its family.

The Samoyed began life in the cold north, helping people to pull sleds, herd reindeer and keep children warm. Today, it is a much-loved family dog all over the world. It has kept the same warm heart, the same love for people, and the same happy smile that it had long ago. So the next time you see a big white fluffy dog with a smile on its face, you will know just what kind of dog it is. It is a Samoyed, the smiling white dog, and it is smiling just for you.
