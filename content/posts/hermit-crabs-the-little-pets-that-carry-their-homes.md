Imagine carrying your whole house on your back wherever you go. That is what a hermit crab does every single day! A hermit crab is a small animal that lives inside an empty sea shell. The shell keeps it safe, soft, and snug. When a hermit crab grows too big for its shell, it goes shopping for a new one. It tries on shell after shell until it finds one that fits just right. Hermit crabs are funny, busy little animals. They climb, dig, explore, and sometimes even swap shells with their friends.

## What is a hermit crab?

A hermit crab is a kind of crab, and crabs are animals with hard bodies and jointed legs. But a hermit crab is different from most crabs. Its back end is soft, so it needs a shell to protect it. The hermit crab tucks its soft tail inside an empty shell and holds on tight with its legs. Its front part is hard, with two claws and walking legs. One claw is usually bigger than the other. The big claw works like a door. When the crab feels scared, it pulls itself into its shell and blocks the opening with its big claw. Hermit crabs have eyes on little stalks that stick up, so they can peek out and look around.

## Where do hermit crabs live?

Wild hermit crabs live near the sea. You can find them on sandy beaches, rocky shores, and in shallow water. Some hermit crabs live in the sea, and some live on land near the beach. Land hermit crabs like warm, damp places with sand to dig in and plants to hide under. They come out at night to look for food and new shells. Empty shells wash up on the beach, and hermit crabs try them on like new clothes. Pet hermit crabs live in a special home called a terrarium. Their home has soft sand to dig in, fresh and salty water dishes, shells to try on, and things to climb.

## What do hermit crabs eat?

Hermit crabs are not fussy eaters. In the wild, they eat all sorts of things they find on the beach. They eat tiny bits of seaweed, fallen fruit, dead leaves, and small pieces of food washed up by the sea. They are nature's cleaners, helping to tidy up the beach. Pet hermit crabs eat a mix of foods that a grown-up chooses for them. They like fresh fruit, vegetables, and special food from a pet shop. Hermit crabs eat at night, when it is cool and quiet. They use their small claws to pick up tiny pieces of food and bring them to their mouths. Pet hermit crabs need two dishes of water: one with fresh water and one with salty water.

## A day in a hermit crab's life

A hermit crab's day is quiet and sleepy, but its night is busy. During the day, a hermit crab likes to rest buried in the sand or tucked away in a shady hiding place. It sleeps inside its shell, safe and snug. When evening comes, it wakes up and starts to explore. It climbs over rocks, digs in the sand, and sniffs the air with its feelers. Hermit crabs are great climbers and love to dig. Sometimes hermit crabs meet each other and have a little shell swap. One crab may try on another crab's shell, and if it fits better, they trade. Hermit crabs are social animals, and they like to live with other hermit crabs.

## Baby hermit crabs

Baby hermit crabs start their lives in the sea. The mother hermit crab carries tiny eggs on her body until they are ready to hatch. When the eggs hatch, tiny baby crabs float in the sea. The babies drift in the water, eating tiny bits of food and growing bigger. As they grow, they change shape many times. After a while, the young crabs are ready to find their first shells. They crawl onto the beach and look for a tiny empty shell that fits. Once they find one, they tuck themselves inside and start their lives as hermit crabs. Pet hermit crabs are not usually bred at home, because their babies need the sea to grow.

## Looking after a pet hermit crab

Hermit crabs can be lovely pets, but they need a grown-up to help look after them. A pet hermit crab needs a warm, damp home with soft sand, fresh and salty water, and lots of empty shells to try on. Its home should be cleaned often, and its water should be changed every day. Hermit crabs are delicate, so you should never pull a crab out of its shell. That would hurt it very badly. If you want to hold a hermit crab, a grown-up should help, and you should be very gentle and slow. Always wash your hands before and after touching a hermit crab or its home. Hermit crabs like quiet and calm, and they need time to rest during the day. They also need friends, so it is best to keep more than one hermit crab together. With kind, patient care, pet hermit crabs can be happy little friends for many years.

## Fun facts about hermit crabs

- A hermit crab carries its home on its back, and it moves into a bigger shell when it grows.
- One claw is usually bigger than the other, and the big claw works like a door to block the shell.
- Hermit crabs are great climbers and love to dig in soft sand.
- Some hermit crabs swap shells with each other if a shell fits better.
- Hermit crabs eat at night, when it is cool and quiet.
- Baby hermit crabs start their lives floating in the sea before they find their first shells.

Hermit crabs are small animals with big personalities. They climb, dig, explore, and try on new homes like we try on new shoes. Next time you walk along a beach, look closely at the shells. One of them might just be a little hermit crab, peeking out with its stalky eyes, ready for its next adventure.
