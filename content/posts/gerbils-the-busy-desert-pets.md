Have you ever met a pet that is always busy? A gerbil is just like that. Gerbils are small, furry animals with big eyes, soft fur, and long tails. They are always doing something. They dig, they hop, they chew, and they run about. If you watch a gerbil for a little while, you will see that it never seems to sit still for long. That is what makes gerbils such fun pets to watch and to care for.

Gerbils come from dry, sandy places. In the wild, many gerbils live in deserts and open grasslands where the ground is dry and the sun is strong. They are very good at living in these places. Their sandy-coloured fur helps them blend in with the sand and dry grass around them, which helps them stay safe. Long ago, people started keeping gerbils as pets, and now gerbils live in homes all over the world.

## Digging Is Their Favourite Job

If there is one thing a gerbil loves more than anything else, it is digging. Gerbils are champion diggers. In the wild, they dig long tunnels under the ground. These tunnels are called burrows. A burrow is like a little underground house. It has rooms for sleeping, rooms for storing food, and tunnels that lead in and out. The burrow keeps the gerbil cool when the sun is hot and warm when the night is cold. It also keeps the gerbil safe from animals that might want to catch it.

Pet gerbils love to dig too. If you give a gerbil deep, soft bedding in its home, it will dig and dig happily. It will push the bedding with its little paws and make tunnels and little hills. Watching a gerbil dig is one of the best parts of having one as a pet. It is what gerbils are made to do.

## Hopping, Running, and Playing

Gerbils have strong back legs, and they use them to hop and jump. When a gerbil is excited, it might hop around its home in little jumps. It looks a bit like a tiny kangaroo. Gerbils also love to run. A wheel made for gerbils can give them a safe place to run and use up their energy. They run and run, and their little legs move so fast that it is hard to follow them with your eyes.

Gerbils also like to play with toys. They enjoy cardboard tubes, small wooden toys, and things they can chew. Chewing is very important for a gerbil. A gerbil's front teeth keep growing all through its life, so chewing helps keep them short and healthy. That is why a gerbil will chew on almost anything you give it. Giving a gerbil safe things to chew keeps it happy and healthy. Always ask a grown-up to check that a toy is safe for a gerbil before you put it in its home.

## Gerbils Love Company

Gerbils are friendly animals, and they like to live with other gerbils. In the wild, gerbils live in family groups. They sleep close together in a pile, groom each other, and play together. Pet gerbils are often happiest when they live with a friend. Two gerbils that grow up together usually get along well and keep each other company when no one is around.

Gerbils also like people, but they need time to get used to you. When a gerbil first comes home, it might be a little shy. That is normal. If you sit quietly near its home and talk to it in a soft voice, it will learn that you are a friend. After a while, it may climb onto your hand to say hello or take a small treat from you. Always move slowly and gently, and never pick a gerbil up by its tail. With patience, a gerbil can become a lovely little friend.

## What Do Gerbils Eat?

Gerbils eat seeds, grains, and small bits of vegetables. In the wild, they search for seeds and dry plants to eat. They also store food in their burrows, so they always have a snack ready. Pet gerbils eat a special mix of seeds and grains made just for them. You can also give them tiny pieces of fresh vegetables, like carrot or broccoli, as a treat. Fresh, clean water should always be ready for them to drink.

## Keeping a Gerbil Happy at Home

A happy gerbil needs a safe, cosy home. Gerbils need a home with lots of space to run and dig. A large tank with a lid is a good home for a gerbil, because it can hold lots of deep bedding for digging. The home should be kept in a quiet place, away from loud noises and away from very hot or very cold spots. Gerbils do not like to be in bright sunlight all day.

A gerbil's home needs to be cleaned often. Fresh bedding, clean water, and fresh food every day will help your gerbil stay healthy. A gerbil also loves a sand bath. That is a small dish of clean sand where the gerbil can roll around. Rolling in sand helps keep its fur clean and soft, and it is very funny to watch.

Gerbils are awake during the day, so they are good pets for children who like to watch them play after school. They sleep at night, often curled up together in a nest made of soft bedding.

## Why Gerbils Make Wonderful Pets

Gerbils are small, gentle, and full of energy. They are curious and love to explore. They do not need to go for walks, and they do not make much noise, so they are good pets for homes and flats. They are also clean animals that spend a lot of time grooming their soft fur.

Looking after a gerbil teaches you how to be kind and responsible. You learn to feed it every day, to clean its home, and to be gentle with a small living thing. In return, the gerbil gives you hours of fun. You will laugh when it pops out of a tunnel, smile when it hops across its home, and feel proud when it trusts you enough to climb onto your hand.

If you ever meet a gerbil, take a moment to watch it. You will see a busy little desert pet that loves to dig, hop, and play. And if you are lucky enough to have a gerbil of your own, you will have a small, furry friend who is always busy and always ready to make you smile.
