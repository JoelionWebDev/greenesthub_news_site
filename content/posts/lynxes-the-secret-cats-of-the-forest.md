Lynxes are shy, secret wild cats that live in the quiet forests of the north. They have soft spotted fur, a short tail, tall ears with little black tufts on top, and huge paws that spread out wide like snowshoes. A lynx is much bigger than a house cat, but it is a master of hiding. Lynxes are excellent hunters with sharp eyes and ears, and they are perfectly made for life in cold, snowy woods. If you ever spot a lynx, you are very lucky indeed, because you have met one of the forest's best-kept secrets.

## What is a lynx?

A lynx is a wild cat, a cousin of the lion, the tiger, and the house cat that might live in your home. There are four kinds of lynx in the world: the Canada lynx, the Eurasian lynx, the Iberian lynx, and the bobcat. They all look quite similar, with thick fur, a short tail, and tall, pointed ears. A lynx has a round face with a little beard of fur around its cheeks, which makes it look as if it is wearing a fluffy ruff. Its coat is usually grey or brown with spots, which helps it blend in with the trees and shadows. The black tufts on the tips of its ears are one of the easiest ways to tell a lynx from other wild cats.

## The amazing ears and paws

A lynx has two wonderful tools that help it live in the snowy forest: its ears and its paws. Its tall ears can turn and twist to catch the tiniest sounds, like a mouse moving under the snow or a bird rustling in the bushes. The little black tufts on the ear tips may help the lynx hear even better. A lynx's paws are very large and furry, much bigger than you would expect for its size. In deep snow, the wide paws spread out like snowshoes, stopping the lynx from sinking in. This lets a lynx walk and run across soft snow with ease, while other animals struggle and sink. With its great ears listening and its snowshoe paws carrying it, a lynx can hunt even in the middle of a cold winter.

## Where do lynxes live?

Lynxes live in the forests of North America, Europe, and Asia. The Canada lynx lives in the cold forests of Canada and Alaska, where snow lies deep for many months of the year. The Eurasian lynx lives in forests across Europe and Asia, from Spain all the way to Siberia. The Iberian lynx lives in Spain and Portugal, and the bobcat lives across North America, from Canada down to Mexico. A lynx lives alone and has a large home range, which is the area of forest it roams in to find food. It marks its territory with scent so that other lynxes know the forest is already taken.

## What do lynxes eat?

Lynxes are carnivores, which means they eat meat. Their favourite food depends on where they live. The Canada lynx loves to hunt snowshoe hares, and its numbers rise and fall with the number of hares in the forest. The Eurasian lynx hunts roe deer and other small deer, as well as hares and birds. The Iberian lynx mostly hunts rabbits, and the bobcat eats rabbits, hares, mice, and birds. A lynx is a patient hunter. It creeps quietly through the forest, crouches low, and watches its prey with its sharp eyes. When it is close enough, it pounces in one quick leap, catching its meal with its sharp claws. They have excellent eyesight and can spot a small animal from far away, even in dim light.

## A secret life in the forest

Lynxes are solitary animals, which means they live alone for most of the year. They are also very shy, and they avoid people whenever they can. A lynx spends its days resting in a hidden spot, such as a hollow tree, a rocky ledge, or a thick bush. At dawn and dusk, it wakes up and goes hunting. They do not make a den in one place for long, but move around their home range, sleeping in different hiding spots. In winter, their thick fur keeps them warm, and their wide paws let them move through deep snow. 

## Baby lynxes

Baby lynxes are called kittens, just like baby house cats. A mother lynx usually has two or three kittens at a time, and they are born in a hidden den, often in a hollow log, a cave, or a thick tangle of bushes. When the kittens are born, their eyes are closed and they are small and helpless. The mother feeds them with milk and keeps them warm and safe in the den. After a few weeks, the kittens open their eyes and start to explore. They play together, pouncing on each other and practising the hunting moves they will need when they grow up. The kittens stay with their mother for many months, learning the secrets of the forest. When they are about a year old, they leave to find their own home range and live as secret cats of the forest.

## Fun facts about lynxes

- The black tufts on a lynx's ears help it hear tiny sounds in the forest.
- A lynx's wide, furry paws work like snowshoes, letting it walk on deep snow without sinking.
- Lynxes have very short tails, which is why the bobcat is sometimes called by its short tail.
- A lynx can spot a small animal from far away, even in dim light at dawn or dusk.
- The Canada lynx loves snowshoe hares so much that its numbers rise and fall with the hares.

Lynxes are quiet, beautiful, and wonderfully secret animals. With their tufted ears, snowshoe paws, and spotted coats, they are perfectly made for life in the cold, quiet forests of the north. Next time you walk through a snowy wood, look closely at the trees and shadows, because a lynx might be watching you.
