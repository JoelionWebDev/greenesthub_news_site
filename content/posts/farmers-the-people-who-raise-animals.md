Think about your breakfast this morning. Maybe you had eggs, milk, bread, or fruit. Where did all that food come from? It came from farms, and from the hard work of farmers. Farmers are the people who raise animals and grow crops, and they feed the whole world. They look after cows, chickens, goats, sheep, and pigs, and they grow grains, fruits, and vegetables in their fields. Farming is one of the oldest jobs in the world, and it is still one of the most important. Without farmers, there would be no food on our tables, and no milk in our cups.

## What is a farmer?

A farmer is a person who works on a farm, raising animals and growing plants for food. Some farmers keep animals, like cows for milk, chickens for eggs, and sheep for wool. Other farmers grow crops, like maize, rice, wheat, and vegetables. Many farmers do both, raising animals and growing crops on the same farm. Farmers work outdoors in all kinds of weather, in sunshine and in rain, because animals and plants need care every single day. A farmer must know a lot about animals, plants, weather, and the soil. Farming is hard work, but it is also a job full of fresh air, animals, and the joy of watching things grow.

## The animals on a farm

A farm is home to many different animals, and each one has its own job. Cows give us milk, which we drink and turn into cheese, butter, and yoghurt. Chickens lay eggs, and they also give us meat. Goats give us milk too, and they are very good at eating grass on rocky hills where other animals cannot graze. Sheep give us wool, which is made into warm clothes, and they also give us milk and meat. Pigs are clever animals that love to roll in the mud to keep cool. Farmers also keep horses and donkeys on some farms, to help carry loads and work in the fields. Every animal on the farm needs food, water, and a clean, safe place to live, and the farmer makes sure they have it all.

## What do farmers do every day?

A farmer's day starts very early, often before the sun comes up. First, the farmer feeds the animals and gives them fresh water. Cows need to be milked, chickens need their feed, and pigs need their breakfast too. Then the farmer checks that all the animals are healthy and happy, looking at their eyes, their coats, and how they move. During the day, the farmer might fix fences, clean the barns, plant seeds, or water the crops. In the fields, the farmer watches the plants grow, and pulls out weeds that would steal water from the crops. At the end of the day, the farmer feeds the animals once more and makes sure they are safe in their barns for the night. Animals need care every day, even on weekends and holidays.

## How do farmers look after their animals?

Farmers look after their animals with great care, because healthy animals give good milk, eggs, and wool. They give the animals plenty of food, like grass, hay, and grains, and they always make sure the animals have clean water to drink. They keep the barns and pens clean, so the animals stay healthy and comfortable. Farmers give their animals space to move around and to rest in the shade on hot days. They also watch their animals closely, and if an animal looks sick, the farmer calls a vet, which is an animal doctor, to come and help. Some farmers let their animals graze in open fields, where they can eat fresh grass and enjoy the sunshine. Happy animals make the best farms.

## Growing food on the farm

Farmers do not only raise animals, they also grow the food that animals and people eat. They plant seeds in the soil, water them, and watch them grow into crops. Some crops, like maize and rice, feed people all over the world. Other crops, like hay and grass, are grown to feed the animals on the farm. Farmers must know when to plant, because seeds need the right season and the right weather to grow. They must also know when to harvest, which is when the crops are ready to be picked. Harvest time is busy and exciting, when the fields are full of golden grains or ripe vegetables, ready to be gathered and taken to market. Good farmers take care of the soil too, so that it stays rich and healthy for the next year's crops.

## Why farmers matter

Farmers matter because they feed us all. Every meal you eat starts with a farmer, whether it is the eggs from a chicken, the milk from a cow, or the bread made from wheat grown in a field. Farmers also help their communities, selling food at markets and giving people fresh, healthy things to eat. They look after the land, keeping fields and pastures green and full of life. Farming also helps the world, because farms can be home to birds, bees, and many other wild animals. When we buy food from farmers and say thank you, we are thanking the people who wake up early every day to make sure we all have something to eat.

## Fun facts about farmers

- Farmers wake up very early to feed and care for their animals.
- Cows give us milk, which can be made into cheese, butter, and yoghurt.
- Chickens lay eggs, and some chickens can lay an egg almost every day.
- Sheep give us wool, which is made into warm jumpers and blankets.
- Farmers grow crops like maize and rice that feed people all over the world.

Farmers are the quiet heroes who feed the world. They wake before dawn, care for their animals in sunshine and rain, and grow the food that fills our plates. The next time you sit down to eat, think of the farmer who raised the chickens, milked the cows, and planted the crops. And if you ever visit a farm, listen to the cows mooing, the chickens clucking, and the sheep baaing, and remember that behind it all is a farmer, working hard with a loving heart.
