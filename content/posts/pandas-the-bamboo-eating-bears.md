Giant pandas are some of the most loved animals in the world. With their round black ears, dark eye patches, and soft white fur, they look like cuddly toys come to life. Pandas are bears, and they live in the misty mountains of China. They are famous for one thing above all else: they love to eat bamboo. Pandas spend most of their waking hours munching on bamboo, sitting down and holding the stems in their paws like we hold food in our hands. They are gentle, calm, and a little bit sleepy, and they make everyone who sees them smile.

## What is a giant panda?

A giant panda is a bear that lives in China. It has thick black-and-white fur, round ears, and a short tail. Pandas are big animals, much bigger than a dog, and they can weigh as much as a grown person or more. Their black patches around their eyes make them look like they are wearing a mask. Some people think the eye patches help pandas recognise each other. Pandas have strong jaws and big teeth that help them crunch through tough bamboo. Their paws are special too. A panda has an extra thumb-like bone that helps it hold bamboo stems. This clever paw makes eating much easier. Pandas are quiet animals, and they like to live alone rather than in big groups.

## Where do pandas live?

Giant pandas live in the mountain forests of central China. These forests are cool and misty, and they are full of bamboo. Pandas live high up in the mountains, where the air is fresh and the trees are thick. They spend their time wandering through the bamboo forests, looking for the best bamboo to eat. Pandas do not build homes like some animals do. They shelter under trees or in hollow logs when it rains. Long ago, pandas lived in many parts of China, but today they live in a smaller area. People are working hard to protect the forests where pandas live, so these gentle bears always have a home.

## A bamboo diet

Pandas eat bamboo, bamboo, and more bamboo. Bamboo is a fast-growing plant that looks like a tall green grass. Pandas eat the leaves, stems, and shoots of bamboo. They sit down, pick up a stem with their paws, and crunch it up. Pandas eat for many hours every day because bamboo does not give them much energy. They need to eat a lot of bamboo to stay full and strong. A panda can spend most of its waking day eating. Pandas also eat a little bit of other plants, and very rarely they may eat small animals, but bamboo is their main food. Pandas are bears, but unlike most bears they do not eat much meat at all. Their whole life is built around bamboo.

## A panda's day

A panda's day is simple and peaceful. It wakes up, eats bamboo, rests, and eats some more. Pandas often take naps during the day, lying down in a comfy spot or leaning against a tree. They are not in a hurry. Pandas like to climb trees too, even though they are big. They climb up to rest on branches or to look around. Young pandas are playful and love to roll around, tumble, and climb. Pandas are mostly quiet, but they do make sounds. They bleat like goats, bark, and make little chirps to talk to each other. Mother pandas talk to their cubs with soft sounds. A panda's calm life is one of the things that makes people love them so much.

## Baby pandas

Baby pandas are called cubs. When a panda cub is born, it is tiny, pink, and has almost no fur. It is one of the smallest babies compared to its mother's size in the animal world. The mother panda holds her cub close and keeps it warm. She feeds it milk and looks after it all the time. The cub grows quickly. Its black-and-white fur grows in, and soon it looks like a tiny panda. After a few months, the cub starts to walk and play. It climbs, tumbles, and explores the forest near its mother. Cubs love to play with each other and with their mothers. They practise climbing and rolling, which helps them grow strong. A cub stays with its mother for a long time before it is ready to live on its own.

## Why are pandas black and white?

Scientists have thought a lot about why pandas are black and white. Their colouring may help them hide in their forest home. The white fur blends in with snowy patches, and the black fur blends in with dark shadows and tree trunks. The dark eye patches may help pandas recognise each other, or they may help cut down glare from the sun. The black ears may help pandas hear better, or they may be a warning to other animals. Nobody knows for sure, but the black-and-white coat is now one of the most famous looks in the animal world. It makes the panda easy to recognise, and it has helped people around the world care about protecting pandas.

## Protecting pandas

Giant pandas need our help. Long ago, there were many more pandas, but their forests were cut down, and they lost their homes. Today, people in China and around the world are working hard to protect pandas. They have made nature reserves where pandas can live safely. They plant bamboo and look after the forests. People also breed pandas in special centres and help them return to the wild. Thanks to all this hard work, panda numbers have grown. The panda is a symbol of caring for nature all over the world. When we protect pandas, we also protect the forests and all the other animals that live there. Every panda is a reminder that we can make a difference.

## Fun facts about pandas

- Giant pandas eat bamboo for most of the day because bamboo gives them very little energy.
- A panda has a special thumb-like bone on its paw that helps it hold bamboo stems while eating.
- Baby panda cubs are born tiny, pink, and almost hairless, and they grow very quickly.
- Pandas can climb trees even though they are big bears, and they often rest on branches.
- The giant panda is a symbol of caring for nature all over the world.
