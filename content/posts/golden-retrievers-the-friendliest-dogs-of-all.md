If dogs could win a prize for friendliness, golden retrievers would take it home every time. These big, happy dogs greet everyone with a wagging tail and a smile. They love children, they love games, and they love being part of a family.

A golden retriever is the kind of dog that follows you from room to room, just to be near you. If you are sad, it will rest its head on your lap. If you are happy, it will bounce around with joy. That is what makes golden retrievers so special — they love people with all their hearts.

## Where Did Golden Retrievers Come From?

Golden retrievers come from Scotland, a country of green hills, cold lakes, and rainy weather. Long ago, hunters in Scotland wanted a dog that could fetch birds from lakes and fields without damaging them. So they bred a dog that was gentle, clever, and a strong swimmer. That dog became the golden retriever.

Their thick, golden coat keeps them warm in cold water, and their webbed feet help them swim. Even today, golden retrievers love water. Give one a paddling pool, and it will splash around happily.

Golden retrievers soon became loved as family pets too, not just hunting dogs. People saw how gentle and patient they were with children, and how eager they were to please. From Scotland, they spread all over the world, and now you can find golden retrievers in homes almost everywhere.

## What Does a Golden Retriever Look Like?

A golden retriever is a large, strong dog with a beautiful golden coat. The coat can be light cream, rich gold, or a deeper golden colour. It is thick and a little wavy, with longer, feathery fur on its legs, chest, and tail. The thick coat keeps the dog warm and dry, even in rain or cold water.

Golden retrievers have kind, dark eyes, floppy ears, and a big, friendly smile. Their long, feathery tails wag all the time when they are happy. They have strong legs and big paws, perfect for running, swimming, and playing fetch.

Golden retrievers shed their fur a lot, so their coats need brushing often. Brushing keeps the fur tidy and stops it from getting tangled. Many owners brush their golden retriever a few times a week, and the dog usually loves the attention.

## A Dog That Loves People

Golden retrievers are famous for their gentle nature. They are patient with children, kind to strangers, and friendly with other animals. That is why they are often chosen as guide dogs for people who cannot see well, and as therapy dogs that visit hospitals and schools to cheer people up.

A golden retriever wants to be with its family all the time. It does not like being left alone for hours and hours. A lonely golden retriever can become sad, so families need to make sure the dog gets plenty of company, cuddles, and play every day.

These dogs are also very clever. They learn commands quickly and love to please their owners. When a golden retriever learns something new, it wags its tail proudly, as if to say, "Look what I can do!" That eagerness to learn makes them one of the easiest dogs to train.

## What Do Golden Retrievers Eat?

Golden retrievers eat good-quality dog food, which gives them everything they need to stay strong and healthy. Puppies eat food made for young dogs, and grown-up dogs eat food made for adults. Owners measure the food carefully, because golden retrievers love to eat and can get too heavy if they eat too much.

They also need fresh, clean water every day, especially after running and playing. Some owners give their dogs healthy treats like carrot sticks or apple slices, but treats should only be a small part of the diet. Foods like chocolate are dangerous for dogs, so they must never be shared.

Feeding times are also a good chance to teach manners. A golden retriever can learn to sit and wait patiently before eating, and to take treats gently from your hand.

## Play, Walks, and Learning

Golden retrievers are full of energy and need lots of exercise every day. They love long walks, running in the park, and playing fetch. Fetch is their favourite game of all — throw a ball, and a golden retriever will race after it, pick it up gently, and bring it straight back to you, ready to go again.

They also love swimming, and many golden retrievers will jump into any water they find. Playing in water is great fun for them and keeps them cool on hot days. Owners should always watch their dogs near water, just to be safe.

Training is important too. Golden retrievers love learning new tricks, like shaking hands, rolling over, and fetching a newspaper. Training works best with kindness and treats, and with short, fun sessions. A well-trained golden retriever is a happy dog that can join in family life safely.

## A Happy Home for a Golden Retriever

A golden retriever needs a home with space to move and a garden to play in, if possible. It needs a cosy bed in a quiet corner, where it can rest after a busy day. Golden retrievers like to sleep near their family, so many of them curl up in the living room or near a bedroom door.

They need brushing, walks, play, and company every single day. They also need visits to the vet to stay healthy, and their ears and teeth need checking often. Looking after a golden retriever takes time, but the dog gives back so much love that it is worth every minute.

If your family is thinking about a golden retriever, talk it over with a grown-up first. A dog is a friend for many years, and the whole family should be ready to help with feeding, walking, and play.

## Fun Facts

- Golden retrievers were first bred in Scotland to fetch birds for hunters.
- Their webbed feet make them excellent swimmers.
- Golden retrievers are often trained as guide dogs and therapy dogs.
- They love fetch more than almost any other game.
- A golden retriever's coat can be light cream or deep gold.
- Golden retrievers are patient and gentle with children.

Golden retrievers are loyal, loving, and always ready to play. If you ever meet one, be ready for a wet nose, a waggy tail, and a brand-new best friend!
