Quack, quack, quack. If you hear a cheerful quacking sound coming from a farmyard pond, it might be a Khaki Campbell duck. This is a small, busy duck with soft khaki-coloured feathers, like the colour of dry grass or sandy earth. It loves to swim, it loves to explore, and most of all, it loves to lay eggs. In fact, the Khaki Campbell is famous for laying more eggs than almost any other kind of duck. It is a hard-working little bird, and farmers and families all over the world love it.

The Khaki Campbell duck was first bred in England, many years ago, by a woman named Adele Campbell. She wanted a duck that was good at laying eggs, and that could live happily on a farm. She bred different kinds of ducks together, and after a lot of careful work, she created a new kind of duck. She named it after herself, Campbell, and after its colour, khaki. That is how the Khaki Campbell duck got its name, and it has been a favourite farm duck ever since.

## What Does a Khaki Campbell Look Like?

Khaki Campbell ducks are medium-sized ducks, smaller than some farm ducks, but bigger than a tiny wild duck. The females, called hens, are a soft khaki brown colour all over, with a slightly darker head and a lighter belly. The males, called drakes, are a little bit different. They have a darker head, with a greenish shine, and their bodies are a mix of khaki and brown. Both the hens and the drakes have orange legs and dark bills, and they walk with a funny little waddle that makes people smile.

Khaki Campbells are not as round and fluffy as some ducks. They are slim and upright, and they often hold their bodies up straight when they walk, almost like a penguin. They are very active ducks, and they love to move around. They are not ducks that like to sit still for long. They are always busy, always exploring, and always looking for something interesting to eat.

## The Busy Little Egg Layers

The most famous thing about Khaki Campbell ducks is their eggs. A healthy Khaki Campbell hen can lay a lot of eggs, far more than many other ducks. Some hens lay eggs almost every day when they are in their laying season, which is why farmers love them so much. Their eggs are white, or sometimes a pale cream colour, and they are a little bit bigger than a chicken egg. They are good to eat, and they are used in cooking and baking, just like chicken eggs.

Khaki Campbell hens start laying eggs when they are still quite young, and they can keep laying for many years.

## Life in the Pond

Like all ducks, Khaki Campbells love water. They love to swim, to splash, to dive their heads under the water, and to preen their feathers.

Khaki Campbells are also great foragers. That means they love to search for food on land as well as in the water. They will wander around a garden or a farmyard, pecking at the ground, looking for insects, slugs, snails and weeds. Farmers love this, because the ducks help to keep the garden free of pests, without using any chemicals. A Khaki Campbell is like a little gardener, working hard all day, and quacking happily as it goes.

These ducks are also very social animals. They love to be with other ducks, and they are happiest when they live in a small flock. A lone duck can become lonely and sad, so Khaki Campbells should always have duck friends to live with. When they are together, they quack to each other, they swim together, and they rest together in a cosy group. It is a lovely sight to see a group of Khaki Campbells waddling along in a line, following each other like a little feathered parade.

## A Friendly Farm Duck

Khaki Campbell ducks are known for being calm and friendly. They are not as shy as some wild ducks, and they can become quite tame if they are handled gently from a young age. They may not like to be picked up, but they will often come close to people, especially if they know that food is coming. They are curious birds, and they love to watch what people are doing.

These ducks are also very hardy. They can live in many different kinds of weather, and they do not mind rain or cold as much as some other animals. Their feathers keep them warm and dry, and they have a layer of oil on their feathers that helps water to roll off.

## Looking After Khaki Campbell Ducks

Looking after Khaki Campbell ducks is not hard, but it does need care. They need a safe home, with a dry shelter where they can sleep at night, away from other animals that might harm them. They need fresh water every day, not just for drinking, but also for swimming and cleaning. They need good duck food, plus fresh greens and grains, and they need plenty of space to walk, swim and explore.

Ducks also need to be kept safe from harm. Their home should have a fence, and their shelter should be closed at night.

## A Duck Worth Meeting

The Khaki Campbell duck may not be the biggest duck, or the loudest duck, or the most colourful duck. But it is one of the hardest-working and friendliest ducks you will ever meet. It swims, it forages, it quacks, and it lays lots of lovely eggs. It is a duck that loves to be busy, and it is a duck that loves to be with its friends.

So, the next time you visit a farm, or a pond, or a garden, listen for a cheerful quack. Look for a small, slim duck with soft khaki feathers, walking upright with a happy waddle. That will be a Khaki Campbell, the busy little egg layer, and it will probably be on its way to the pond for a swim, or to the garden for a snack, or to its nest to lay another egg. It is a duck that never stops, and that is just what makes it so special.
