High in the mountains of Japan, long ago, there lived a strong and noble dog called the Akita. With its thick coat, pointed ears and curled tail, the Akita looks proud and brave, like a dog from a storybook. But the most famous thing about the Akita is not how it looks. It is how loyal it is. An Akita loves its family with all its heart, and it will stay faithful to the people it loves, no matter what happens.

The Akita comes from a place in Japan called Akita, and the dog was named after that place. For hundreds of years, Akitas lived with people in the cold, snowy north of Japan. They were strong working dogs. They helped people to hunt, to guard homes and to protect families. They had to be brave, clever and tough, because life in the mountains could be hard. Over time, people in Japan came to love and respect the Akita very much. In Japan, the Akita is seen as a symbol of good health, happiness and long life. People even give Akita statues and gifts to new babies, to wish them a healthy and happy life.

## The Story of Hachiko

The most famous Akita of all was a dog named Hachiko. Hachiko lived in Japan many years ago, and his story is still told all over the world today. Every morning, Hachiko walked with his owner to the train station. His owner went to work, and Hachiko went home. Then, every afternoon, Hachiko walked back to the station and waited for his owner to come home. He did this every single day.

One sad day, his owner did not come home, and he never came home again. But Hachiko did not understand that. He went back to the station the next day, and the day after that, and the day after that. He waited at the same place, at the same time, for many years, hoping his owner would step off the train. People at the station began to notice him. They brought him food, they petted him, and they told his story to others. Hachiko became famous all over Japan for his loyalty.

Today, there is a statue of Hachiko at the train station where he waited. People visit it from all over the world. They take pictures, they leave flowers, and they remember a dog who loved his owner so much that he never stopped waiting. Hachiko shows us what loyalty really means, and he is the reason so many people think of the Akita as the most loyal dog in the world.

## What Does an Akita Look Like?

Akitas are big, strong dogs with a thick double coat. Their fur can be many colours, including white, red, brown and brindle, which is a mix of dark and light stripes. Some Akitas have a white face with darker markings, and some are all white. Their ears stand up straight and point forward, which gives them an alert and watchful look. Their tails curl up over their backs, like a fluffy ring.

An Akita has a broad head, deep eyes and a strong body. It looks powerful, but it moves with a calm and quiet grace.

## A Quiet and Gentle Friend

Akitas are not noisy dogs. They do not bark a lot, and they often like quiet and calm. They are very clean dogs, too.

With its own family, an Akita is gentle, loving and playful. It loves to be close to the people it trusts, and it often follows them around the house. It may lean against its owner, rest its big head on their lap, or sit quietly beside them. Akitas are not dogs that jump up and down with excitement all the time. Their love is quiet and deep, like a warm blanket on a cold day.

Akitas can be shy around strangers. They do not usually run up to new people for a cuddle. Instead, they watch and wait, and they decide for themselves if the new person is a friend. That is part of what makes them such good guard dogs. They are brave and they will protect their home and family if they need to. For this reason, Akitas need an owner who understands them and who can help them meet new people in a calm and friendly way.

## Akitas and Children

Akitas can be wonderful with children in their own family, especially when they grow up together. They are patient and protective, and they often watch over children as if they were their own puppies. But Akitas are big and strong, so children should always be gentle with them, and a grown-up should always be nearby when young children play with any large dog. Akitas also need plenty of space and exercise. They love long walks, games and time outdoors, but they also love a quiet rest at home after a good walk.

Because Akitas are so loyal, they do not like to be left alone for a very long time. They want to be with their family.

## A Dog to Remember

The Akita is more than just a beautiful dog. It is a dog with a big heart and a brave spirit. It has been loved in Japan for hundreds of years, and today it is loved all over the world. People love the Akita for its strength, its calm nature, and most of all, its loyalty.

Hachiko waited at the train station for many years, and his story still touches the hearts of people everywhere. It reminds us that a dog's love is one of the strongest things in the world. An Akita may be quiet, it may be proud, and it may take time to trust a stranger, but once it loves you, it loves you for ever.

So, if you ever meet an Akita, look at its proud face, its pointed ears and its curled tail, and remember the story of Hachiko. You will be looking at one of the most loyal dogs on earth, a true friend from Japan, with a heart as big as the mountains where it came from.
