The Sahara is one of the hottest and driest places on Earth. During the day, the sand can feel like the inside of a hot oven, and rain may not fall for a very long time. Most animals would find it far too hard to live there. But one amazing animal calls this great desert home: the addax antelope.

The addax is a true desert survivor. It has clever ways of finding food and water in a place where both are very hard to come by. It can go for a long time without drinking, and it knows how to rest when the sun is at its fiercest. Let us meet this wonderful desert animal and find out how it manages to live where almost nothing else can.

## What Do They Look Like?

Addax antelopes are easy to spot once you know what to look for. They have a pale, sandy-white coat that helps them blend in with the desert sand. In the hot summer months, their coat turns almost white, which helps to reflect the strong sunlight. In the cooler months, their coat becomes a little darker and more grey-brown.

An addax has a dark patch of hair on its forehead, a bit like a little tuft, and a short dark beard under its chin. Its face often has white markings that look a little bit like a mask. Both male and female addax have long horns that twist upwards in a spiral shape, a bit like a corkscrew. These horns can grow very long, and they make the addax look quite grand.

Addax are sturdy animals with strong legs and wide, flat hooves. Their wide hooves work a bit like snowshoes, spreading their weight so they do not sink into the soft desert sand as they walk. This helps them move across the dunes without getting stuck. They are about as tall as a big dog at the shoulder, and they have a calm, gentle way of moving.

## Where Do They Live?

Addax live in the Sahara Desert, which stretches across the northern part of Africa. This is the largest hot desert in the world, full of sand dunes, rocky plains, and wide open spaces. It is a tough place to live, with very hot days, cold nights, and almost no rain.

In the past, addax roamed across a huge part of the Sahara. Today, they are much rarer, and most of the addax left in the wild live in a small area in the country of Niger. People who care about wildlife are working hard to protect them, and some addax also live in safe wildlife parks and reserves around the world, where they can be looked after and kept safe.

Addax are always on the move. They wander across the desert looking for food, sometimes travelling a long way to find fresh plants to eat. They often rest during the hottest part of the day, lying down in the shade of a dune or a small bush. They are most active in the cooler hours of the morning and evening, and sometimes they move around at night too.

## What Do They Eat?

Addax are herbivores, which means they eat only plants. In the desert, plants are not easy to find, but the addax knows just where to look. They munch on dry grasses, the leaves of small desert shrubs, and any green shoots they can find after the rare desert rains.

One of their cleverest tricks is that they get most of the water they need from the plants they eat. Desert plants often hold a little moisture inside them, and the addax can make good use of every drop. This means they can go for a very long time without finding a pool of water to drink from. When they do find water, they will drink, but they do not need it every day the way many other animals do.

Addax also eat at night sometimes, when the desert plants are cooler and hold a little more moisture from the night air. Eating in the cool of the night is another smart desert trick that helps them save water.

## How Do They Stay Cool in the Desert?

Living in the Sahara means dealing with very hot sunshine. The addax has some wonderful ways of coping. Its pale coat reflects the sun, a bit like wearing a white shirt on a sunny day. When the sun is at its hottest, the addax rests in the shade and waits for the cooler hours.

Addax can also let their body temperature rise a little during the hot day, which means they lose less water through sweating. Then, when the night comes and the desert cools down, their body temperature drops again. This is a clever way to save water, and it helps them survive in a place where water is so precious.

Their wide hooves help them walk on soft sand, and their strong legs can carry them a long way across the dunes. They are patient, steady walkers, and they do not rush around in the heat. Everything about the addax is built for desert life.


## Fun Facts

Here are some fun facts about addax antelopes:

- The addax is sometimes called the white antelope because of its pale coat.
- Its spiral horns twist like a corkscrew and can grow very long.
- Addax can live for a long time without drinking water, getting most of their moisture from plants.
- Their wide, flat hooves help them walk on soft sand without sinking.
- Addax are most active in the cool morning and evening, and they rest during the hottest part of the day.
- In the wild, addax are very rare today, which is why people are working hard to protect them.
- The addax is one of the few antelopes that can live in the deep desert, far from any water.
