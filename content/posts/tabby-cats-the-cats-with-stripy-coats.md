Have you ever seen a cat with stripes all over its coat, like a tiny tiger? That cat is a tabby. Tabby cats are some of the most loved pet cats in the whole world, and once you meet one, you will see why. They are friendly, playful, and full of character.

Here is a fun fact to start with: a tabby is not a breed of cat. Tabby is a coat pattern, which means it is the way the fur looks. Cats of many different breeds can be tabbies, and so can ordinary pet cats. What makes a cat a tabby is its stripes, swirls, or spots, and the little letter "M" on its forehead. If you look closely at a tabby cat, you will see that M, right between its ears. Isn't that amazing?

## The Little M on the Forehead

Almost every tabby cat has a dark "M" shape on its forehead. It is one of the easiest ways to tell a tabby from other cats. People have told stories about this M for hundreds of years. Some old tales say it is a special mark given to tabby cats long ago. Whatever the story, the M is real, and you can see it on tabbies all over the world.

Tabby cats also have stripes on their cheeks and around their eyes, like lines of eyeliner. They often have stripes on their legs and tail too, and rings around their neck like a little necklace. Some tabbies have stripes all over, while others have swirls on their sides that look like marble cake.

## Four Kinds of Tabby Patterns

Tabby cats come in four main patterns, and each one is beautiful in its own way. The first is the classic tabby, which has big swirls on its sides. The swirls look like the patterns on a marble cake or a seashell. Classic tabbies are very striking, and their swirls are easy to see.

The second pattern is the mackerel tabby. This cat has thin stripes running down its sides, like the bones of a fish. That is how it got its name, because a mackerel is a fish. Mackerel tabbies look a bit like little tigers, with neat stripes from head to tail.

The third pattern is the spotted tabby. Instead of stripes, this cat has spots all over its body. The spots can be big or small, and they make the cat look a little like a tiny leopard. Spotted tabbies are less common, so they always get a second look.

The fourth pattern is the ticked tabby. This cat looks plain from far away, but up close each hair has bands of light and dark colour, like tiny stripes on every hair. Ticked tabbies often have a plain-looking coat with stripes only on their face, legs, and tail. Each pattern is lovely, and tabby lovers all have a favourite.

## Colours of the Rainbow

Tabby cats come in many colours. The most common is the brown tabby, with dark stripes on warm brown fur. But tabbies can also be grey, with soft silver stripes, or orange, with bright ginger stripes. Orange tabbies are often called ginger tabbies, and they are famous for being chatty and friendly.

Some tabbies have white patches on their chest, belly, or paws. A tabby with white paws looks like it is wearing little socks. Some tabbies are almost all white with just a few stripes on their head and tail.

## Playful, Chatty and Loving

Tabby cats are known for being friendly and full of fun. They love to play with toys, chase balls, and pounce on anything that moves. A tabby kitten can play for a very long time, jumping and tumbling and chasing its own tail. Even grown-up tabbies love a good game.

Many tabbies are also very chatty. They meow to say hello, to ask for food, or just to tell you about their day. Some tabbies will follow their owners from room to room, chatting all the way. They like to be where the people are, and they often curl up on a lap for a cuddle and a purr.

Tabbies are clever cats too. They can learn where their food is kept, how to open a door, or how to find the sunniest spot in the house. They are curious and love to explore. If you leave a box on the floor, a tabby will climb inside it within moments.

## Taking Care of a Tabby Cat

Tabby cats make wonderful pets, but they need care and love every day. They need fresh food and clean water, a cosy bed, and a clean place to use as a toilet. They also need someone to play with them and brush their fur to keep it soft and shiny. Short-haired tabbies need only a little brushing, but long-haired tabbies need more.

Tabbies need to stay safe too. Pet cats should live indoors or in a safe garden, away from busy roads. They need visits to the animal doctor to keep them healthy, just like children visit the doctor. A healthy tabby that is loved and cared for is a happy tabby.

If you have a tabby cat, you have a friend for life. Tabbies are loyal, loving, and always ready for a game or a cuddle. They will greet you at the door, sleep at the end of your bed, and purr you to sleep at night. There is nothing quite like the sound of a happy tabby purring.

So next time you see a cat with stripes and a little M on its forehead, say hello. It is a tabby, the cat with the stripy coat, and it might just be the friendliest cat you will ever meet.
