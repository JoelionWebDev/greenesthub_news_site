## What is a parrot?

A parrot is a bright, clever bird with a strong curved beak and a long tail. Parrots are famous for their beautiful colours. Some are green, some are red, some are blue, and some are a mix of many colours at once. Their feathers look like a rainbow, and they make parrots one of the most eye-catching birds in the world.

Parrots have strong feet with two toes pointing forward and two toes pointing backward. This special shape helps them grip branches and hold their food, a bit like hands. A parrot can pick up a piece of fruit with its foot and bring it to its beak to eat, just like you might pick up a snack with your hand.

A parrot's beak is big and curved, and it is very strong. Parrots use their beaks to crack open hard nuts and seeds, to climb, and to explore new things. Parrots are very clever birds. They can solve puzzles, learn tricks, and remember the people and places they know.

## Where do parrots live?

Wild parrots live in warm places around the world. Many parrots come from rainforests in South America, Africa, Asia and Australia. In the rainforest, parrots live high up in the trees, where they can find fruit, nuts and seeds. They fly in noisy flocks, and their bright colours flash through the green leaves.

Pet parrots live in people's homes. They live in a large cage or an aviary, which is a big enclosed space where birds can fly and play. A pet parrot also needs time outside its cage every day, so it can stretch its wings, explore and spend time with its family. Parrots are social birds, and a pet parrot can become lonely if it is left alone for too long.

## What do parrots eat?

Parrots eat many different foods. In the wild, they eat fruit, nuts, seeds, berries, flowers and leaves. They are very good at finding the ripest fruit in the trees, and they use their strong beaks to crack open nuts that other animals cannot open. Pet parrots also eat special parrot pellets, fresh fruit and vegetables.

Parrots need fresh water every day for drinking and for bathing. Many parrots love to splash in a shallow bowl of water.

## How do parrots talk?

Parrots are famous for copying human speech, and that is one of the reasons people love them. Parrots do not really understand words the way people do, but they are very good at copying sounds. They listen carefully to the people around them and repeat the words they hear again and again.

Some parrots can learn many words, and some can even put words together in short sentences. The African grey parrot is one of the best talkers of all. African greys can learn a large number of words and use them at the right time, like saying "hello" when someone comes into the room.

Parrots also copy other sounds. They can copy the sound of a phone ringing, a doorbell, a dog barking, or even laughter. A parrot that lives in a busy home may surprise its family by copying the sound of the microwave or the voice of a favourite person.

## Parrot families

Parrots are caring parents. A female parrot usually lays her eggs in a hole in a tree, and both parents take turns keeping the eggs warm. When the chicks hatch, they are tiny, pink and have no feathers. They cannot see yet, and they depend on their parents for everything.

The parent parrots feed their chicks by bringing food back to the nest. The chicks grow quickly, and soon they are covered in soft feathers. After a while, the young parrots learn to fly and to find food for themselves, but they stay near their parents while they are learning.

Parrots often stay with the same partner for a long time. They groom each other's feathers, share food and sit close together. This close bond helps them raise their chicks together and look after each other in the wild.

## Parrots as pets

Parrots make wonderful pets, but they need a lot of care and attention. A pet parrot is not like a fish that you can just watch. Parrots are clever, social and active, and they need their owners to spend time with them every day.

A pet parrot needs a big cage with room to move, toys to play with, and things to chew. Parrots love to chew wood and to solve puzzles, because their minds need exercise just like their bodies do. Owners often give their parrots new toys to keep them happy and busy.

Parrots can live for a very long time. Some small parrots live for many years, and some large parrots can live for decades. This means that getting a parrot is a big promise, and a family that brings home a parrot should be ready to look after it for a very long time.

## Parrots and people

People and parrots have been friends for a very long time. Long ago, sailors and traders brought parrots home from faraway lands, and people were amazed by their bright feathers and their ability to copy words.

In the wild, many parrots need our help. Some parrots are losing their homes because rainforests are being cut down, and some are caught to be sold as pets. People who love parrots work hard to protect them, by saving their forest homes and by making sure pet parrots are treated well.

If you ever meet a pet parrot, speak to it gently and move slowly. Parrots can be shy at first, but with patience and kindness, they often become friendly and chatty.

## Fun facts about parrots

- Some parrots can learn many words and copy human speech very well.
- The African grey parrot is one of the best talkers in the bird world.
- Parrots use their feet like hands to hold their food.
- A parrot's beak is strong enough to crack open a hard nut.
- Parrots can live for a very long time, and some large parrots live for decades.
- Wild parrots often fly in noisy flocks through the rainforest.
- Parrots groom each other as a sign of friendship and love.
- Some parrots can copy sounds like a phone ringing or a dog barking.
- Parrots have very good memories and can remember people and places.
- A group of parrots is called a flock, and a young parrot is called a chick.
