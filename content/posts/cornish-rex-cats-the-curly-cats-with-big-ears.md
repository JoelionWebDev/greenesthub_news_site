Imagine a cat with a coat like soft velvet waves, enormous ears, a slender body and legs like a ballet dancer. That is the Cornish rex. These unusual cats come from Cornwall, a beautiful county in the south-west of England, and they are some of the friendliest, silliest and most playful cats in the whole world. If you have ever wanted a cat that behaves a little bit like a dog, the Cornish rex might be the cat for you.

## A Curly Kitten in Cornwall

The story of the Cornish rex begins in 1950, on a farm in Cornwall. A farm cat had a litter of kittens, and one of them looked very different from the rest. While his brothers and sisters had ordinary fur, this little kitten had a soft, wavy, curly coat. The farmer's family named him Kallibunker, and they quickly realised he was something special.

People who knew about cats were very excited. They had never seen a cat with a curly coat before. Soon, Kallibunker became the father of a whole new breed of cats. Because he came from Cornwall, the new breed was called the Cornish rex. Rex is a word that is often used for animals with curly coats, and it suited these cats perfectly.

From that one curly kitten, Cornish rex cats spread all over the world. Today, they are loved by cat fans everywhere, and they still carry the same curly coats and big ears as their famous ancestor.

## What Makes Their Coat So Special?

Most cats have three layers of fur: a soft undercoat, a middle layer, and longer guard hairs on top. Cornish rex cats are different. They have only the soft undercoat, and it grows in tight little waves and curls. That is why their fur looks wavy and feels like soft velvet or crushed silk when you stroke it.

Because their coats are so fine, Cornish rex cats can look a little bit naked in places, especially on their tummies. But do not worry, they are not cold all the time. They love warm places, sunny windowsills, cosy blankets and, best of all, warm laps. If you have a Cornish rex, you will soon find it curled up under the covers with you or sitting on the warmest spot in the house.

Their coats come in many colours and patterns. Some are black, some are white, some are ginger, and some have beautiful patterns like tabby stripes or patches of different colours. Whatever the colour, every Cornish rex has that same soft, curly coat.

## Big Ears and a Clever Face

One of the first things you notice about a Cornish rex is its ears. They are very large, tall and pointed, and they sit high on its head like two little satellite dishes. Those big ears help the cat hear very well, and they give it a funny, alert expression, as if it is always listening for something interesting.

The Cornish rex has a small, egg-shaped head, high cheekbones, and big oval eyes that seem to look right into your soul. Its body is long, slim and elegant, with long legs and a long, whippy tail. When a Cornish rex runs, it looks like a tiny greyhound, fast and graceful. When it jumps, it can leap to surprising heights, sometimes landing on top of a bookshelf or a wardrobe before you have even blinked.

## Playful Like a Dog

Cornish rex cats are famous for acting like dogs. They follow their owners from room to room, greet them at the door when they come home, and love to be involved in everything. They are not the kind of cat that hides under the bed all day. They want to be where the action is.

They are very playful, even as adults. A Cornish rex will chase a toy mouse, pounce on a feather wand, and bat a ball around the house for hours. Many of them learn to play fetch, bringing a toy back to their owner again and again, just like a dog. Some even learn to walk on a lead, trot alongside their owners, and come when they are called.

They are also very clever. Cornish rex cats can learn to open doors, work out puzzle toys, and figure out where the treats are hidden. Owners need to be creative to keep them entertained, because a bored Cornish rex will find its own fun, which might mean opening a cupboard or knocking things off a shelf.

## A Loving Lap Cat

For all their energy and mischief, Cornish rex cats are deeply loving. They adore people and love to cuddle. They will curl up on your lap, tuck themselves under your chin, and purr loudly. Their purr is a happy, rumbling sound that tells you they are content.

They get along well with children, other cats and even friendly dogs, because they are social and easy-going. They do not like being left alone for long periods, so they are happiest in homes where someone is around most of the time. If you go out for the day, a Cornish rex will be waiting by the door when you return, ready for a cuddle and a game.

Because their coats are so fine, Cornish rex cats need gentle grooming. A soft brush once a week is enough to keep their curls looking lovely. They also need good food, fresh water, regular vet visits, and a warm, safe home. In return, they give their families years of fun, affection and companionship.

## Why We Love Them

Cornish rex cats remind us that being different is beautiful. Their curly coats, big ears and slender bodies make them stand out from other cats, but it is their personalities that people love most. They are playful, clever, loyal and loving, all wrapped up in a velvety little package.

So if you ever meet a Cornish rex, give it a gentle stroke and feel that amazing curly coat. Look into those big eyes and you will see a cat that is ready to play, ready to cuddle, and ready to be your best friend. That is the magic of the Cornish rex, the curly cat with big ears from Cornwall.
