The Pekingese is a small dog with a very big history. Long ago, in China, these little dogs lived in the emperor's palace and were treated like royalty. They were not allowed to leave the palace, and ordinary people were not allowed to own one. Today, anyone can have a Pekingese, but these dogs still act like they own the place. With their long, flowing coats and their lion-like faces, they are one of the most charming little dogs in the world.

## The Dogs of the Emperor

Pekingese dogs come from China, where they were kept by emperors for hundreds of years. In the Forbidden City in Beijing, the emperor's palace, Pekingese dogs lived in luxury. They had their own servants to look after them, and they were carried around in the sleeves of royal robes. Some people even believed that Pekingese dogs were sacred, and that they carried the spirits of the emperors' ancestors.

Because they were so precious, it was against the law for ordinary people to own a Pekingese. Anyone caught stealing one could be punished very severely. The dogs were so closely guarded that for a long time, nobody outside China had ever seen one.

That changed in 1860, when British soldiers entered the emperor's summer palace and found five Pekingese dogs. The dogs were brought to England, and one was given to Queen Victoria herself. From there, Pekingese dogs spread around the world, and they became one of the most popular toy breeds of all.

## A Little Lion

The Pekingese is often called a lion dog, and you can see why. It has a long, thick mane of fur around its neck and shoulders, like a lion's mane. Its face is flat, with a short nose, big dark eyes and ears that hang down. Its body is low and long, with short legs, and its tail curls up over its back in a plume of fur.

Pekingese dogs come in many colours, including gold, red, black, white, cream and grey. Their coats are long and straight, and they need regular brushing to keep them looking beautiful. When a Pekingese walks, it has a special rolling walk, because its front legs are bowed. It looks a bit like a little lion strolling proudly along.

Even though they are small, Pekingese are sturdy dogs. They are not fragile like some toy breeds. They were bred to be companions, and they are happy to sit on a lap, but they also enjoy a short walk and a play in the garden.

## A Big Personality in a Small Dog

Pekingese dogs have big personalities. They are brave, proud and independent. They are not like some small dogs that follow their owners everywhere and want constant attention. A Pekingese likes affection, but on its own terms. It will come to you when it wants a cuddle, and it will go off to its favourite spot when it wants some peace and quiet.

This independence can make Pekingese a little stubborn. They are clever dogs, but they do not always do what they are told straight away. They need patient, gentle training, with lots of praise and treats. Once a Pekingese trusts you, it is loyal and loving, and it will be your devoted friend for life.

Pekingese are also good watchdogs. Even though they are small, they have a loud bark and they are not afraid to use it if they hear something strange. They will alert you to visitors and to anything unusual. In a way, they still think they are guarding a palace.

## Living with a Pekingese

Pekingese dogs are good pets for families with older children, and for people who live in flats or small houses. They do not need a lot of exercise — a couple of short walks a day and some playtime indoors is usually enough. They are happy in a small space, as long as they have a comfortable bed and someone to love.

Their long coats need regular care. A Pekingese should be brushed several times a week to stop its fur from getting tangled. Some owners keep their dogs' coats trimmed shorter, which is easier to look after and keeps the dog cooler in hot weather. Pekingese can get too hot in summer, because their flat faces make it harder for them to breathe and cool down. On hot days, they need shade, water and a cool place to rest.

Pekingese dogs also need their faces cleaned regularly. The folds around their flat noses can collect dirt, so a gentle wipe with a damp cloth keeps them clean and healthy. Their eyes are large and need to be checked too, to make sure they are clear and bright.

## A Friend for Life

Pekingese dogs can live for a long time, and they form a strong bond with their owners. They are happiest when they are part of the family, sitting near you while you read or watch television, or curled up on your lap. They are not dogs that like to be left alone all day, so they suit homes where someone is around most of the time.

Because they are so proud and independent, Pekingese are best for owners who understand them. They are not dogs that will obey every command like a soldier. They are more like little emperors — they expect respect, and in return, they give you loyalty, charm and love.
