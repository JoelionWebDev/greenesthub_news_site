Mollies are peaceful, colourful little fish that are perfect for a home aquarium. They come in many colours and they love to swim together. If you have ever looked into a fish tank and seen a group of bright, friendly fish swimming in a happy school, they may well have been mollies. They are one of the most popular pet fish in the world, and it is easy to see why.

## Little Fish from Warm Waters

Mollies come from warm waters in Central America and Mexico, where they live in rivers, streams and coastal waters. In the wild, they swim in slow-moving water, near plants and among the roots of trees that grow along the riverbank. They are used to warm, sunny water, and they need the same in a home aquarium.

There are many kinds of mollies, and they come in a rainbow of colours. Some are black, some are orange, some are silver, and some are a mix of colours. The black molly is one of the most famous, with its deep, velvety black body. The orange molly is bright and cheerful, like a little flame swimming in the water.

Mollies are small fish, usually about the size of a child's thumb. They have round bodies, flowing fins and bright eyes. The males are usually smaller and more colourful than the females, and they have a special fin that helps them swim quickly.

## Peaceful Fish That Love Company

Mollies are peaceful fish, which means they get along well with other fish. They are not aggressive, and they do not chase or bully their tank mates. This makes them wonderful fish for a community tank, where many different kinds of peaceful fish live together.

They are also social fish, which means they love company. Mollies are happiest when they live in a group with other mollies. A single molly kept alone can become lonely and sad, so it is best to keep at least three or four together. When they swim in a group, they look beautiful, moving through the water like a little school of bright colours.

Mollies are active fish, and they love to swim around their tank, exploring plants, hiding places and decorations. They are curious, and they will investigate anything new in their tank. Watching mollies swim is relaxing and fun, and many people keep fish tanks just to enjoy watching them.

## What Mollies Eat

Mollies are not fussy eaters. In the wild, they eat algae, which is the green stuff that grows on rocks and plants in the water, as well as small insects and tiny water creatures. In a home aquarium, they eat fish flakes, small pellets and vegetables such as blanched peas and courgette.

They also love to nibble algae that grows in the tank, which helps keep the tank clean. This is one of the reasons people like mollies — they are little helpers that keep the aquarium tidy.

Mollies need to be fed small amounts once or twice a day. They should not be overfed, because leftover food can dirty the water. A little food, given regularly, is the best way to keep mollies healthy and happy.

## Baby Mollies

One of the most interesting things about mollies is how they have their babies. Unlike many fish that lay eggs, mollies give birth to live babies, called fry. The mother molly carries the babies inside her body until they are ready to swim, and then she gives birth to a group of tiny, fully formed fish.

Baby mollies are very small, but they can swim straight away. They hide among plants and decorations in the tank, where they are safe from bigger fish. They grow quickly, eating tiny pieces of food and growing bigger every day. Within a few months, they are almost as big as their parents.

If you keep mollies, you may find baby mollies in your tank one day, which is a lovely surprise. It is important to give them plenty of plants to hide in, so they can stay safe while they grow.

## Looking After Mollies

Mollies need a clean tank with warm water, a filter to keep the water clean, and plants and decorations to hide in and explore. They need space to swim, so a tank that is too small is not good for them. They also need a lid on the tank, because mollies can sometimes jump out of the water.

The water in a molly tank should be changed regularly, and the tank should be cleaned to keep it healthy. Mollies are hardy fish, which means they are quite tough and can live in a range of conditions, but they still need clean water and good food to stay healthy.

Mollies can live for several years if they are well cared for, which means they can be a friend for a long time. They are a good first fish for children who are learning to look after pets, because they are friendly, hardy and fun to watch.

## A Fish for Everyone

Mollies are a wonderful example of how a small, simple fish can bring a lot of joy. They are colourful, peaceful and friendly, and they turn an ordinary fish tank into a little underwater world full of life and movement.

If you ever see a group of mollies swimming together, watch how they move in and out of the plants, nibbling algae and chasing each other playfully. They are a reminder that even the smallest pets can bring big happiness, and that a little tank can hold a whole world of wonder.
