Some cats are loud, bouncy, and always in the middle of everything. Russian Blue cats are not like that at all. They are quiet, gentle, and a little shy — but once they trust you, they are loyal friends for life. With their beautiful silver-blue fur and bright green eyes, they are one of the prettiest cats in the world.

Russian Blues are calm, clever cats that love a peaceful home. They enjoy sitting near their favourite person, playing quietly, and watching the world go by from a sunny window. Let us meet this lovely, quiet cat.

## What Do They Look Like?

Russian Blue cats are medium-sized cats with a slim, graceful body. Their most famous feature is their coat — a soft, thick fur that is a beautiful blue-grey colour, with silvery tips that seem to shimmer in the light. When you stroke a Russian Blue, its fur feels like soft velvet.

They have bright green eyes that stand out against their silver-grey fur. Their eyes are large and round, and they give the cat a gentle, thoughtful look. Russian Blues have long, pointed ears, a long tail, and neat, dainty paws.

Their fur is double-coated, which means they have a soft undercoat and a longer top coat. This thick fur keeps them warm and makes them look extra fluffy and soft. They are elegant, graceful cats, and they move with a quiet, smooth way of walking.

## Where Do They Come From?

Russian Blue cats come from Russia, a large, cold country in the north of Europe and Asia. Long ago, they lived in the port city of Arkhangelsk, in the north of Russia, where the winters are very cold and snowy. Their thick, warm fur helped them to stay cosy in the cold weather.

Sailors are said to have taken Russian Blue cats on their ships long ago, and the cats travelled to many different countries. Today, Russian Blues live as beloved pets in homes all over the world. They are happiest indoors, in a warm, quiet home where they feel safe.

Russian Blues like a calm, peaceful place to live. They are not fans of loud noises or big, busy crowds. They prefer a home where they can have their own quiet corners, a sunny spot by the window, and a soft bed to curl up in.

## What Do They Eat?

Like all cats, Russian Blues are carnivores, which means they eat meat. At home, they are usually fed cat food, which is specially made to give them all the goodness they need. They eat both wet food, which comes in tins or pouches, and dry food, which comes as little crunchy biscuits.

Russian Blues also like the occasional treat, like a small piece of cooked chicken or fish. But they should not eat too many treats, and they should never eat chocolate, onions, or other foods that are bad for cats. Fresh, clean water is very important, and they should always have a bowl of water to drink from.

Russian Blues are not greedy eaters. They tend to eat slowly and neatly, and they like to have their food in the same place every day. They are tidy cats, and they like their routines!

## A Quiet, Loyal Friend

Russian Blues are known for being shy with strangers, but very loyal to their own family. They may hide when visitors come to the house, but with the people they love, they are affectionate and gentle. They often choose one favourite person in the family and follow them around the house, sitting nearby while they read, work, or watch television.

They are quiet cats, and they do not meow very much. When they do speak, their voice is soft and gentle. They are happy to sit on your lap for a cuddle, but they also like their own space, and they will go off to a quiet corner when they want some alone time.

Russian Blues are very clever cats. They can learn to open doors, find hidden treats, and even play fetch with a small toy! They love puzzle toys and games that make them think. They are playful too, especially when they are young, and they enjoy chasing a toy mouse or a ball around the house.

## Family Life at Home

Russian Blue kittens are small, fluffy, and very cute. When they are born, their eyes are blue, and as they grow older, their eyes slowly change to the bright green that adult Russian Blues are famous for. Kittens love to play, climb, and explore, and they learn a lot from their mother in the first few weeks of their lives.

Russian Blues make wonderful family pets, especially in calm, quiet homes. They are gentle with children who treat them kindly, and they are happy to play with older children who know how to be gentle with a cat. They do best in homes where they are given love, patience, and plenty of time to get to know their family.

They are clean cats, and they spend a lot of time grooming themselves, licking their fur to keep it soft and tidy. They are also easy to look after, because their short, thick fur does not need a lot of brushing — just a gentle brush now and then to keep it looking lovely.
