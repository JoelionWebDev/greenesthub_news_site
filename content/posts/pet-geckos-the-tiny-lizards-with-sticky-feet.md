Have you ever seen a tiny lizard walking upside down on a ceiling? That was probably a gecko! Geckos are small lizards with big eyes, soft skin, and the most amazing feet in the animal world. Their toes are covered in tiny hairs that help them stick to walls, windows, and even ceilings. Some geckos are bright green, some are spotty, and some are a soft sandy colour. Pet geckos are quiet, gentle, and fun to watch.

## What is a gecko?

A gecko is a kind of lizard, and lizards are reptiles. That means a gecko has scaly skin, lays eggs, and likes to feel warm. Geckos come in many colours and patterns. Most geckos are quite small, small enough to sit in the palm of your hand, although some kinds grow bigger. A gecko has a long tail, four legs, and a wide head with large eyes. Unlike many other lizards, most geckos do not have eyelids that blink. Instead, they lick their eyes clean with their long tongues! Geckos are not slimy at all. Their skin feels dry and soft, a bit like smooth paper.

## Where do geckos live?

Wild geckos live in warm places all around the world. You can find them in deserts, forests, and rocky hills. Many geckos live in warm countries in Africa, Asia, and Australia, and some live in the Americas too. Some geckos live in trees, hiding among the leaves during the day and coming out at night. Others live on rocks or on the ground, hiding under stones. A lot of geckos like to live near people, because houses are warm and full of tiny insects to eat. Pet geckos live indoors in a special home called a terrarium. A terrarium is like a small glass house with warm lights, places to hide, and things to climb on.

## What do geckos eat?

Geckos are hunters, and they love to eat small insects. In the wild, a gecko will sit very still and wait for a tiny insect to wander past. Then, quick as a flash, it snaps the insect up with its mouth. Geckos eat crickets, small moths, flies, and other little insects. Pet geckos eat the same kind of food. Their owners give them small insects that are bought from a pet shop. Geckos do not eat plants or fruit very often. They are mostly insect eaters, and they need fresh water to drink too. A pet gecko has a small dish of clean water in its home. A gecko does not need to eat every single day. Grown-up geckos often eat every few days, while young geckos eat more often because they are growing.

## A day in a gecko's life

Most pet geckos are most active in the evening and at night. During the day, they like to rest in a warm, dark hiding place. They might sleep curled up under a log, inside a little cave, or behind a leaf. When evening comes, they wake up and start to explore. They climb up the walls of their home, walk along branches, and look for food. Their sticky toes help them walk up smooth glass and hang upside down without falling. Some geckos make little sounds too. They may chirp, squeak, or click to talk to other geckos. A gecko sheds its skin as it grows. The old skin peels off, and fresh new skin appears underneath.

## Baby geckos

Baby geckos hatch from small, soft eggs. The mother gecko lays her eggs in a warm, safe place, often hidden under a rock or in soft soil. The baby gecko grows inside the egg until it is ready to hatch. Baby geckos are very small, but they look like tiny copies of their parents. They can walk, climb, and hunt for tiny insects almost straight away. A baby gecko grows quickly, and it sheds its skin many times as it gets bigger. Young geckos need a warm home, fresh water, and small insects to eat. With good care, a baby gecko grows into a healthy adult gecko, and pet geckos can live for many years.

## Looking after a pet gecko

Geckos can be lovely pets, but they need a grown-up to help look after them. A pet gecko needs a warm, clean home with places to hide and climb. It needs fresh water every day and the right insects to eat. Its home should be cleaned often so it stays healthy. A gecko is a gentle animal, but it is also delicate. Its tail can drop off if it feels scared, so you should never grab a gecko by its tail. A new tail can grow back, but it is better to be gentle and calm. Always move slowly and let the gecko get used to you. Wash your hands before and after holding a pet gecko, and always ask a grown-up for help. A gecko is not a toy, and it needs quiet time to rest.

## Fun facts about geckos

- A gecko can walk upside down on a ceiling because its toes are covered in tiny hairs that grip smooth surfaces.
- Most geckos do not blink, so they lick their eyes clean with their long tongues.
- Some geckos can make chirping and clicking sounds to talk to each other.
- A gecko can drop its tail if it feels scared, and a new tail can grow back.
- Geckos shed their skin as they grow, and fresh new skin appears underneath.
- Many geckos are most active in the evening and at night.

Pet geckos are small, quiet, and full of surprises. From their sticky feet to their big bright eyes, they are some of the most amazing little lizards in the world. They teach us to be gentle, patient, and kind to small animals. Next time you see a tiny lizard on a wall, take a quiet look. It might be a gecko, waiting for its dinner, with its wonderful sticky toes holding on tight.
