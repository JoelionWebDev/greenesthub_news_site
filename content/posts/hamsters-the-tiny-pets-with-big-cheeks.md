Hamsters are some of the smallest pets you can keep, but they are full of big fun. They are tiny, fluffy and quick, with bright eyes, round ears and a very short tail. A hamster can fit in the palm of your hand, and it loves to run, climb, dig and explore.

## What is a hamster?

A hamster is a small rodent with a short, round body, soft fur and tiny paws. Hamsters belong to the same family as mice and rats, but they are their own special kind of animal. There are different kinds of hamsters, and some are bigger than others. The Syrian hamster is one of the biggest pet hamsters, while dwarf hamsters are much smaller and quicker.

Hamsters come in many colours. Some are golden, some are white, some are grey, and some have spots or stripes. Their fur is soft and thick, and their ears are small and round. A hamster's eyes are dark and shiny, and they always seem to be watching everything.

## The famous cheek pouches

The most famous thing about a hamster is its big cheeks. A hamster has special pockets inside its cheeks, called cheek pouches. When a hamster finds food, it stuffs it into these pouches until its cheeks puff out like two little balloons. A hamster with full cheeks looks very funny, and it is one of the cutest things you can see.

The cheek pouches are like shopping bags. A hamster fills them with seeds and food, then carries everything back to its nest to eat later or to store away. When the hamster gets home, it pushes the food out of its cheeks with its paws and hides it in a secret spot. In the wild, hamsters store food in their burrows, so they always have something to eat, even when food is hard to find.

## Night-time adventures

Hamsters are nocturnal, which means they sleep during the day and wake up at night. When you go to bed, your hamster is just getting ready for its busy evening. It runs on its wheel, climbs, digs and explores its cage. A hamster can run a very long way on its wheel in one night, and it does it because it loves to move.

During the day, a hamster sleeps curled up in its nest, tucked inside soft bedding. It likes a quiet, dark corner where it feels safe. If you wake a hamster up suddenly, it can get frightened, so it is best to let it sleep and play with it in the evening, when it is wide awake and ready for fun. A happy hamster at night is a busy, bouncy little ball of energy.

## What do hamsters eat?

Hamsters eat seeds, grains, nuts, fruits and vegetables. A good hamster food mix has seeds and grains in it, and you can also give your hamster small pieces of fresh food, like carrot, apple or cucumber. Hamsters love sunflower seeds, but those are fatty, so they should only be a small treat, not the main meal.

Hamsters also need clean, fresh water every day, in a bottle or a small bowl. They eat little and often, and they like to carry food to their nest and hide it there. If you look inside a hamster's nest, you may find a little store of seeds, neatly tucked away. That is your hamster's secret food cupboard, and it is a very clever habit from life in the wild.

## A hamster's cosy home

A hamster needs a home that is safe, roomy and fun. Its cage should be big enough for it to run, climb and dig. It needs a wheel to run on, a little house to sleep in, tunnels to explore, and soft bedding to burrow into. Hamsters love to dig and burrow, so deep bedding is very important. It lets them make little tunnels and nests, just like they do in the wild.

A hamster's cage should be kept in a quiet place, away from loud noise and bright sun. The bedding should be clean and dry, and the cage should be cleaned often. A hamster also needs things to chew on, like a wooden toy or a safe chew stick, because its teeth never stop growing. Chewing keeps its teeth short and healthy.

## How hamsters play

Hamsters love to play, and they are very good at finding fun. They run on their wheels, climb up tubes, dig in their bedding and squeeze through tunnels. Some hamsters enjoy a small ball that they can roll around in on the floor, and some like to climb on a little wooden ladder. Play keeps a hamster fit and happy.

Hamsters are also clever. They learn where their food is kept, they learn the way through their tunnels, and they soon learn to know the person who feeds them. Some hamsters will climb onto your hand for a treat, and some will let you stroke them gently. Every hamster has its own character. Some are bold and curious, and some are shy and like to take their time.

## Looking after a hamster kindly

Hamsters are small and gentle, so they need kind and careful hands. Always be calm and quiet around your hamster. Never grab it suddenly, and never squeeze it. To pick a hamster up, let it walk onto your open hand, then lift it slowly and hold it close to your body. If it wants to get down, let it go back to its cage. Never wake a sleeping hamster up to play with it.

Give your hamster fresh food and water every day, and clean its cage often. Wash your hands before and after holding your hamster. Hamsters live for a few years, so keeping one is a promise to care for it every day. A hamster is not a toy. It is a tiny living friend that needs food, a clean home and gentle love.

## Fun facts about hamsters

- A hamster can stuff so much food into its cheek pouches that its cheeks puff out like balloons.
- Hamsters are nocturnal, which means they sleep during the day and wake up at night.
- A hamster's teeth never stop growing, so it must chew things to keep them short.
- In the wild, hamsters dig burrows underground and store food in secret rooms.
- Some hamsters run a very long way on their wheels every single night.
