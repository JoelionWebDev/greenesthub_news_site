If you have ever visited an aquarium and seen a round, flat fish with bright colours that looks like a jewel swimming in the water, you may have met a discus fish. Discus fish are some of the most beautiful fish in the world, and they are loved by aquarium keepers everywhere. They come in many colours, such as red, blue, green, and yellow, and they glide gracefully through the water like living rainbows. Let us dive into their watery world and discover what makes discus fish so special.

## What Is a Discus Fish?

A discus fish is a freshwater fish that comes from the Amazon River in South America. It gets its name from its round, disc-shaped body, which is flat on the sides and looks like a discus, the round object thrown in sports. Discus fish can grow to be quite large for aquarium fish, and they have long, flowing fins that make them look elegant. Their colours are amazing. Some are bright red, some are deep blue, and some have beautiful patterns of stripes and spots. Their colours can change a little depending on their mood, their health, and the light in the tank. Discus fish are peaceful and gentle, and they are known as the kings and queens of the aquarium.

## Where Discus Fish Come From

Discus fish come from the slow-moving rivers and flooded forests of the Amazon in South America, in countries such as Brazil, Peru, and Colombia. In the wild, they live in warm, soft water among tree roots, fallen leaves, and plants. The water in the Amazon is often stained brown by leaves, which makes it look like tea. Discus fish love this warm, calm water, and they swim in groups called shoals. In the wild, they hide among plants and roots to stay safe from bigger fish. Today, many discus fish are bred in aquariums and fish farms, where they are cared for by people who love them.

## A Peaceful and Social Fish

Discus fish are peaceful and social animals. In the wild and in aquariums, they like to live in groups, and they feel safest when they are with other discus fish. They communicate with each other through body language, such as flaring their fins, changing colour, and swimming together. Discus fish are also very intelligent for fish. They can recognise their owners and will often swim to the front of the tank when they see them, hoping for food. They are gentle and do not fight with other fish, but they need a calm tank with peaceful tank mates, because they can become stressed if they are bullied.

## What Discus Fish Eat

In the wild, discus fish eat small insects, worms, tiny crustaceans, and plant matter that they find among the roots and leaves. In an aquarium, they need a varied diet to stay healthy and colourful. Owners feed them special discus food, frozen foods such as bloodworms and brine shrimp, and sometimes vegetables such as peas and spinach. Discus fish need to be fed several small meals a day, because they have small stomachs and need regular food. A good diet helps their colours stay bright and their bodies stay strong. Clean water and good food are the keys to happy discus fish.

## Baby Discus Fish

Discus fish are wonderful parents. When they are ready to have babies, a pair of discus fish will choose a flat surface, such as a leaf, a rock, or the side of the tank, and lay their eggs there. Both parents take turns guarding the eggs, fanning them with their fins to keep them clean and full of oxygen. When the eggs hatch, the tiny baby fish, called fry, swim to their parents and feed on a special slime that the parents produce on their skin. This is a very special way of feeding babies, and it helps the fry grow strong. The parents care for their fry for several weeks, protecting them and teaching them to find food.

## How to Care for Discus Fish

Caring for discus fish takes time and patience, because they need clean, warm water to stay healthy. Their tank water should be warm, soft, and clean, with regular water changes to remove waste. Discus fish need a large tank, because they grow big and like to swim in groups. They also need plants, hiding places, and gentle lighting to feel safe and comfortable. Owners should test the water regularly and feed their discus fish a healthy diet. Discus fish are not the easiest fish to keep, but with love and care, they can live for many years and become beautiful, beloved pets.

## Why Discus Fish Are Loved

Discus fish are loved by aquarium keepers around the world because they are beautiful, graceful, and intelligent. They are often called the jewels of the aquarium, because their bright colours and round shapes make them stand out. Watching discus fish glide through the water is peaceful and relaxing, and many people find it calming to watch them swim. Discus fish remind us that the underwater world is full of beauty and wonder, and that even a small fish can bring great joy. If you ever visit an aquarium, look for the round, colourful discus fish, and watch them swim gracefully through their watery home.

So next time you see a discus fish, remember the round jewel of the Amazon. It may be a quiet, peaceful fish, but its bright colours, gentle nature, and loving parents make it one of the most special fish in the whole aquarium world.
