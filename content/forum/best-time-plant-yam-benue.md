Every year I argue with my father about when to plant yam. He says plant early, as soon as the first rains come. My uncle says wait until the rains are steady, or the setts will rot. Last year I planted early and some setts rotted, but the ones that survived gave big tubers.

For Benue farmers, when do you plant your yam, and why? Does early planting really give bigger yam, or is it safer to wait?
