I grow tomatoes in Zaria during the dry season with irrigation. The fruits look fine when small, but as they grow the bottom turns black and rots. I lose almost half the harvest this way. Some people say it is a disease, others say it is lack of water. I water every two days. What is really causing this, and how do I stop it? I use the same soil every season.
