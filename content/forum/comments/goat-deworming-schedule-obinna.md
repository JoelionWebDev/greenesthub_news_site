Dose by weight, not by eye — underdosing is the fastest way to breed resistant worms. Weigh a few goats with a hanging scale and dose for the heaviest in the group, or dose each one properly if you have time. Also take a dung sample to the vet once, so you know which worms you are actually fighting instead of guessing. And check your feed: goats that eat well fight worms better. A mineral lick and some concentrate for the nursing does will help them carry less worm load.
