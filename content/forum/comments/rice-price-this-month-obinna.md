Do not sell to the first buyer who comes to your village. Call two or three traders in Minna and one in Onitsha market and compare before you agree to anything. Farm-gate price is always lower than market price, but if the gap is too big, hire transport and take it to the market yourself — with 30 bags it is worth the trip. Also ask for the price per bag and per mudu, because some buyers quote per mudu to make the price sound better than it is.
