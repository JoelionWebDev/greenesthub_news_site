Garri is cheapest right after harvest when everyone is processing, and dearest in the months before the next harvest. If you can store it dry and safe, wait. White garri sells better in the East, yellow in the West — know your market. Keep a small notebook of the price every market day for two months; after that you will see the pattern yourself and you will not need to guess.
