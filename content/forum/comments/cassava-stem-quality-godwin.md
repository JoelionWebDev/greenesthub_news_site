Buy from a source you trust, not just any neighbour. If you can, get stems from the agricultural development programme or a seed company in your state — they sell certified cuttings of improved varieties and the germination rate is far higher. When you cut the stems into 20cm pieces, cut them the same day you plant, or store them upright in shade for no more than two weeks. Stems left lying flat in the sun lose their sprouting power fast. For 3 hectares, budget for stems properly — cheap bad stems are the most expensive mistake in cassava farming.
