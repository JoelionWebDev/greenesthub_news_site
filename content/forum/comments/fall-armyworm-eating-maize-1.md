The trick is to spray INTO the whorl where the caterpillars hide, not just on the leaves. I use a knapsack sprayer and aim down into the centre of each plant early in the morning. A neem-based spray worked for me when the attack was small. When it is heavy, I use an approved chemical with emamectin benzoate — ask your agro-dealer for the one labelled for fall armyworm and follow the dose on the label exactly.
