I keep crop rotation and farmyard manure from the old ways — my soil stays alive and my tomatoes taste better than the ones grown on chemicals alone. But I have taken drip irrigation and improved seed from the modern side, because watering with a can in dry season wastes half the day. The mistake is to throw away everything old at once. Keep what still works on your own farm, test one new thing at a time on a small plot, and only expand it when you see the result with your own eyes.
