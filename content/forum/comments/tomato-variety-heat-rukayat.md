My tomatoes wilted every hot season until I changed how I water. Use raised beds so water does not sit on the roots, water deeply in the early morning, and mulch with dry leaves to hold the moisture. Avoid planting tomatoes on the same bed every season — that builds up bacterial wilt in the soil. Ask for heat-tolerant seed at the agro shop and plant a small trial bed first before committing the whole farm.
