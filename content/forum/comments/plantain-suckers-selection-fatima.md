Choose sword suckers, not water suckers. Sword suckers have a broad base and narrow pointed leaves; water suckers have broad leaves and a thin base and they grow poorly. Check the base of the sucker for small holes — that is weevil damage. Peel off the outer layer and look for dark spots inside; a healthy sucker is clean and white inside. Always buy from a farm you have seen with your own eyes, not from a stranger on the roadside.
