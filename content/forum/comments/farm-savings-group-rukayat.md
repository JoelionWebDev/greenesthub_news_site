Ajo works well for farm inputs if the group is serious. Agree a fixed amount everyone pays each week or month, write down the order in which each person collects, and keep one notebook where every payment is recorded and signed. Start small — even ₦2,000 a week adds up to enough for a bag of fertilizer in a few months. Only join with people you trust and who farm themselves, because one person who stops paying breaks the whole circle.
