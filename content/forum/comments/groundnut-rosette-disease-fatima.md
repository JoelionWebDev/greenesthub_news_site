Rosette is spread by aphids, so the fight starts before planting. Use a resistant variety like SAMNUT 24 or SAMNUT 26 if you can get certified seed, plant early with the first good rains, and spray neem on young plants to keep aphids away. Walk the farm weekly and uproot any plant showing yellow mottled leaves at once, before the aphids carry it to the rest.
