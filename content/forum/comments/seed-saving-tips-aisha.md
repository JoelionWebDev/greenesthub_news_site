I save my own maize seed for the feed I grow for my layers, and the rule I follow is simple: only save seed from open-pollinated varieties, never from hybrid. Hybrid seed will not grow true the second season — you will get small cobs and uneven plants. Dry the seed very well in the sun, then store it in an airtight container with dry wood ash or neem leaves to keep weevils away. Label the container with the variety and the year, because after two seasons the germination drops.
