Raise the barn off the ground on strong sticks and keep the grass cleared around it — termites love a damp, bushy base. I dust wood ash around the legs of my own store and it helps, but check the yams every week and remove any tuber that has started to rot, because one bad yam invites the rest to spoil. If the attack is already heavy, do not waste money on chemicals inside the barn — move the good yams to a dry, airy place first, then treat the old structure.
