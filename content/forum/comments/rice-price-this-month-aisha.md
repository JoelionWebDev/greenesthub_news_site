Check the price in two places before you sell: call a trader in Minna market and check the price in Lagos if you can. The village buyers always offer less because they add transport and profit. If the difference is more than 10,000 naira per bag, it is worth hiring transport to the town market yourself. Also, do not sell everything at once — sell half now and keep half for when the price rises.
