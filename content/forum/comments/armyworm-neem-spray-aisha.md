Neem works, but you must spray it right: use fresh neem leaves, soak them overnight, and spray early in the morning or evening directly into the centre of the plant where the worms hide. Spray three times, 5 days apart. If the worms are big and the damage is heavy, neem alone will not save the crop — use a recommended chemical like emamectin benzoate once, then continue with neem. The key is catching them when they are small.
