I buy rice for Onitsha market. If your rice is well milled and stone-free, buyers here pay better than the village middlemen. But do not carry 50 bags yourself — transport from Kebbi will eat your profit. First call two or three big buyers in Onitsha, agree a price per bag on the phone, then send a sample of 2 bags by transport. If they like it, they will pay for the rest and arrange the transport themselves.
