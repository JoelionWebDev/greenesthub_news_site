Ada is right about the money, but do not buy your first snails from the open market — many are old and will not lay. Buy from a known snail farmer, and ask to see the parents laying eggs. Also, snails need shade and moisture always. If your backyard is hot and dry, build the pen under a tree or cover it well, and water the soil every evening. Dry soil kills snails fast.
