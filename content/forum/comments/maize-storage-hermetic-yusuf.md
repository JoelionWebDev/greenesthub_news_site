They pay for themselves if you sell late. Dry the maize very well first — it must be under 13% moisture or it will mould inside the sealed bag. Fill the bag, press out the air, and seal it tight; without oxygen the weevils die in a few weeks. The bags last 2 to 3 seasons if you handle them gently. Sell 3 months after harvest when the price is up, and the bag has paid for itself twice.
