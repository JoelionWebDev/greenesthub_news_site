Neem works, but one spray is not enough — you need to spray two or three times, about three or four days apart. Spray early in the morning, straight into the centre of the plant where the worms hide, not just on the leaves. Add a small spoon of liquid soap to the mixture so it sticks to the leaves. If the worms are already big, use one chemical spray to knock them down, then go back to neem to keep them away.
