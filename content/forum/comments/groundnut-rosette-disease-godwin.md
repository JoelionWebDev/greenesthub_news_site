I lost groundnut to rosette two seasons ago and early planting made the biggest difference. Plant as soon as the rains settle, space the rows well so air moves between plants, and ask your extension officer where to buy genuine resistant seed — the seed sold in open market is often mixed. If aphids appear, spray soapy water or neem early in the morning before they spread.
