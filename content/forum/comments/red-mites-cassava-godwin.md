First, check how bad it is — if only a few leaves are bronze, the tubers are usually safe, because mites attack the leaves, not the roots. Spray the underside of the leaves with strong water first; mites hate being washed off. Then spray neem oil in the evening, and repeat after four days. Avoid strong chemicals close to harvest, you are right to worry about that. If the whole field turns bronze, cut and burn the worst leaves so the mites do not spread to the next plot.
