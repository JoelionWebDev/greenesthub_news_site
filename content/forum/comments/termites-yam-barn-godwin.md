Raise the barn on concrete or stone footings so the wood never touches the soil — that alone stops most termites. For the current barn, replace the eaten poles and coat the bottom of each new pole with used engine oil or a wood preservative, keeping it away from the yams. Clear grass and dead wood around the barn, because termites travel through them. Check the poles every month in rainy season.
