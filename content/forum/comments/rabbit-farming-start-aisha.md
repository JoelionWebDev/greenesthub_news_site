I keep poultry, and the biggest lesson that also applies to rabbits is housing. Like chickens, rabbits fall sick quickly in a dirty, wet hutch. Keep the hutch dry, clean it often, and give fresh water every day.

Start small — two does and one buck is enough to learn. Learn what they eat and how fast they grow before you build ten hutches. Many people rush to buy many rabbits and then lose them because they did not learn the feeding first.
