Your father is right and your uncle is also right — it depends on the rain. In Benue, I plant as soon as we get two good rains in a row, usually late March or early April. Planting too early before the rains settle rots the setts, but waiting until May gives smaller tubers. The trick: plant when the soil is moist deep down, not just wet on top. Dig and check — if the soil is dry 10cm down, wait.
