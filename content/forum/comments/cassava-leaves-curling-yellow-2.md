I had the same problem two seasons ago. The tubers still formed, but they were smaller than normal. My advice: do not panic and do not uproot everything. Feed the crop well — apply NPK if you have not — and keep the farm weed-free so the plants can recover. For the white patches, I used a simple soap spray (liquid soap mixed with water) on the leaves and it helped with the insects.
