Dry season pepper usually pays for irrigation if you do the maths per bed first. Add up the fuel and water for one bed, then count how many baskets that bed will give you and what a basket sells for in the town market. Sell in small measures at retail if you can — the price per basket is much better than selling everything to one wholesale buyer. If the numbers do not add up for a far market, sell at the nearest market day and save the transport money.
