Start with your farmers' cooperative — exporters rarely buy from one small farmer, they buy from groups that can supply several tonnes. Dry your ginger very well before you sell; buyers reject ginger with mould or moisture, and one bad bag can spoil your name with a buyer. Ask the cooperative to show you what grade they pay most for, then grow and dry towards that grade. Also compare the exporter's price with the local market price after transport — sometimes the local market pays almost the same without the long trip.
