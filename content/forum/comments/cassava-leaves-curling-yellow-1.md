Curling and yellowing young leaves with white patches sounds like cassava mosaic disease or mealybug. First check the underside of the leaves — if you see tiny white insects, it is mealybug. Remove the worst leaves and burn them far from the farm, do not compost them. Next season, plant only clean cuttings from healthy plants. Do not spray blindly before you know what it is — show a leaf to your extension officer.
