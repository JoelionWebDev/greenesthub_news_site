In the hot season, variety matters more than fertiliser. Roma VF and UC82B handle heat better than the soft local types, and Tropimech was bred for hot climates. Mulch heavily with dry grass to keep the soil cool and water early in the morning, never at midday. If you can, put a light shade net over the bed during the hottest weeks — it stops the flowers from dropping.
