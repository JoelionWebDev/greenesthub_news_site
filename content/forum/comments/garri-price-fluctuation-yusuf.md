Garri has one big advantage: it stores well if you keep it dry. So there is no need to rush and sell everything at harvest time when the price is lowest.

My own practice is to sell a small part when I need cash for the farm, then keep the rest in sealed bags off the floor. Around festive season the price usually rises, and that is when the remaining bags bring better money. Just check the bags often so moisture does not spoil them.
