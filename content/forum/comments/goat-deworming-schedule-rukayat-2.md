Rotate your dewormer — use a different drug family each time, not the same one every quarter. Dose by weight, not by guessing: under-dosing is what builds resistance. Clean the pen weekly and do not let goats graze where they drop dung. Also deworm the kids at 6 weeks and again at 10 weeks; that is when most deaths happen. If a goat stays thin after two correct doses, call a vet — it may not be worms.
