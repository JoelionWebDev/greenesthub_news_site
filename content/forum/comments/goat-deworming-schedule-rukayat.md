The resistance problem is real — using the same drug every three months for years is how worms get used to it. Ask your vet to rotate to a different class of dewormer, albendazole and ivermectin work differently, and switch every year. Also deworm the kids at 6 to 8 weeks old, they carry the most worms. And keep the pen dry: worms spread fast when goats stand in wet dung. Clean the pen every week and do not let them graze on wet grass in the early morning when the worm larvae are on the leaves.
