In Zaria heat I have had the best results with Roma VF and UC82B — they set fruit even when the afternoons are very hot, while the big beefsteak types drop their flowers. Mulch thickly with dry grass to keep the soil cool, and water early in the morning so the plants are not stressed by midday. If you plant a few rows of maize beside the tomatoes, the light shade in the afternoon helps too. Avoid varieties bred for cool climates; they will wilt no matter how much you water.
