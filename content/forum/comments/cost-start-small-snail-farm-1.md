I started with ₦80,000 two years ago. My breakdown: pen (wood and net) ₦25,000, good loamy soil and leaves free from my compound, 50 point-of-lay snails at ₦800 each = ₦40,000, feed (pawpaw leaves, cocoyam leaves, maize) about ₦10,000 for the first months. Snails are slow money — mine started laying after 6 months and I sold the first batch after about 10 months. Start small, learn, then grow.
