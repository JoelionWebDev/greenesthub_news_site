Do not wait — armyworm can finish a young maize farm in days. I mix wood ash with a little water and pour it into the whorl of each plant; it kills the small caterpillars and costs almost nothing. For a big farm, ash alone is too slow, so combine it: ash on the worst plants today, then spray tomorrow. And plant early next season — early maize escapes the worst of the armyworm.
