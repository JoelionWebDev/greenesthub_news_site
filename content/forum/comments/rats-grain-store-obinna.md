Metal drums with tight lids are the surest fix — rats cannot chew through them, and they keep the maize dry as well. Move the remaining bags off the floor onto a raised wooden platform, away from the walls, and check the store every few days. Set a few snap traps along the walls at night, and if you can keep a cat around the store it helps a lot. Also make sure the maize is fully dry before you bag it, because damp grain attracts rats faster.
