I have faced this on my farm. First check the underside of the leaves — that is where the mites gather. Spray the leaves with water mixed with a little liquid soap early in the morning, and neem leaf extract works well too if you repeat it every five days. The good news is the tubers are usually safe; mites attack the leaves, and the plant recovers once you break their cycle. Avoid strong chemicals close to harvest — soap and neem are enough if you start early.
