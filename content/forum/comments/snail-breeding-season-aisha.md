The eggs dry out when the pen is too hot or too dry, so shade is everything. I keep my snail pen under a tree and cover it with banana leaves, and I feed pawpaw leaves and crushed eggshells for calcium so the shells grow strong. Do not disturb the eggs once laid — mark the spot, wait about three weeks, and move the baby snails to their own pen so the big ones do not crush them.
