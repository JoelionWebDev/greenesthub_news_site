Both good answers. One warning: never mix different chemicals together, and never spray more than the label says — it does not kill more worms, it only poisons your soil and your pocket. Spray early morning or late evening when the caterpillars are feeding. Check the plants again after 3 days; if you still see live caterpillars, spray once more.
