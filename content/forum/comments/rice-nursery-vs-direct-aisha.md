One more thing on nursery: use a raised bed near your water source and cover it with palm fronds for the first week so the sun does not dry the seedlings. I sow about 25kg of seed per hectare of nursery for one hectare of field. When transplanting, put 2 to 3 seedlings per hill and keep the spacing at 20cm by 20cm — too close and they compete, too wide and weeds take over. Direct planting is cheaper on labour, but on your sandy soil the nursery gives you control you will not get any other way.
