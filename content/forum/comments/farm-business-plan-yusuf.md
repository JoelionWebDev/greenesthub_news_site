Yes, write it — but keep it to one or two pages, not a big document. The bank wants to see four things: what you will plant, what it will cost you, what you expect to sell, and how you will repay the loan month by month. Write your costs from your notebook — seed, fertilizer, labour, transport — and be honest about them. A plan that shows a small profit you can defend is better than a big profit the bank does not believe. Take your notebook records with you; they prove you already keep track of your money.
