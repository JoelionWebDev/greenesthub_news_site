Snails breed best in the rainy season when the soil stays moist. Keep the pen damp with mulching and water it lightly every evening, and give the snails a shallow container of loose, moist soil to lay eggs in. Cover the pen with fine mesh — rats and soldier ants eat the eggs at night. Check the egg container weekly and move the eggs to a separate small pen to hatch.
