Good advice from Ngozi and Emeka. One more check: if the yellowing starts from the older leaves and moves up, it can also be a nutrient problem — cassava needs potassium. If it starts from the young leaves, it is more likely disease or insects. Either way, take a photo of the leaves to your local agriculture extension office before buying any chemical. You can also read our Safe Crops guides for how to spot the common cassava problems.
