Do not cut the feed — the birds will lay fewer eggs and the ones they lay will be small, so you lose more than you save. The real fix is selling direct: take crates to shops, restaurants and households in town instead of the wholesaler, and you keep the margin he was taking. If you can, also mix part of your own feed with maize, soya and fish meal; even replacing a quarter of the bag feed brings the cost down. I know a farmer in Nsukka who did both and his profit came back without raising his egg price.
