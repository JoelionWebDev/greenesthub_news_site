I planted OBA Super 2 last season in Nasarawa and got about 4 tonnes per hectare with good fertilizer and weeding. SAMMAZ 15 is also good and resists striga, which matters if your soil has striga. Buy seed only from a known agro-dealer or the seed company agent — check the pack for the seal and expiry date. Fake seed is the biggest problem, not the variety.
