With yam, the farm-gate buyer has one advantage: they take everything at once. When your barn is full and you need space, selling all the tubers in one day saves you weeks of storage wahala.

But the price is always lower than town market. If you can wait and carry small quantities to the market yourself over a few weeks, you earn more per tuber. I do a mix — sell the big bulk to farm-gate buyers and carry the best tubers to market myself.
