Never use fresh droppings straight on the crop — they burn the roots. Compost them first: pile the droppings with dry grass or sawdust, keep the pile moist, and turn it every week for 4 to 6 weeks until it stops smelling. Then apply about 2 tonnes per hectare before planting and mix it into the soil. Composted droppings feed the soil for two seasons and cost you almost nothing.
