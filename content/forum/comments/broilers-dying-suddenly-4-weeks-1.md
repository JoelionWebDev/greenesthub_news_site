Green watery droppings and sudden deaths at 4 weeks — stop the antibiotics and call a vet today. It could be Newcastle disease, and antibiotics do not cure a virus. Did the birds get their Newcastle vaccine at day 1 and the booster? If not, vaccinate the remaining healthy birds quickly, separate the sick ones, and disinfect the pen. A vet needs to see a dead bird to confirm — do not guess with drugs from the shop.
