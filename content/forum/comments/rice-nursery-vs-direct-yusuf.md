I grow rice in Minna and I switched to nursery two seasons ago. On sandy soil like yours it pays: you get uniform stands because you only transplant the strong seedlings, and you save seed — direct sowing wastes a lot to birds and poor germination. The extra work is real, about 3 to 4 days for the nursery bed and transplanting, but transplant at 21 days, not later, or the yield drops. If your rains are unreliable, nursery is even better because you control the water in the bed while you wait for the rains to settle.
