Choose stems from plants that are 9 to 12 months old — not too young, not too old. The stem should be thick like your thumb, with healthy buds and no spots or cracks. Cut it into 20cm pieces with at least 5 buds each. Improved varieties like TME 419 give far more tubers, so paying a little more for clean stems is worth it if you can find a trusted source.
