For vegetables, farm-gate is safer because they spoil fast. If you carry tomatoes or peppers to town and the market is slow that day, you come back with waste. The farm-gate buyer may pay less, but the money is sure and you save transport.

One thing that helps is grouping with neighbours. If three of us carry our vegetables together and share one transport cost, the town market price becomes worth it. Alone, the transport eats all the profit.
