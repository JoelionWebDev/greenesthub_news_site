This is not a disease — it is blossom end rot, and it comes from the plant not getting enough calcium when the fruit is forming. Watering every two days can still be uneven if the soil dries out between waterings, so water a little every day and mulch around the plants with dry grass to keep the soil moist. Crushed eggshells or a little lime mixed into the soil helps over time. The fruits that are already black will not recover, so pick them off and the new ones should come out clean.
