answer: "White, cotton-like patches on cassava leaves are often cassava mealybug, and a fine white dust can be powdery mildew. Do not spray blindly. First, remove the worst-affected leaves and throw them away from the farm (do not compost them). Check whether the white patches are insects (tiny white bugs that move) or a dust that wipes off. Then show a leaf — or a photo of it — to your local extension officer, who can confirm what it is and what to use. Planting clean, healthy cuttings next season prevents most of it."
