answer: "Layers stop laying for many reasons. Check these in order: 1) Water — do they have clean water all day? No water, no eggs. 2) Feed — are they getting layers feed (not broiler feed)? 3) Heat — very hot weather cuts laying; give shade and cool water. 4) Light — layers need about 14–16 hours of light a day. 5) Age — after about 72 weeks, laying drops naturally. 6) Sickness — if many birds look dull, stop eating or have runny droppings at the same time, call a vet quickly. Fix water and feed first — that solves most cases."
