answer: "Yellow maize leaves often mean the plant lacks nitrogen, or the field is holding too much water. First check: does water stand in the field after rain? If yes, improve the drainage. If the whole plant is pale yellow, it likely needs fertilizer — apply urea or NPK as your local extension officer advises. If only some leaves have yellow spots or patches, it may be a disease — take a photo of the leaf and show it to your extension officer before spraying anything."
